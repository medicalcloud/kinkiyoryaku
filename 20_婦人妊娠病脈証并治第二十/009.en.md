# Clause 9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When a woman is pregnant, it is suitable to take [this] regularly: Danggui san governs it.

Danggui san (Angelica Powder) formula

Danggui, huangqin [base: 黄苓], shaoyao, and xiongqiong, 1 liang [most other sources: 1 jin] each; baizhu half a jin.

Pound the five ingredients above into powder; take a square-inch spoonful with wine, twice a day. Taken regularly in pregnancy, delivery is easy, the fetus has no suffering or illness, and it governs all the hundred diseases after delivery.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

"Suitable to take regularly" means it is good to take even without disease. What generates things is earth, and the means by which earth generates things is dampness. Blood is a transformation of dampness, and the fetus especially depends on it. So danggui nourishes blood and shaoyao contracts yin; the liver governs blood, so xiongqiong frees liver qi; the spleen controls blood, so baizhu strengthens spleen earth. Huangqin is used because the method of securing the fetus is mainly to cool the blood and disinhibit qi; so sharen, zhiqiao, and sugeng are all considered good for securing the fetus, but people do not know that qi is especially governed by the lung. Huangqin can clear the lung and disinhibit the source of qi; assisted by baizhu, dampness has no heat and does not stagnate. So baizhu assisting huangqin has the power to secure the fetus. This is the intent of the formula: huangqin is the mainstay. Difficulties in pregnancy and delivery all arise from depressed heat causing dryness and inhibiting the mechanisms. Nourishing blood and strengthening the spleen with huangqin as sovereign, there is naturally no trouble from dry heat. Hence "taken regularly, delivery is easy, the fetus has no illness, and it governs all the hundred diseases after delivery."

[Part of Xu Bin's formula:] danggui 1 jin; huangqin 1 jin; shaoyao 1 jin; xiongqiong 1 jin; baizhu half a jin.

... delivery is easy, the fetus has no illness, and it governs all the hundred diseases after delivery.

## Notes

A clause presenting Danggui san as a formula to be taken regularly during pregnancy to secure the fetus.

- Danggui, shaoyao, and chuanxiong nourish blood, baizhu strengthens the spleen, and huangqin clears heat. In later times baizhu and huangqin were regarded as key medicinals for securing the fetus.
- The base's 黄苓 is 黄芩 in all other sources and appears to be an error.
