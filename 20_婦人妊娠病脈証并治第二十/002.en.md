# Clause 2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When a woman has long had an abdominal mass disease, her menses have stopped for not yet three months, and she has unceasing spotting, with fetal movement above the navel, it is harm from the chronic mass. When the fetus moves at six months of pregnancy, it is a fetus [conceived] while the menses were flowing freely in the previous three months. When there is bleeding, it is clotted blood of the three months after [the menses] stopped. The bleeding does not stop because the mass has not gone. The mass should be purged; Guizhi Fuling wan governs it.

Guizhi Fuling wan (Cinnamon Twig and Poria Pill) formula

Guizhi; fuling; mudan (heart removed); taoren (skin and tips removed, dry-fried); shaoyao; equal amounts of each.

Powder the five ingredients above, mix with refined honey into pills the size of rabbit droppings, and take one pill daily before meals. If there is no effect, increase up to three pills.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

When a woman meets with cold during her menses, residual blood lodges and forms a mass. 癥 (mass) means something with form that can be verified. But mass disease is common in women; if it is not in the uterus, she still menstruates and can conceive, and when the menses stop she has conceived. "Not yet three months" means nearly three months. Bleeding after conception is called spotting. Now, if spotting does not stop before three months, the blood nourishing the fetus is damaged, so the fetus moves. If the fetus were below the navel it would truly be about to fall; now it is above the navel, which means the new blood gathering each month is obstructed by the qi of the mass and becomes spotting; it is not actually a disease of the fetus. Hence "harm from the chronic mass." 痼 means a long-standing disease hard to cure; 害 means something burdening it without cause.

Fetal movement at six months is the proper time for movement. But compared with the time the menses flowed freely in the previous three months, if there is fetal movement with bleeding, the blood had already stopped and not flowed for three months, and now again the bleeding does not stop: the new blood has gone with the earlier spotting, but the mass, on the contrary, is firm and does not go; so it must be purged to bring calm.

Guizhi Fuling tang is used: guizhi and shaoyao are one yang and one yin, fuling and mudanpi one qi and one blood, regulating cold and warmth and supporting the right qi; taoren breaks malign blood and dissolves masses, without fear of damaging the fetal blood — what is called "when there is disease, the disease bears it." Moreover, a mass at its beginning is always due to cold, and guizhi can transform qi and dissolve the root cold; once formed, a mass always carries damp-heat and forms a nest-sac, so fuling percolates damp qi, mudanpi clears blood heat, and shaoyao contracts liver blood and supports the spleen so that it can control the blood. Nourishing the right is precisely the way to remove the evil. There are very many mass-dissolving formulas, but none as skillful as this one in achieving two ends at once. Each dose being very small and frequent is more skillful still. One should understand that the mass does not obstruct the fetus because its binding is originally slight, so it is ground away gradually.

## Notes

A clause stating that persistent bleeding in a pregnant woman with a pre-existing abdominal mass is due to the mass, which is purged with Guizhi Fuling wan.

- The middle of the clause (from "when the fetus moves at six months" to "clotted blood of the three months after [the menses] stopped") is obscure and variously interpreted.
- Guizhi Fuling wan has been widely used in later times as a representative formula for removing static blood.
- Xu Bin's text has many lacunae in this clause (marked 欠), and after 胎下血者 it has the note 該是動字 ("should be the character 動").
