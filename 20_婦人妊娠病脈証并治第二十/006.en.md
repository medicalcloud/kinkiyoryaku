# Clause 6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Unceasing vomiting in pregnancy: Ganjiang Renshen Banxia wan governs it.

Ganjiang Renshen Banxia wan (Dried Ginger, Ginseng, and Pinellia Pill) formula

Ganjiang and renshen, 1 liang each; banxia 2 liang.

Powder the three ingredients above and make pills with a paste of fresh ginger juice, the size of firmiana seeds. Take 10 pills with a drink, three times a day.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

"All retching, vomiting, and acid regurgitation belong to fire" refers to temporary vomiting from unclear stomach qi. If vomiting in pregnancy does not stop, it is vomiting due to cold; what comes out upward is retching, and if it does not stop, there is deficiency. So banxia treats the retching, ganjiang treats the cold, renshen supplements the deficiency, and fresh ginger juice assists banxia in bringing down the counterflowing retained fluid.

[Part of Xu Bin's formula:] ganjiang 1 liang; renshen 1 liang; banxia 2 liang.

## Notes

A clause using Ganjiang Renshen Banxia wan for unceasing vomiting in pregnancy (morning sickness).
