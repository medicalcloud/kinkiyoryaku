# Clause 10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Nourishing the fetus in pregnancy: Baizhu san governs it.

Baizhu san (White Atractylodes Powder) formula (appears in the *Waitai*)

Baizhu; xiongqiong; shujiao (Sichuan pepper) 3 *fen* (remove the sweat [base: "juice"]); muli (oyster shell).

Pound the four ingredients above into powder and take one qianbi with wine, three times in the day and once at night. If one suffers only from [abdominal] pain, add shaoyao. For severe pain below the heart, add double xiongqiong. For heart vexation with vomiting and pain and inability to eat or drink, add 1 liang of xixin and 20 large pieces of banxia; after taking it, further take it with sour fermented water. If there is retching, take it with sour fermented water; if it still [other sources: again] does not resolve, take it with wheat juice. If thirst follows, take it with barley gruel. Even after the disease heals, do not stop taking it.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

The fetus is carried by earth and nourished by blood. So baizhu banks up earth and xiongqiong disinhibits the liver. The fetus dislikes the upward counterflow of yin qi, so pepper, purely yang by nature and taking yin as its home, is used to gather the heat of the upper burner qi aspect and send it down, and also to remove cold contracted by chance in the abdomen and level it. But though it enters yin, it cannot nourish yin; so muli, a substance purely masculine in qi transformation and yin in nature, disperses heat qi congealed in the yin aspect and harmonizes yin and yang. I once treated the wife of Dike's younger brother. Before conceiving she had phlegm and cough with blood, which did not lessen after conception, and she was thin. I treated her with this formula, adding shaoyao for her abdominal pain; after two large doses the phlegm lessened, the cough stopped, she felt refreshed, and the fetus was secure. If there is severe pain below the heart, liver qi depression has not yet been freed, so xiongqiong is doubled. When it reaches heart vexation, vomiting with pain, and inability to eat or drink, it is not liver depression alone: there is visiting cold with severe counterflow causing vomiting and pain, and fire congesting above causes vexation. So xixin is added to dispel cold, banxia to stop counterflow, and a vinegar decoction is used to harmonize the blood and calm the lower part. If not cured, wheat juice is used to nourish heart fluid and calm the upper part. If still not cured, barley gruel harmonizes the middle. "Even after the disease heals, do not stop taking it," because the medicinal nature is mild and not one-sided. Hence "Baizhu san for nourishing the fetus." Without using blood medicinals, regulating the qi lets the blood harmonize by itself.

[Part of Xu Bin's formula:] baizhu; xiongqiong; shujiao 3 *fen* (remove the sweat); muli.

## Notes

A clause presenting Baizhu san as a formula to nourish the fetus in pregnancy, with modifications according to symptoms.

- The base lacks most of the amounts (Daizhige gives baizhu and xiongqiong 4 *fen* and muli 2 *fen*).
- For "suffers only from pain," Xu Bin's text has the note 脱一腹字 ("a character 腹 [abdomen] is missing"), and it is translated as "suffers only from abdominal pain."
- 巳 in 巳後 is read as 已 ("afterward").
