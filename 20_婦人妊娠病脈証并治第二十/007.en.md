# Clause 7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Difficult urination in pregnancy, with eating and drinking as usual: [Dang]gui [Bei]mu Kushen wan (Tokibaimokujingan) governs it.

Danggui Beimu Kushen wan (Tokibaimokujingan; Angelica, Fritillaria, and Sophora Pill) formula (for men, add half a liang of huashi [kasseki])

Danggui, beimu (baimo; fritillaria), and kushen (sophora), 4 liang each.

Powder the three ingredients above and make pills with refined honey the size of adzuki beans. Take 3 pills with a drink, increasing up to 10 pills.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

[Part of Xu Bin's formula:] danggui 4 liang; beimu 4 liang; kushen 4 liang.

Commentary: Difficult urination occurs when, in cold damage, pathogenic heat passes into the interior, but then exterior signs must appear first. It occurs with depressed heat at the source of transformation, but then thirst must appear above. It occurs when the center qi fails to transform, but then eating and drinking must be disordered. It occurs with sinking of the center qi, but then spleen and stomach signs must appear first. And it occurs with depressed heat in the lower jiao, with no thirst and eating and drinking as usual. Now, in pregnancy with eating and drinking as usual but difficult urination, it must be that wind-cold was contracted while urinating or defecating, depressed in the lower jiao and turned to heat, consuming the water of the bladder. So Danggui Beimu Kushen wan governs it. Kushen can enter yin, treat great wind, open bound qi, and remove latent heat; so it is the sovereign. Danggui, acrid and warm, can enter yin and disinhibit qi, and treats diseases of the thoroughfare and girdle vessels well; so it is the minister. Though the pattern does not originate in the lung, the bladder is the gate of qi transformation, and when the lower orifice is obstructed, the upper is surely not free either; so beimu, opening depression of lung qi, is the assistant. No water-disinhibiting medicinals are used at all, because the disease is not due to water depression.

## Notes

A clause using Danggui Beimu Kushen wan for difficult urination in pregnancy with normal eating and drinking.

- "Gui Mu Kushen wan" in the main text is an abbreviation; Wikisource (*Jingui yaolüe*) and Daizhige read "Danggui Beimu Kushen wan."
- Xu Bin attributes it to depressed heat in the lower jiao and explains why water-disinhibiting medicinals are not used.
