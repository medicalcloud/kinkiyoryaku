# Clause 8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Water qi in pregnancy, with heaviness of the body, inhibited urination, shivering aversion to cold, and dizziness on rising: Kuizi Fuling san governs it.

Kuizi Fuling san (Mallow Seed and Poria Powder) formula

Kuizi (mallow seed) [amount missing; most other sources: 1 jin]; fuling 3 liang.

Pound the two ingredients above into powder and take a square-inch spoonful with a drink, three times a day. When urination is freed, there is recovery.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

Having water qi means that even without great swelling, the waterways in the meridians are already inhibited, and the wei-defensive qi, carrying water, cannot be regulated and free as in a healthy person. When the waterways are inhibited, the qi of the whole body is stagnated by water, hence heaviness. Water flows smoothly when freed and regulated; in counterflow, urination is inhibited. Shivering aversion to cold is the wei-defensive qi not moving. Dizziness on rising: with water qi inside, when one does not move, a trace of yang still stays in the eyes and vision is clear; on rising, the fire of reverting yang drives yin qi counter upward and veils the top, so all one sees is dark; hence dizziness. Kuizi and fuling are used: kuizi lubricates the orifices and fuling disinhibits water. When the lower orifice is free, the upper is naturally not congested; all the more since kuizi, bland and slippery, belongs to yang and can also free the meridians, collaterals, and qi vessels above. Although kuizi can make the fetus slippery, it is not avoided, because when there is disease, the disease bears it. Moreover, the liver governs free coursing, and kuizi is especially able to free stagnation in the liver meridian so that free coursing does not fail in its duty; so urination is always freed, and it is especially quick to work in other conditions such as blocked lactation and swollen breasts.

[Part of Xu Bin's formula:] kuizi 1 jin; fuling 3 liang.

## Notes

A clause using Kuizi Fuling san for water qi (edema) in pregnancy with heaviness of the body, inability to urinate, aversion to cold, and dizziness on standing.

- A formula lubricating the orifices with kuizi and disinhibiting water with fuling.
- The base lacks the amount of kuizi. Three other sources and the formula appended to Xu Bin's commentary give 1 jin.
