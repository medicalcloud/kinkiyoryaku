# Clause 8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Formula to rescue sudden death with open mouth and arched-back rigidity

After applying fourteen cones of moxa behind the nails [base: 瓜 "melon"] of both hands and feet, have [the person] drink the various plasters and powders of the five toxins [other sources add the note "(those containing badou (hazu))"].

## Notes

A formula to rescue sudden death with an open mouth and the body arched backward.

- For the base's 瓜, all other sources read 爪 ("nail").
- This part is absent from Xu Bin's text, so there is no commentary.
