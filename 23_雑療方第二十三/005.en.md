# Clause 5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Another formula

Cut the comb of a rooster to take blood and blow it into the nose through a tube. Boil lard the size of a hen's egg with 1 sheng of vinegar and pour it into the throat. Smear chicken liver and blood on the face and surround [the person] with ashes on four sides, and he or she will rise at once. Mix twice seven soybeans with egg white and wine and have all of it swallowed.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

As long as a person's yang qi is not exhausted by even one part, the person does not die. So in rescuing sudden death, the aim is simply to restore the yang qi. The qi of the nose communicates with heaven, where heavenly yang passes; the qi of the mouth communicates with earth, where earthly yang passes; the face is where all yang gathers, belonging to yangming and central earth, where human yang passes. So either xie or cockscomb blood is used — both can free the yang of the heavenly part — and poured into the nose. Lard can free the yang in the skin, with vinegar as a guide; egg white can free the yang in the kidney, with soybeans as a guide; so they are poured into the throat. The chicken belongs to the trigram xun, and the liver is master of the ethereal soul. Smeared on the face, it communicates inwardly with the stomach; surrounded with ashes on four sides, the qi is further bound and enters inward, drawing into the liver; so the liver qi flows and there is recovery.

## Notes

Four further formulas to rescue sudden death.
