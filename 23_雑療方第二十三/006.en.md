# Clause 6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Formula to rescue sudden death with vigorous heat

Boil half a jin of alum in 1.5 dou of water until dissolved, and soak the feet in it, submerging the ankles.

## Notes

A formula to rescue sudden death with high fever.

- This part is absent from Xu Bin's text, so there is no commentary.
