# Clause 10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Formula to rescue a child who suddenly dies with vomiting and diarrhea when one does not know what disease it is

Squeeze one ball of dog dung to take its juice and pour it in. If there is no moist dung, boil dry dung in water and take the liquid.

## Notes

A formula to rescue a child's sudden death with vomiting and diarrhea.

- This part is absent from Xu Bin's text, so there is no commentary.
