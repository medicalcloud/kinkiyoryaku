# Clause 7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Formula to rescue sudden death with closed eyes

Set [the person] astride an ox facing it, pour pounded xie juice into the ears, and blow zaojia (sokyo; gleditsia) powder into the nose. It works at once.

## Notes

A formula to rescue sudden death with closed eyes.

- This part is absent from Xu Bin's text, so there is no commentary.
