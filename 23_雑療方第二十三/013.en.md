# Clause 13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Another formula

Jiugen (Chinese chive root) 1 handful; wumei (ubai) 14 pieces; wuzhuyu (goshuyu) half a sheng (stir-fried).

Boil the three ingredients above in 1 dou of water. Put the patient's comb in and boil three times: if the comb floats, the person lives; if it sinks, the person dies. Boil down to 3 sheng, remove the dregs, and give it to drink in portions.

## Notes

Another formula to rescue sudden death and death from visiting hostility. Life or death is foretold by whether the patient's comb floats or sinks in the decoction.

- This part is absent from Xu Bin's text, so there is no commentary.
