# Clause 9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Formula to rescue sudden death with limp limbs and incontinence

Boil 1 sheng of horse dung in 3 dou of water down to 2 dou and wash with it. Also take 1 sheng of niudong (thin cow dung), and pour it into the mouth with warm wine. Apply 100 cones of moxa each at 1 cun below the heart, 3 cun above the navel, and 4 cun below the navel; there will be improvement.

## Notes

A formula to rescue sudden death with flaccid limbs and incontinence.

- This part is absent from Xu Bin's text, so there is no commentary.
