# Clause 11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Corpse-like reversal: the pulse moves but there is no breath; the qi is blocked and does not flow, so the person is still and as if dead. Treatment formula (the pulse and signs appear in the first fascicle): put powdered changpu (acorus) into both nostrils and blow it in, and have someone place powdered gui (cinnamon) under the tongue.

Another formula

Take a square inch of hair from the left corner of the forehead [other sources: shave], burn it to powder, mix with wine, and pour it in so that it enters the throat; the person will rise at once.

## Notes

Formulas to treat corpse-like reversal (a deathlike state with a pulse but no breath).

- The pulse and signs appear in chapter 1 (on the zang-fu, channels, and the order of diseases).
- This part is absent from Xu Bin's text, so there is no commentary.
