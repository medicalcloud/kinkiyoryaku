# Clause 12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

To rescue sudden death or death from visiting hostility, Huanhun tang (Kankonto; Soul-Returning Decoction) governs it. Formula (the *Qianjin fang* says: it governs sudden hostility, ghost strike, flying corpse, and all cases of abrupt cessation of breath without regaining consciousness, or with the pulse already gone [base: 以死; other sources: 已無], and the jaw clenched and impossible to pry open. Remove teeth and administer the decoction; if the decoction enters the mouth but does not go down, part the patient's hair to left and right, grasp the shoulders, and pull. When the medicine goes down, take another [other sources: an additional] 1 sheng; after a while the person revives at once)

Mahuang (mao) 3 liang (nodes removed; one version 4 liang); xingren (kyonin; skin and tips removed) 70 pieces; gancao (kanzo) 1 liang (mix-fried) (the *Qianjin* uses 2 liang of guixin).

Of the three ingredients above, with 8 sheng of water boil down to 3 sheng, remove the dregs, and have it swallowed in portions. It treats [base: 冶] all kinds of hostility generally.

## Notes

The formula of Huanhun tang for rescuing sudden death and death from visiting hostility.

- Huanhun tang consists of mahuang, xingren, and gancao; the *Qianjin* adds guixin (making it the same as Mahuang tang [Maoto]).
- The interlinear note shows many character differences between the base and the other sources (see the variants).
- This part is absent from Xu Bin's text, so there is no commentary.
