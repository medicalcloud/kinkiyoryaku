# Clause 7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold-strike, if the person has diarrhea, it is because the interior is deficient. If he wants to sneeze but cannot, this person has cold in the belly (one version says pain).

## Notes

A clause stating that diarrhea after cold-strike is interior deficiency, and that wanting to sneeze but being unable to is due to cold in the abdomen. It contrasts with the previous clause (sneezing = shallow pathogen). Xu Bin's commentary is after unit 8.
