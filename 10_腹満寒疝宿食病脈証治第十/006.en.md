# Clause 6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Those prone to cold-strike often yawn. If the person has clear snivel, fever, and a harmonious complexion, he often sneezes.

## Notes

A clause on the signs of a person struck by cold (yawning, clear snivel, sneezing). A harmonious complexion indicates the evil is shallow, and sneezing is said to be a reaction to expel the evil. Xu Bin's commentary is after unit 8.
