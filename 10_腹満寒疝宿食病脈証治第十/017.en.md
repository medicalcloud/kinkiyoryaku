# Clause 17 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Abdominal pain with a wiry, tight pulse. Wiry means the defensive qi does not move, so there is aversion to cold; tight means no desire to eat. When evil and right contend, it becomes cold *shan*. Cold *shan* is pain around the umbilicus; when it breaks out, white sweat comes out and the hands and feet are reversal-cold. If the pulse is sunken and wiry, Da Wutou Jian (Major Aconite Main Root Decoction) governs it.

Wutou Jian formula

Wutou, five large pieces (dry-fried, skin removed, not cut).

Boil the above in 3 sheng of water down to 1 sheng, remove the dregs, add 2 sheng of honey, decoct until the water has evaporated, and take 2 sheng. A strong person takes 7 ge, a weak person 5 ge. If not cured, take more the next day; it must not be taken twice in one day.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

These are the general pulse and signs of cold *shan*. At first there is only abdominal fullness, but the pulse is distinctly wiry and tight. Wiry means the defensive qi in the exterior does not move, so there is aversion to cold; tight means cold qi impedes the stomach, so there is no desire to eat. Then wind-cold pours into the umbilicus, evil and right contend, and there is pain around the umbilicus. This means the yang guarding the outside, the yang in the stomach, and the yang of the lower burner are all impeded by cold. Since the umbilical pain is due to cold, it is called *shan*. When it breaks out with white fluid coming out, the cold is heavy, so cold drool comes out. Reversal-cold of the hands and feet is reverse-flow. A sunken, tight pulse means the cold has already entered straight inside. So the single ingredient wutou with honey is taken all at once. This is the most exclusive formula for attacking cold, what later people call Pili san (Thunderbolt Powder).

[Xu Bin's text:] ... may be taken twice in one day.

## Notes

A clause describing the pulse and signs of cold *shan* (attacks of abdominal pain due to cold), using Da Wutou Jian.

- There is pain around the umbilicus, with cold sweat and cold limbs during attacks. It is a powerful formula, a large dose of wutou decocted with honey. Because of its strong toxicity, it must not be taken twice in one day.
- The clause calls it Da Wutou Jian; the heading reads Wutou Jian.
- For the final "must not be taken twice in one day," Xu Bin's edition reads "may be taken twice in one day," the opposite meaning.
- For 白汗 ("white sweat"), Daizhige and Xu Bin's edition read 白津 ("white fluid").
