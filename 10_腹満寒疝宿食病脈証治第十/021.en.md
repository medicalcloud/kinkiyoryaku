# Clause 21 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Appended formulas

Wutou tang (Aconite Main Root Decoction) from the *Waitai*: treats cold *shan* with wringing pain in the abdomen, thief wind entering and attacking the five zang organs, hypertonicity with inability to turn over, attacks occurring at times, retraction of the genitals [base: 痩人 "thin people"; read 使人 "causing"], and reverse-flow of the hands and feet (the formula appears above).

Chaihu Gui[zhi] tang formula from the *Waitai*: treats sudden strike with pain in the heart and abdomen.

Chaihu 4 liang; huangqin, renshen, shaoyao, guizhi, and shengjiang, 1.5 liang each; gancao 1 liang; banxia 2 [.5] ge; dazao 6 pieces.

Of the nine ingredients above, with 6 sheng of water boil down to 3 sheng and take 1 sheng warm, three times a day.

Zouma tang (Running Horse Decoction) from the *Waitai*: treats malign strike with heart pain, abdominal distension, and blocked stool.

Badou 2 pieces (remove the skin and core, dry-fry); xingren 2 pieces.

Wrap the two ingredients above in cotton, pound to crush them, and with 2 ge of hot water squeeze out the white juice; drinking it should bring purging. For the elderly and children, adjust the amount. It also treats flying corpse and demonic blow diseases.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

This is the earlier Da Wutou Jian formula; the *Waitai* also uses it because of its frequent efficacy. The indications are similar, but the note says "thief wind entering and attacking the five zang organs," so we know this is external pathogen invading inward, extremely urgent. Yet the pathogen is not yet stored in the kidney; it is only on the verge of invading the kidney at every moment. So when the kidney is not invaded there is no attack, and the moment it is invaded even slightly the attack comes. During an attack the genitals retract because the cold qi contracts and presses. When the kidney yang does not rise, all the yang becomes feeble, so the hands and feet have reverse-flow.

External pathogen entering inward differs from interior deficiency cold. So by combining Guizhi tang and Chaihu tang, it is convenient to drive out the exterior pathogen that has entered gradually from within. Hence "treats sudden strike with pain in the abdomen": what entered from the exterior is treated from the half exterior. [Xu Bin's formula:] chaihu 4 liang; huangqin 1.5 liang; renshen 1.5 liang; banxia 6 pieces; dazao 6 pieces; shengjiang 1.5 liang; gancao 1 liang; guizhi 1.5 liang; shaoyao 1 liang.

Malign strike with heart pain is visiting hostility. Abdominal distension without defecation means the healthy qi can no longer move. At such a time there is no leisure for gentle treatment, so one must attack drastically with badou and disinhibit the qi of lung and large intestine with xingren. Once it is opened, everything opens. So it also governs flying corpse and demonic blow. In all cases yin pathogen cannot stay.

## Notes

Three appended formulas: Wutou tang from the *Waitai* (severe abdominal pain of cold *shan*; the same formula as Da Wutou Jian), Chaihu Guizhi tang from the *Waitai* (sudden pain of heart and abdomen), and Zouma tang from the *Waitai* (drastic purging with badou for malign strike with heart pain, abdominal distension, and constipation).

- For 痩 in 痩人陰縮, all other sources read 使 ("causing the genitals to retract"), which the translation follows.
- For the formula name 柴胡桂湯, the other sources read 柴胡桂枝湯. Most other sources give banxia as 2.5 ge.
- "Flying corpse" and "demonic blow" are names of diseases with sudden severe chest or abdominal pain and impaired consciousness. 冶 in 通冶 is an error for 治.
