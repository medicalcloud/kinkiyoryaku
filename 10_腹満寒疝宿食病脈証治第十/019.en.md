# Clause 19 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold *shan* with abdominal pain, counterflow cold, numbness of the hands and feet, or aching of the body, which moxibustion, needling, and various medicines cannot cure: it is fitting to use the following formula.

Wutou Guizhi tang (Aconite Main Root and Cinnamon Twig Decoction) formula

Wutou.

Decoct the single ingredient above in 2 jin of honey until reduced by half, remove the dregs, dissolve it with 5 ge of Guizhi tang to obtain 1 sheng; then first take 2 ge. If there is no effect, take 3 ge; if there is still no effect, increase to 5 ge. One on whom it takes effect appears drunk; one who vomits has been hit [by the medicine].

Guizhi tang formula

Guizhi 3 liang (remove the bark); shaoyao 3 liang; gancao 2 liang (mix-fried); shengjiang 3 liang; dazao 12 pieces.

Cut the five ingredients above, boil over a low fire with 7 sheng of water down to 3 sheng, and remove the dregs.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

Starting from the abdominal pain of cold *shan* and reaching counterflow cold and numbness of the hands and feet, the yang qi is greatly impeded. With body aches added, the nutritive and defensive are both disharmonious. Further, that moxibustion, needling, and various medicines cannot cure it means that whether attacking the inside or the outside, the evil qi is held back and does not submit. So wutou attacking cold is the mainstay, combined with the whole of Guizhi tang to harmonize the nutritive and defensive — what is called treating the interior seven parts and the exterior three. Appearing drunk means the nutritive and defensive have obtained warmth and the qi prevails; hence "takes effect." Vomiting means the yin evil is not tolerated by the yang and comes out upward; hence "hit."

## Notes

A clause using Wutou Guizhi tang for cold *shan* with abdominal pain, cold and numb limbs, and body aches, unresponsive to other treatment.

- Wutou decocted in honey (Wutou Jian) is dissolved in Guizhi tang: wutou treats the interior cold, Guizhi tang the nutritive and defensive of the exterior.
- The drunken feeling after taking it is the action of wutou and a sign of effect. Since it borders on wutou poisoning, the dose is increased gradually.
- 抵当 in 抵当用後方 is understood as "fitting." The other sources read "Wutou Guizhi tang governs it."
