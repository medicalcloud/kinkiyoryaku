# Clause 13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When abdominal fullness does not lessen, or lessens so little as to be not worth mentioning, it must be purged; Da Chengqi tang (Daijokito) is appropriate.

Da Chengqi tang formula

Dahuang (daio) 4 liang (washed in wine); houpo (koboku) half a jin (remove the bark, mix-fried); zhishi (kijitsu) 5 pieces (mix-fried); mangxiao (bosho) 2 ge.

Of the four ingredients above, with 1 dou of water first boil the two items down to 5 sheng, remove the dregs, add the dahuang, boil down to 2 sheng, add the mangxiao, put it on the fire again for one or two slight boilings, and take warm in two doses. When purging is obtained, do not take the rest.

## Notes

A clause using Da Chengqi tang for excess fullness that does not (or barely) lessen. It contrasts with the cold fullness of unit 3 that "at times lessens but returns." The same text appears in the Yangming chapter of the *Shanghan lun*.

- The formula is the same as Da Chengqi tang in the chapter on tetany, dampness, and summer heat stroke. Daizhige and Xu Bin's edition omit it, saying "the formula appears under tetany." Some sources give mangxiao as 3 ge.
- This unit has no commentary by Xu Bin.
