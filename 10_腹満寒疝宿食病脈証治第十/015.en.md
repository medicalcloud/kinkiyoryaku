# Clause 15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

One-sided pain below the ribs with fever, and a tight, wiry pulse: this is cold. Purge it with warm medicinals; the following decoction is appropriate.

Dahuang Fuzi tang (Rhubarb and Aconite Decoction) formula

Dahuang 3 liang; fuzi 3 pieces (blast-fried); xixin 2 liang.

Of the three ingredients above, with 5 sheng of water boil down to 2 sheng and take warm in three doses. For a strong person, boil down to 2.5 sheng and take warm in three doses. After taking it, wait about as long as it takes to walk four or five li, then take the next dose.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

Compared with the previous clause, this too is cold, but one-sided pain is replete evil. Moreover, with a tight, wiry pulse, even with fever the inside is cold — just what the *Neijing* calls "all who are affected by cold develop heat disease." But since internal cold predominates, it is purged with warm medicinals. Fuzi and xixin are used together with dahuang, moving together without conflict. This is the method of Dahuang Fuzi Xiexin tang in the *Shanghan lun*.

## Notes

A clause using Dahuang Fuzi tang, combining warming (fuzi, xixin) and purging (dahuang) medicinals, for replete cold with one-sided pain below the ribs and constipation. It is regarded as the representative formula of the "warm purging" method.

- For "the following decoction," most other sources give the name Dahuang Fuzi tang.
- Xu Bin's "Dahuang Fuzi Xiexin tang" probably refers to Fuzi Xiexin tang in the *Shanghan lun*.
