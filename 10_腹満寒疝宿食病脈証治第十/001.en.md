# Clause 1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the instep yang pulse is faint and wiry, by rule there should be abdominal fullness. If there is no fullness, there will surely be difficult defecation and aching pain in both flanks. This is vacuity cold rising from below; one should give warming medicinals.

## Notes

The opening clause of the chapter on abdominal fullness, cold *shan*, and abiding food. A faint, wiry instep yang pulse (the pulse of spleen and stomach) indicates yang vacuity and cold of the spleen and stomach; abdominal fullness should occur, and if not, constipation and pain in both flanks. Both are due to vacuity cold, so one warms. Xu Bin's commentary is attached after unit 8.

- 胠 is the region below the armpits along the sides.
