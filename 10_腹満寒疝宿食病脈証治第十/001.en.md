# Clause 1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the anterior tibial (Fuyang) pulse is feeble and wiry, by rule there should be abdominal fullness. If there is no fullness, there will surely be difficult defecation and aching pain in both flanks. This is deficiency cold rising from below; one should give warming medicinals.

## Notes

The opening clause of the chapter on abdominal fullness, cold *shan*, and abiding food. A feeble, wiry anterior tibial (Fuyang) pulse (the pulse of spleen and stomach) indicates yang deficiency and cold of the spleen and stomach; abdominal fullness should occur, and if not, constipation and pain in both flanks. Both are due to deficiency cold, so one warms. Xu Bin's commentary is attached after unit 8.

- 胠 is the region below the armpits along the sides.
