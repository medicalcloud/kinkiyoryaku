# Clause 5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the pulse at the inch opening is wiry, there is tension and pain below the ribs, and the person shivers with aversion to cold.

## Notes

A wiry pulse at the inch opening indicates pathogenic cold in the exterior and the liver, with tension and pain below the ribs and aversion to cold. It is regarded as a preface to the clauses on cold *shan*. Xu Bin's commentary is after unit 8.
