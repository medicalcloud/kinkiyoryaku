# Clause 11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Pain with blockage: the following decoction governs it.

Houpo Sanwu tang (Kobokusanmotsuto; Three-Ingredient Magnolia Bark Decoction) formula

Houpo (koboku) 8 liang; dahuang (daio) 4 liang; zhishi (kijitsu) 5 pieces.

Of the three ingredients above, with 1 dou 2 sheng of water first boil two ingredients down to 5 sheng, add the dahuang, boil down to 3 sheng, and take 1 sheng warm [base: 分]. Continue until there is a bowel movement.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

Pain with blockage means long-standing dry heat has consumed the yin qi. So the medicine need not avoid harshness, and Xiao Chengqi tang (Shojokito) is used. It lacks mangxiao (bosho) compared with Da Chengqi tang (Daijokito) because this is not comparable to external pathogen binding within. It is called Sanwu tang rather than Xiao Chengqi tang to distinguish it from the double resolution of Qiwu tang.

## Notes

A clause using Houpo Sanwu tang for abdominal pain with constipation (blockage).

- Houpo Sanwu tang has the same three ingredients as Xiao Chengqi tang, but with houpo as the chief in larger amount; it is said mainly to remove qi stagnation.
- For "the following decoction" the other sources give the name Houpo Sanwu tang. For 分 in 温分一升, all other sources read 服 ("take").
