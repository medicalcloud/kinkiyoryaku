# Clause 26 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

A pulse that is tight like a twisting rope, without constancy, means there is abiding food.

A tight pulse with headache as if from wind-cold means there is abiding food in the abdomen that is not transformed (one version says: the pulse at the inch opening is tight).

## Notes

Two clauses on the pulse of abiding food.

- A tight pulse that is unsteady like a twisting rope is due to abiding food. Even with a tight pulse and headache resembling wind-cold, it may be due to abiding food in the abdomen.
- In the base the two clauses are in one paragraph, separated by 〇.
- This unit has no commentary by Xu Bin.
