# Clause 4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When a sick person has a withered-yellow complexion, is agitated but not thirsty, has replete cold in the chest, and has unceasing diarrhea, he dies.

## Notes

A dull yellow complexion, agitation without thirst, replete cold in the chest, and unceasing diarrhea indicate exhaustion of the yang qi of spleen and kidney, a fatal pattern. Xu Bin's commentary is after unit 8.
