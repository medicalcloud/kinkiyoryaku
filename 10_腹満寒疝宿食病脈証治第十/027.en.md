# Clause 27 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

*Xinbian Jingui yaolüe fanglun*, first scroll [end].

## Notes

The end title of the first scroll in the base. In the base, chapters 1 through 10 form the first scroll. Daizhige reads "middle scroll."
