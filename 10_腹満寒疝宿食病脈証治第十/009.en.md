# Clause 9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Abdominal fullness with fever for ten days, a floating, rapid pulse, and eating and drinking as usual: Houpo Qiwu tang (Seven-Ingredient Magnolia Bark Decoction) governs it.

Houpo Qiwu tang formula

Houpo half a jin; gancao and dahuang, 3 liang each; dazao 10 pieces; zhishi 5 pieces; guizhi 2 liang; shengjiang 5 liang.

Of the seven ingredients above, with 1 dou of water boil down to 4 sheng and take 8 ge warm, three times a day. For retching, add 5 ge of banxia; for diarrhea, remove the dahuang; for much cold, increase the shengjiang to half a jin.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

Here there is an exterior and also an interior, but the interior carries a dry evil; so Xiao Chengqi tang is the mainstay, combined with guizhi, gancao, shengjiang, and dazao to harmonize the exterior. The abdominal fullness at first is due to slight cold, but the stomach is originally strong, so the exterior cold does not enter and eating and drinking are as usual. But there is abdominal fullness and fever, and the pulse has stayed floating and rapid for ten days. This is disease of both exterior and interior, so both are resolved. If cold predominates, the shengjiang is increased to half a jin, meaning the exterior cold predominates. If there is retching, lodged fluid is counterflowing upward, so banxia is added. If there is diarrhea, the qi of exterior and interior is originally vacuous and cold, so dahuang is removed.

[Part of Xu Bin's formula:] houpo half a jin; gancao 3 liang; dahuang 3 liang; dazao 10 pieces.

## Notes

A clause using Houpo Qiwu tang for abdominal fullness and fever lasting ten days, with a floating, rapid pulse and normal eating and drinking.

- Houpo Qiwu tang combines Houpo Sanwu tang (the same medicinals as Xiao Chengqi tang, centered on houpo) with Guizhi tang minus shaoyao, treating interior repletion (abdominal fullness) and exterior heat together.
