# Clause 2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When a sick person's abdomen is full and pressing it causes no pain, it is vacuity; if it causes pain, it is repletion, and one may purge. If the tongue is yellow and he has not yet been purged, purge him and the yellow will go away by itself.

## Notes

A clause judging vacuity or repletion of abdominal fullness by whether pressure causes pain. Pain on pressure is repletion, which may be purged; a yellow tongue coating is a sign of replete heat in the stomach, which disappears with purging. Xu Bin's commentary is after unit 8.
