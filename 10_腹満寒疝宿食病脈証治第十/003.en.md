# Clause 3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When abdominal fullness at times lessens but returns as before, this is cold; warming medicinals should be given.

## Notes

A clause stating that abdominal fullness that sometimes eases and then returns is due not to excess (which does not lessen) but to cold, and calls for warming medicinals. Xu Bin's commentary is after unit 8.
