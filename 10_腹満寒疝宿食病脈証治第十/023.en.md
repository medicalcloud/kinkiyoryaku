# Clause 23 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

A rapid, slippery pulse is repletion; there is abiding food. Purging cures it; Da Chengqi tang is appropriate.

## Notes

A clause stating that a rapid, slippery pulse is repletion due to abiding food, to be purged with Da Chengqi tang. Xu Bin's commentary is after unit 24.

- The base lacks 也 after 実; it is supplied because all other sources have it (see the emendation note).
