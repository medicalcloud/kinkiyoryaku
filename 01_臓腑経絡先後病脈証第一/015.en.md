# Clause 15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When one suffers from a chronic illness and a sudden illness is added to it, one should first treat the sudden illness and only afterward treat the chronic illness.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

The previous [clause] concerned priority in acute disease; this concerns priority in long-standing disease. "Sudden" means incidental; therefore it comes first. "Chronic" means hard, fixed, and difficult to uproot; therefore it comes after.

## Notes

A clause stating the principle that when a new illness (sudden illness) arises in someone with a chronic illness, the new illness is treated first.

- It is generally explained that a new illness is shallow and easy to treat, and if neglected may go deep and combine with the chronic illness, whereas a chronic illness takes time to cure and so comes after.
