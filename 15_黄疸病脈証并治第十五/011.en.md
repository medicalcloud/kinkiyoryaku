# Clause 11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jaundice should take eighteen days as its term. Treated, it recovers after more than ten days; if instead it becomes severe [base: 極], it is difficult to cure.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

This says that once jaundice has formed, the disease goes from shallow to deep, so it should be treated quickly. In jaundice, after three periods the qi changes once: five days make one period, fifteen days one *qi*; adding three days to fifteen gives eighteen days, a little more than one *qi* but less than four periods. If it recovers, it recovers for good; hence "take as its term." Otherwise the root gradually deepens and becomes hard to pull out. Hence "treated, it recovers after more than ten days": beyond ten days it should surely recover; if it does not recover but worsens, it can no longer be taken as surely as at the beginning of treatment; hence "difficult to cure."

## Notes

A clause stating that jaundice takes eighteen days as one term; it normally recovers after more than ten days of treatment, and if it worsens instead, it is hard to cure.

- "Eighteen days" is often said to be based on the idea that earth (spleen) governs the last eighteen days of each season.
- For 極 in 反極, most other sources read 劇 ("severe").
