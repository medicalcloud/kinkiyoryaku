# Clause 21 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

All yellowing with abdominal pain and retching: Chaihu tang (Saikoto) is appropriate (it must be Xiao Chaihu tang (Shosaikoto); the formula appears under retching and vomiting).

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

Pathogen high and pain low is a Shaoyang pattern. Here, though yellowing is damage to spleen and stomach, it is in fact Shaoyang depressed heat; so Xiao Chaihu tang still removes the pathogen of its own meridian. But since Xiao Chaihu tang governs harmonization, this must be a case where the yellowing is not severe and not long-standing.

## Notes

A clause using Xiao Chaihu tang for jaundice with abdominal pain and retching, regarded as jaundice due to Shaoyang depressed heat.

- The base reads 諸労; it is emended to 諸黄 because all other sources so read (see the emendation note).
