# Clause 5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In alcohol jaundice, there may be no heat, the speech clear [base: 請言了; other sources: 靖言了了], with abdominal fullness, desire to vomit, and a dry nose. If the pulse is floating, first induce vomiting; if deep and wiry, first purge.

## Notes

A clause on a variant of alcohol jaundice without heat and with a clear mind but with abdominal fullness, nausea, and a dry nose: vomiting is induced if the pulse is floating, purging if deep and wiry. Xu Bin's commentary is after unit 7.

- For 請言了, most other sources read 靖言了了 ("quiet, with clear speech"); Xu Bin's edition suggests 靖 may be an error for 清.
