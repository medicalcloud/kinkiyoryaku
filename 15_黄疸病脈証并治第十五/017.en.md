# Clause 17 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

For all yellowing, use the following formula.

Zhugao Fa Jian (Lard and Hair Decoction)

Zhugao (lard) half a jin; luanfa (hair), three balls the size of a hen's egg.

Mix the two ingredients, decoct the hair in the lard; when the hair has dissolved, the medicine is ready. Take in two doses. The disease comes out through the urine.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

This is designed for jaundice with excess grain qi. The kidney is the gate of the stomach; when the grain qi of the stomach is in excess, the qi is closed and the kidney becomes dry. So lard moistens the kidney's dryness and hair ash disinhibits yin blood; taken together, the stomach's dryness is harmonized and the depression resolved. Zhongjing also uses this formula for women whose stomach qi drains downward with vaginal flatus and true binding, noting, "This is excess of grain qi; guide it out with Zhugao Fa Jian," which disinhibits the yin of Yangming to discharge the excess of grain qi. But this excess of grain qi is not comparable to grain jaundice: grain jaundice originally arises from wind and cold preventing the dispersal of grain, whereas this is true grain qi excessively excess, hot, and closed. My friend Luo Tianyou had jaundice with an abdomen as large as a drum, and a hundred medicines failed; with 4 liang of lard and 4 liang of hair ash, one prescription cured him. How could Zhongjing deceive me?

## Notes

A clause using Zhugao Fa Jian for all kinds of jaundice.

- Lard moistens dryness and hair (charred hair) removes static blood and disinhibits urination. The same formula is used for vaginal flatus in the later chapter on miscellaneous diseases of women.
- For "use the following formula," the other sources read "Zhugao Fa Jian governs it."
