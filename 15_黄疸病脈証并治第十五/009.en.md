# Clause 9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

A deep pulse, thirst with desire to drink water, and inhibited urination: all will turn yellow.

## Notes

A clause giving a deep pulse, thirst, and inhibited urination as precursors of jaundice, attributed to heat depressed in the interior with no outlet. Xu Bin's commentary is attached after the next unit.

- The base reads 脙沈; it is emended to 脈沈 because all other sources so read (see the emendation note).
