# Clause 18 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jaundice: Yinchen Wuling san (Virgate Wormwood and Five Ingredients Powder with Poria) governs it (one version says: Yinchen tang and Wuling san both govern it).

Yinchen Wuling san

Powdered yinchenhao [base: 稿] 10 *fen*; Wuling san 5 *fen* (the formula appears under phlegm-fluid retention).

Mix the two items and take a square-inch spoonful with a drink before meals, three times a day.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

This is a formula resolving both exterior and interior. But since Wuling san contains gui and zhu, it is designed for cases slightly involving deficiency. In treating jaundice, supplementation is not valued; it is kept here to provide for deficiency patterns.

[Xu Bin's formula:] powdered yinchenhao 10 *fen*; Wuling san 5 *fen*. Mix the two ingredients and take a square-inch spoonful with a drink before meals, three times a day.

## Notes

A clause using Yinchen Wuling san (yinchenhao combined with Wuling san) for jaundice. It is said to be used for jaundice in which dampness predominates over heat.

- 稿 in 茵陳稿 is an error for 蒿 (the other sources read 蒿).
