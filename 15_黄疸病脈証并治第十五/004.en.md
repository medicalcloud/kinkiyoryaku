# Clause 4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In alcohol jaundice there is always inhibited urination; its signs are heat in the heart and heat in the soles. These are its signs.

## Notes

The first of four clauses on alcohol jaundice, giving its basic signs: inhibited urination, heat in the chest, and heat in the soles. Xu Bin's commentary is attached after unit 7.
