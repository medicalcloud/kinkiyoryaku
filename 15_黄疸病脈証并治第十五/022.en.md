# Clause 22 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yellowing in a man with spontaneously free urination: give Xiao Jianzhong tang for vacuity taxation (the formula appears under vacuity taxation).

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

With no exterior signs and spontaneously free urination, there is no evil in exterior or interior. Yet there is yellowing: the central qi is not robust, so the qi of the upper burner is depressed. One should treat entirely its vacuity. When vacuity is supplemented, the qi flows freely and the depression opens; when the depression opens, the yellowing goes. Hence "Xiao Jianzhong tang for vacuity taxation is appropriate." Guizhi, shaoyao, gancao, shengjiang, and dazao can harmonize the nutritive and defensive, and malt sugar greatly supplements the center. But it says "man" only, meaning that in women there may be heat in the blood aspect, which cannot yet be known, and one should consider separately.

## Notes

A clause stating that yellowing in a man with free urination is due not to damp-heat but to vacuity (vacuity yellowing), and using Xiao Jianzhong tang from vacuity taxation.
