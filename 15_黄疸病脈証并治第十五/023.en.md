# Clause 23 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Appended formulas

Guadi tang (Kateito; Melon Stalk Decoction): treats all yellowing (the formula appears under summer heat stroke).

Mahuang Chunjiu tang (Ephedra and Pure Wine Decoction) from the *Qianjin*: treats jaundice.

Mahuang (mao) 3 liang.

Boil the single ingredient above in 5 sheng of good clear wine down to 2.5 sheng and take it all in a single dose. In winter use wine; in spring boil it in water.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

Guadi (katei) can resolve depressed heat of the upper jiao, so it suits jaundice due to upper-jiao depression. Moreover guadi governs vomiting, and vomiting also has the sense of dispersal; so it is appended to show that in treating jaundice there are also cases using the vomiting method.

This speaks of jaundice in which, due to cold, heat is depressed in the ying-nutrient aspect. Mahuang can release the yang within the ying-nutrient, and with pure wine added, the yin pathogen reaching through above and below is like frost before the sun. So this is appended to provide for treating ying-nutrient heat. Mahuang Chunjiu formula.

## Notes

Appended formulas: Guadi tang (treating jaundice by vomiting) and Mahuang Chunjiu tang from the *Qianjin* (mahuang boiled in wine to induce sweating). This is the last unit of the chapter on jaundice.

- The Guadi tang formula is Yiwu Guadi tang in the chapter on tetany, dampness, and summer heat stroke.
