# Clause 23 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Appended formulas

Guadi tang (Melon Stalk Decoction): treats all yellowing (the formula appears under summerheat stroke).

Mahuang Chunjiu tang (Ephedra and Pure Wine Decoction) from the *Qianjin*: treats jaundice.

Mahuang 3 liang.

Boil the single ingredient above in 5 sheng of good clear wine down to 2.5 sheng and take it all in a single dose. In winter use wine; in spring boil it in water.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

Guadi can resolve depressed heat of the upper burner, so it suits jaundice due to upper-burner depression. Moreover guadi governs vomiting, and vomiting also has the sense of dispersal; so it is appended to show that in treating jaundice there are also cases using the vomiting method.

This speaks of jaundice in which, due to cold, heat is depressed in the nutritive aspect. Mahuang can release the yang within the nutritive, and with pure wine added, the yin evil reaching through above and below is like frost before the sun. So this is appended to provide for treating nutritive heat. Mahuang Chunjiu formula.

## Notes

Appended formulas: Guadi tang (treating jaundice by vomiting) and Mahuang Chunjiu tang from the *Qianjin* (mahuang boiled in wine to induce sweating). This is the last unit of the chapter on jaundice.

- The Guadi tang formula is Yiwu Guadi tang in the chapter on tetany, dampness, and summerheat stroke.
