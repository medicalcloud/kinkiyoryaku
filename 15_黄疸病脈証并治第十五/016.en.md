# Clause 16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

For all who suffer from yellowing, simply disinhibit the urine. If the pulse is floating, it should be resolved by sweating; Guizhi jia Huangqi tang is appropriate and governs it (the formula appears under water disease).

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

From here on all are formulas for regular jaundice; hence "all who suffer from yellowing," regardless of how it was contracted. Yellowing generally belongs to qi depression, and urination is the master of qi transformation; so simply disinhibit the urine. When the qi of the lower orifice flows, the qi of all the orifices cannot stay closed for long. But where the disease qi is entirely stagnant in the exterior, the outward-moving qi is strong, and disinhibiting urination does not help; so with a floating pulse, Guizhi tang resolves the flesh and releases the exterior, huangqi supports from within, and thin gruel aids the right; then the evil naturally cannot remain.

Discussion: Among those with jaundice, not only are grain, alcohol, and sexual-taxation jaundice distinguished, but even in regular jaundice the disease evil attaches differently according to where the vacuity is. I once treated a person with jaundice for whom a hundred medicines had failed and who was near death; seeing that it leaned upward, I had him take about a jin of fresh shegan alone, and he recovered. I saw another leaning toward yin and had him take several jin of fresh yimucao (motherwort) alone, and he recovered. Whenever jaundice was just beginning and was not grain, alcohol, or sexual-taxation jaundice, I had the root, leaves, and seeds of cheqian (plantain) pounded together for their natural juice, taken with wine by the bowlful, and they recovered. Even one bedridden and unable to rise I had place several cups of the natural juice of cheqian alone by the bed to drink at will, and he recovered. So the theories of sweating and purging are also stated provisionally to stimulate understanding; can one do without flexibility?

## Notes

A clause giving disinhibiting urination as the great principle of treating jaundice, and sweating with Guizhi jia Huangqi tang when the pulse is floating with disease in the exterior. The formula is in the chapter on water qi.

- Xu Bin's "Discussion" recounts his own cases using shegan, yimucao (motherwort), cheqian (plantain), and the like.
