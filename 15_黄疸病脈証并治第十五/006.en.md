# Clause 6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Alcohol jaundice with heat in the heart and desire to retch: inducing vomiting cures it.

## Notes

A clause stating that alcohol jaundice with heat in the chest and nausea is cured by inducing vomiting. Xu Bin's commentary is after the next unit.

- For 欲嘔, Daizhige and Xu Bin's edition read 欲吐.
