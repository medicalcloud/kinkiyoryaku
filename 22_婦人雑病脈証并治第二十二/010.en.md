# Clause 10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Disease below the girdle, with inhibited menses, fullness and pain of the lesser abdomen, and menses appearing twice in one month: Tuguagen san (Dokakonsan) governs it.

Tuguagen san (Trichosanthes Root Powder) formula (it also governs swelling of the genitals)

Tuguagen (dokakon; root of Trichosanthes cucumeroides), shaoyao (shakuyaku), guizhi (keishi), and zhechong (shachu), 3 *fen* each.

Pound the four ingredients above into powder and take a square-inch spoonful with wine, three times a day.

## Notes

A clause using Tuguagen san for irregular menses with distension and pain of the lower abdomen and menstruation twice a month.

- A formula that removes static blood with tuguagen and zhechong and regulates the blood vessels with shaoyao and guizhi.
- Xu Bin's text is missing the leaves for this part, so there is no commentary (see unit 9).
