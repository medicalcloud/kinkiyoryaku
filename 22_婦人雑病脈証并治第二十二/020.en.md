# Clause 20 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the shaoyin pulse is slippery and rapid, sores will arise in the vagina. When the vagina is eroded by sores and ulcerated, wash it with Langya tang.

Langya tang (Agrimony Root Decoction) formula

Langya 3 liang.

Of the one ingredient above, with 4 sheng of water boil down to half a sheng; wrap silk floss around a chopstick like a cocoon, soak it in the decoction, and drip it into the vagina, four times a day.

## Notes

A clause on washing ulcerated sores of the genitals with Langya tang.

- A slippery, rapid shaoyin pulse (the chi pulse, or the pulse at Taixi) is said to indicate damp-heat in the lower burner.
- Xu Bin's text is missing the leaves for this part, so there is no commentary.
