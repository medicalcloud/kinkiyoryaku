# Clause 11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the cun kou pulse is wiry and large, wiry means diminished and large means hollow; diminished means cold and hollow means vacuity. When cold and vacuity contend, it is called leather. In women there is half-term delivery or spotting. Xuanfuhua tang governs it.

Xuanfuhua tang (Inula Flower Decoction) formula

Xuanfuhua (inula flower) 3 liang; scallion 14 stalks; xinjiang (fresh red-dyed silk) a little.

Of the three ingredients above, with 3 sheng of water boil down to 1 sheng and take it in a single dose.

## Notes

A clause explaining the leather pulse (cun kou pulse wiry and large), stating that in women it leads to miscarriage or spotting, and using Xuanfuhua tang.

- The first part is nearly the same as the leather-pulse clauses in the chapter on blood impediment and vacuity taxation and the chapter on fright palpitations, nosebleed, bloody stool, chest fullness, and static blood, which mention blood loss and seminal loss in men. Xuanfuhua tang is also used for liver fixity in the chapter on wind, cold, and accumulations of the five zang.
- Xu Bin's text is missing the leaves for this part, so there is no commentary.
