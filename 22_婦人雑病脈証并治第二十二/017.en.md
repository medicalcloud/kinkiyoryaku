# Clause 17 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

For the various painful disorders in a woman's abdomen, Danggui Shaoyao san governs it.

Danggui Shaoyao san (Angelica and Peony Powder) formula (appears above under pregnancy)

## Notes

A clause using Danggui Shaoyao san for the various pains in a woman's abdomen. The formula is in the pregnancy chapter (chapter 20, unit 5).

- Xu Bin's text is missing the leaves for this part, so there is no commentary.
