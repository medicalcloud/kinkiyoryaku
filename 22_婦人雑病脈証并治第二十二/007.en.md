# Clause 7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When a woman spits foamy drool and the physician instead purges her, and stuffiness immediately forms below the heart, one should first treat the spitting of foamy drool; Xiao Qinglong tang governs it. When the foamy drool stops, then treat the stuffiness; Xiexin tang governs it.

Xiao Qinglong tang (Minor Blue-Green Dragon Decoction) formula (appears under lung abscess)

Xiexin tang (Heart-Draining Decoction) formula (appears under fright palpitations)

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

This clause is what is later called "congealing and hardening above, with retching and vomiting of drool and spittle." Women habitually have accumulated cold in the lower jiao that congeals above and becomes retained fluid inside, and when they also contract visiting cold, they spit foamy drool. Accumulated cold is the root and visiting pathogen the branch. But the pathogen is high, in the lung, so it should be treated according to cold damage with "water qi below the heart"; only there, without accumulated cold, there is dry retching, whereas here, with congealed cold, there is foamy drool. The physician purging it punishes the stomach, which has not yet received pathogen and is guiltless; hence "instead." The medicine damages the stomach and visiting qi stirs the diaphragm, so stuffiness immediately forms below the heart. After all, though stuffiness has formed below, the visiting cold and water qi above have not yet submitted, so one should first treat the root. Thus Xiao Qinglong tang governs it, and the water qi and visiting cold both depart and the foamy drool stops. The stuffiness is merely yin pathogen from mistaken purgation lodging below the heart, so dahuang, huangqin, and huanglian drastically drain the pathogen of stuffiness and depression below the heart, and one dose can cure it.

## Notes

A clause stating that when a woman spitting foamy drool has been mistakenly purged, producing stuffiness below the heart, one first treats the foamy drool with Xiao Qinglong tang and then the stuffiness with Xiexin tang.

- It shows the principle of order: treat the exterior (upper) first and the stuffiness (lower) afterward.
- Xiexin tang refers to the Xiexin tang (dahuang, huanglian, huangqin) of the chapter on fright palpitations, nosebleed, bloody stool, chest fullness, and static blood.
