# Clause 6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

A woman with visceral agitation, prone to sorrow and wanting to weep, as if possessed by spirits, frequently yawning and stretching: Gan Mai Dazao tang governs it.

Gancao Xiaomai Dazao tang (Licorice, Wheat, and Jujube Decoction) formula

Gancao 2 liang; xiaomai (wheat) 1 sheng; dazao 10 pieces.

Of the three ingredients above, with 6 sheng of water boil down to 3 sheng and take warm in three doses. It also supplements spleen qi.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

This clause is what is later called "or there is anxiety, sorrow, and much anger." 蔵 means the five zang. 燥 (dryness) means that when a woman's blood chamber first receives accumulated cold, and after long depression it turns to heat, the zang become dry because of it. The *Lingshu* says, "The first yin governs the pivot; when the closing of the pivot breaks, liver qi expires and one is prone to sorrow." So one knows that dry qi overrides the liver, causing sorrow and wanting to weep. "As if possessed by spirits" is because the disease comes from the blood, so a yin image appears. The *Lingshu* says, "Stomach disease brings frequent stretching, frequent yawning, and a dark face." So one knows that dry qi invades the stomach, causing yawning and stretching. If liver qi were moistened by fluids and sovereign fire were not hyperactive, zang yin would not be dry; how then would it override the liver and invade the stomach? Now that there is sorrow, yawning, and stretching, one knows there is heat in liver yin, and also heat in the heart and lung. So Gan Mai Dazao tang governs it. Xiaomai can harmonize the visiting heat of liver yin and nourish heart fluid, and also has the merit of dispersing vexation, disinhibiting urine, and stopping sweat; so it is the sovereign. Gancao drains heart fire, rescues the lung, and harmonizes the stomach; so it is the minister. Dazao regulates the stomach and frees the dryness congested above; so it is the assistant. The disease is rooted in the blood; the heart is master of the blood and child of the liver. When heart fire is drained and the qi above is harmonized, stomach qi reaches downward, the liver is moistened, lung qi is regulated, the dryness stops, and the disease is removed by itself. "Supplements liver [spleen] qi" because fire is the mother of earth; when the heart is nourished, fire can generate earth.

## Notes

A clause using Gan Mai Dazao tang for visceral agitation with groundless sorrow and wanting to weep, and frequent yawning and stretching.

- For 蔵躁 ("visceral agitation"), Xu Bin's text reads 蔵燥, and Xu Bin takes it as dryness of the zang.
- All other sources give gancao as 3 liang.
- The end of Xu Bin's commentary reads "supplements liver qi," but the main text reads "supplements spleen qi," and the commentary's explanation (fire generating earth) also concerns the spleen.
