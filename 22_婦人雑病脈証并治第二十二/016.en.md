# Clause 16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

For the sixty-two kinds of wind in women, and stabbing pain from blood and qi in the abdomen, Honglanhua jiu governs it.

Honglanhua jiu (Safflower Wine) formula (probably not Zhongjing's formula)

Honglanhua (safflower) 1 liang.

Decoct the one ingredient above in 1 large sheng of wine until reduced by half, and take half of it in a single dose. If [the pain] does not stop, take it again.

## Notes

A clause using Honglanhua jiu for the various winds of women and stabbing pain from blood and qi in the abdomen.

- A formula invigorating the blood with honglanhua (safflower). A note after the formula says "probably not Zhongjing's formula."
- Xu Bin's text is missing the leaves for this part, so there is no commentary.
