# Clause 18 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Pain in a woman's abdomen: Xiao Jianzhong tang (Shokenchuto) governs it.

Xiao Jianzhong tang (Minor Center-Building Decoction) formula (appears above under deficiency/consumption)

## Notes

A clause using Xiao Jianzhong tang for abdominal pain in women. The formula is in the chapter on blood impediment and deficiency/consumption.

- Xu Bin's text is missing the leaves for this part, so there is no commentary.
