# Clause 12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When a woman has sunken menses, with black spotting that does not resolve, Jiao Jiang tang governs it (your servant [Lin] Yi and others collated the various editions and found no Jiao Jiang tang formula; we suppose it is the Jiao Ai tang under pregnancy).

## Notes

A clause using Jiao Jiang tang (Ass-Hide Glue and Ginger Decoction) for sunken menses with unceasing spotting of black blood. The formula has not survived.

- The interlinear note is by Lin Yi and others of the Song Bureau for Revising Medical Texts, who suggest it is the Xiong Gui Jiao Ai tang of the pregnancy chapter (chapter 20, unit 4).
- Xu Bin's text is missing the leaves for this part, so there is no commentary.
