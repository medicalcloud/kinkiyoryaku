# Clause 21 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When stomach qi discharges downward and there is vaginal flatus that is truly noisy, this is excess of grain qi. Conduct it with Gao Fa jian.

Gao Fa jian (Lard and Hair Brew) formula (appears under jaundice)

Formula for children's gan worms eating the teeth (probably not Zhongjing's formula)

Xionghuang (realgar); tingli (lepidium seed).

Powder the two ingredients above; take lard of the la month [base: la day] and melt it; with four or five pagoda-tree twigs with heads wrapped in silk floss, dab on the medicine and cauterize.

## Notes

A clause using Gao Fa jian for vaginal flatus, followed by the formula for children's gan worms eating the teeth at the end of this chapter.

- Gao Fa jian (Zhugao Fa jian) is in the jaundice chapter.
- The formula for children's gan worms eating the teeth has the note "probably not Zhongjing's formula" after it. In Xu Bin's text only the end of the commentary on this formula survives, attached after the commentary of unit 9 (see unit 9).
