# Clause 15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When a woman's menses are blocked and inhibited, the viscus is hard with an unceasing lump, there is dry blood inside, and white matter comes down, Fanshi wan governs it.

Fanshi wan (Alum Pill) formula

Fanshi (alum) 3 *fen* (calcined); xingren 1 *fen*.

Powder the two ingredients above, mix with refined honey into pills the size of a jujube pit, and insert into the viscus (vagina). In severe cases, insert again.

## Notes

A clause using Fanshi wan, inserted into the vagina, for blocked menses, a hard uterus, dry blood inside, and white vaginal discharge.

- It is not taken internally but is an external formula inserted into the vagina.
- Xu Bin's text is missing the leaves for this part, so there is no commentary.
