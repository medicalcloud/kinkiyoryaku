# Clause 14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When a woman's menses do not flow down freely, Didang [base: 抵党] tang governs it (it also treats men with fullness and tension of the bladder and static blood).

Didang tang (Teitoto; Resistance Decoction) formula

Shuizhi (leech) 30 pieces (dry-fried); mengchong (bochu; gadfly) 30 pieces (dry-fried, wings and legs removed); taoren (tonin) 20 pieces (skin and tips removed); dahuang (daio) 3 liang (soaked in wine).

Powder the four ingredients above, boil with 5 sheng of water down to 3 sheng, remove the dregs, and take 1 sheng warm.

## Notes

A clause using Didang tang for menses that do not flow down freely.

- Didang tang is a drastic formula attacking and purging static blood, also used for blood amassment in the middle Taiyang chapter of the *Shanghan lun*.
- The base's 抵党 is 抵当 in all other sources.
- Xu Bin's text is missing the leaves for this part, so there is no commentary.
