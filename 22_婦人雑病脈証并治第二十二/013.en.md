# Clause 13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When a woman's lesser abdomen is full like an overturned bowl, urination is slightly difficult but there is no thirst, and it is after childbirth: this is water and blood binding together in the blood chamber. Dahuang Gansui tang (Daiokanzuito) governs it.

Dahuang Gansui tang (Rhubarb and Kansui Decoction) formula

Dahuang (daio) 4 liang; gansui (kanzui; kansui) 2 liang; ejiao (akyo) 2 liang.

Of the three ingredients above, with 3 sheng of water boil down to 1 sheng and take it in a single dose. The blood should come down.

## Notes

A clause using Dahuang Gansui tang when, after childbirth, the lower abdomen bulges like an overturned bowl, with slightly difficult urination and no thirst: water and blood binding in the blood chamber.

- A formula that purges static blood with dahuang and water with gansui, and nourishes blood with ejiao.
- Xu Bin's text is missing the leaves for this part, so there is no commentary.
