# Clause 4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yangming disease with bleeding and delirious speech: this is heat entering the blood chamber. There is only sweating of the head. One should needle Qimen and drain it according to the repletion. When there is a generalized light sweat, there is recovery.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

This says that yangming disease also has heat entering the blood chamber, differing only in bleeding and sweating of the head. Yangming disease is headache, dry nose, and sleeplessness. If heat entered the yangming fu, there would surely be sweating, delirious speech, and so on — a pattern to be purged; why would it stir the blood? Because there is bleeding with delirious speech, one knows it is heat entering the blood chamber. Yangming should have sweating of the whole body; but here there is heat in the blood and the blood is consumed; when it is consumed, the lower part is vacuous and contends with the evil, and the body is held by dry yin, so there is no sweat. Only at the head, where yin cannot enter and yang still flows, is there sweat. This disease too arises from liver repletion and yangming should not be blamed, so again Qimen is needled, saying "drain it according to the repletion." 濈然 means a faint sweat-like moisture over the whole body. With sweating, the liver is no longer forceful and yin and yang are balanced; hence [recovery].

## Notes

A clause stating that yangming disease with bleeding, delirious speech, and sweating only of the head is heat entering the blood chamber, treated by needling Qimen. The same clause appears in the yangming chapter of the *Shanghan lun* (corresponding to clause 216).
