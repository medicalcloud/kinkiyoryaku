# Clause 9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Appended formulas

Xuming tang (Zokumeito; Life-Prolonging Decoction) from the *Gujin lu yan*: treats wind stroke paralysis (*fei*), in which the body cannot hold itself together, the mouth cannot speak, the mind is clouded and does not know where the pain is, or there is hypertonicity with inability to turn over ([Yao] says it is the same as Da Xuming tang, and also treats women who have lost blood after childbirth, and the elderly and children).

Mahuang (mao), guizhi (keishi), danggui, renshen (ninjin), shigao (sekko), ganjiang (kankyo), gancao (kanzo), 3 liang each; xiongqiong; xingren (kyonin) 40 pieces.

Of the nine ingredients above, with 1 dou of water boil down to 4 sheng. There should be a slight sweat: cover the back lightly and sit leaning on a low table. When sweat comes out, recovery follows; if there is no sweat, take more. There are no prohibitions, but do not be exposed to wind. It also treats inability to lie [on the back] but only face down, cough with counterflow and ascending qi, and puffy swelling of the face and eyes.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

[Xu Bin's text:] Xuming tang from the *Gujin lu yan*: treats wind stroke paralysis, in which the body cannot hold itself together, the mouth cannot ...

*Fei* is another name for impediment. The ying-nutrient and wei-defense are originally deficient, and wind enters and impedes them; so the outer ying-nutrient and wei-defense are impeded, and the body cannot hold itself together, or there is hypertonicity with inability to turn over; the inner ying-nutrient and wei-defense are impeded, so the mouth cannot speak and the mind is clouded and does not know where the pain is. Since it comes from external contraction, Mahuang tang (Maoto) is used to move the ying-nutrient and wei-defense, ganjiang and shigao to regulate cold and heat, and chuanxiong, danggui, and renshen are added to nourish the deficiency. A slight sweat must be obtained so that the pathogen still comes out through the exterior. As for inability to lie but face down, cough with counterflow and ascending qi, and puffy swelling of the face and eyes, wind has entered and impeded the qi of the chest and diaphragm, so the lung qi cannot flow and only counterflows upward to attack the face and eyes. So this too it governs.

[Xu Bin's formula:] mahuang 3 liang; guizhi 3 liang; xingren 40 kernels; gancao 3 liang; ganjiang 3 liang; shigao 3 liang; chuanxiong 1 liang 5 *qian*; danggui 3 liang; renshen 3 liang.

## Notes

The appended formulas (supplemented from other books by the Song editors) begin here. Xuming tang from the *Gujin lu yan fang* is said to treat wind stroke paralysis (*fei*: paralysis of the body, speech disorder, clouded consciousness).

- It consists of Mahuang tang (mahuang, guizhi, xingren, gancao) plus shigao, ganjiang, danggui, renshen, and xiongqiong.
- In the base formula the amounts of mahuang, guizhi, danggui, and xiongqiong are missing, and the paragraph breaks partway. Other sources give xiongqiong as 1 liang.
- After "boil down to 4 sheng," most other sources have "take 1 sheng warm."
- Before 云与大続命同 in the interlinear note, all other sources have 姚, making it "Yao [Sengyuan] says it is the same as Da Xuming tang"; the translation supplies it.
