# Clause 5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The instep yang pulse is floating and slippery. Slippery means grain qi is replete; floating means sweat comes out spontaneously.

## Notes

Together with the next unit ("The lesser yin pulse ..."), the first half of a passage giving drinking alcohol and exposure to wind while sweating as causes of joint-running disease. Xu Bin's commentary follows the next unit.

- The instep yang (*fuyang*, the pulse on the top of the foot) reflects the spleen and stomach. Slippery is taken as replete damp-heat from food and drink (alcohol) in the stomach, floating as heat causing sweating.
- The base reads 跌陽; it is emended to 趺陽 because all other sources so read (see the emendation note).
