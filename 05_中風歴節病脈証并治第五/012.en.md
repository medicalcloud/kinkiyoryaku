# Clause 12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Master Cui's Bawei wan (Eight-Ingredient Pill): treats leg qi that has risen into the lesser abdomen with numbness.

Gandihuang (kanjio; dried rehmannia) 8 liang; shanzhuyu (sanshuyu; cornus) and shuyu (shoyo; dioscorea) [base: 署蕷], 4 liang each; zexie (takusha; alisma), fuling (bukuryo), and mudanpi (moutan bark), 3 liang each; guizhi (keishi) and fuzi (bushi; blast-fried), 1 liang each.

Powder the eight ingredients above, mix with refined honey, and make pills the size of firmiana seeds. Take fifteen pills down with wine, twice a day.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

Having discussed joint wind, he extends the discussion here. Although joint wind is due to both wind and dampness, in general it mostly has swollen feet and cold shins. This means the disease is in the lower jiao; the lower jiao belongs to yin, and whether yin is deficient and pathogen has taken advantage of it is not yet known. But for leg qi that has risen into the lesser abdomen with numbness, Bawei wan is the mainstay. Leg qi need not involve wind, but the treatment of moving yang and removing dampness is quite similar. Only, since it is guizhi, it has the power to move the ying-nutrient and wei-defense particularly; rougui (cinnamon bark) would go solely downward and supplement. People today habitually use rougui, not knowing this principle.

[Xu Bin's formula:] dried prepared dihuang 8 liang; shanzhuyu 4 liang; shuyu 4 liang; zexie 3 liang; fuling 3 liang; mudanpi 3 liang; guizhi 1 liang; fuzi 1 liang.

## Notes

An appended formula, Master Cui's Bawei wan (the later Bawei Dihuang wan (Hachimijiogan) or Shenqi wan (Jinkigan)), for leg qi reaching the lower abdomen with numbness. It appears again later as "Bawei Shenqi wan (Hachimijinkigan)" in the chapter on blood impediment and deficiency/consumption, among others.

- A representative formula for supplementing kidney yin and yang: dihuang, shanzhuyu, and shanyao supplement kidney yin; guizhi and fuzi warm kidney yang; zexie, fuling, and mudanpi remove water and heat.
- 署蕷 is a graphic error for 薯蕷 (shanyao), which the other sources read.
- The base reads 小腹; it is emended to 少腹 because all other sources so read (see the emendation note).
- Xu Bin stresses that the gui in the formula should be guizhi, not the later rougui.
