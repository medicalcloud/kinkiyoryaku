# Clause 6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The anterior tibial (Fuyang) pulse should be hidden, but now it is instead tight; this is because there was originally cold *shan* or conglomeration with abdominal pain. If the physician purges instead, purging immediately causes fullness of the chest and shortness of breath.

## Notes

A clause stating that when a person with water disease has, contrary to expectation, a tight anterior tibial (Fuyang) pulse, it is because of a pre-existing condition such as cold *shan*, and mistaken purging causes chest fullness and shortness of breath. Xu Bin's commentary is attached after the next unit.
