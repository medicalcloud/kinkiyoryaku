# Clause 9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The pulse at the inch opening is wiry and tight. Wiry means the wei-defensive qi does not move, so there is aversion to cold; water does not moisten and flow [properly] but runs into the intestines.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

This says that some pulses of water disease about to form carry wiriness and tightness, clarifying why water does not follow its old path. A tight pulse belongs to cold; wiry and tight means wiry like a bowstring and not moving under pressure. Wiry means the wei-defensive qi is bound by cold and does not move; with no wei-defensive qi outside, there is aversion to cold, and the water cannot be moved. So it follows its own course and, unable to moisten and flow, runs into the intestines. When water does not run straight into the intestines, it cannot but spill sideways into the flesh and skin.

## Notes

A clause stating that a wiry, tight cun pulse means the wei-defensive qi does not move, causing aversion to cold, and the water does not flow properly but runs into the intestines.
