# Clause 18 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The master said: In all who have water, if there is swelling below the lower back, urination should be disinhibited; if there is swelling above the lower back, sweating should be induced; then there is recovery.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

Earlier, water patterns were divided into inside and outside, exterior and interior; here they are divided again into upper and lower. One should know that where the swelling reaches, the water reaches. So dividing treatment by inside and outside is not as precise as dividing it by upper and lower. Hence "all who have water," no longer distinguishing names such as wind water and regular water. "Swelling below the lower back: urination should be disinhibited" — below the lower back, yin is the master of function, so cleansing the clean residence (freeing the bladder) is urgent. "Swelling above the lower back: sweating should be induced" — above the lower back, yang is the master of function, so opening the ghost gates (the sweat pores) is urgent.

## Notes

A clause stating the great principle of treating water disease: edema below the waist is treated by promoting urination, edema above the waist by sweating.

- In the base this clause is written continuously after the preceding kidney-water clause, separated by ○.
