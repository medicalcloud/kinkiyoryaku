# Clause 13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Heart water: the body is heavy with shallow breathing, one cannot lie down, there is restlessness [base: 燥 "dryness"], and the genitals are swollen.

## Notes

The first of five clauses on water of the five zang organs (heart, liver, lung, spleen, kidney water). Xu Bin's commentary is attached after unit 17.

- For 燥 in 煩而燥, most other sources read 躁 ("agitation").
