# Clause 23 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Skin edema disease with swelling of the limbs, water qi in the skin, and twitching of the limbs: Fangji Fuling tang (Boibukuryoto; Stephania and Poria Decoction) governs it.

Fangji Fuling tang

Fangji (boi) 3 liang; huangqi (ogi) 3 liang; guizhi (keishi) 3 liang; fuling (bukuryo) 6 liang; gancao (kanzo) 2 liang.

Of the five ingredients above, with 6 sheng of water boil down to 2 sheng and take warm in three doses.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

Note: the earlier signs of skin edema are not all listed here; naming "skin edema" sums them up. Further, it specially sets out "swelling of the limbs with twitching," clarifying the state of water qi in the skin, and then the meaning of the word "skin" is clear. The medicine is also Fangji Huangqi tang (Boiogito), but without zhu and with gui and fuling added, because the dampness of wind edema is in the meridians, nearer the inside, while that of skin edema is in the skin, nearer the outside; so only fuling with gui percolates the dampness of the whole body, and zhu is not used to dry the central qi. Shengjiang (shokyo) and dazao (taiso) are not used because the dampness is not in the ying-nutrient and wei-defense of the upper jiao, so there is no need to diffuse it.

[Xu Bin's formula:] fangji, huangqi, and guizhi, 3 liang each; fuling 6 liang; gancao 2 liang.

## Notes

A clause using Fangji Fuling tang for skin edema with swelling of the limbs and twitching due to water qi in the skin.

- It is like Fangji Huangqi tang without baizhu (byakujutsu), shengjiang, and dazao, plus guizhi and fuling, said to disinhibit water in the skin.
