# Clause 5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Interior water: the whole body, face, and eyes are yellow and swollen, the pulse is deep, and urination is inhibited; hence the water disease. If urination is spontaneously free, the fluids are lost, hence thirst. Yuebi jia Zhu tang (Eppikajutsuto) governs it (the formula appears below).

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

This speaks of generalized edema distinguished by an accompanying yellow color. Because it is distinguished from wind edema and skin edema, which are on the outside, it is called "interior water." In water disease the face and eyes are usually fresh and clean; here alone the whole body, face, and eyes are yellow and swollen, meaning long depression has turned to heat. Also, in water disease urination must be difficult without thirst; but if depression lasts long, fluids are lost and heat congests, causing thirst, and urination is, on the contrary, spontaneously free. The heat is in the qi aspect of the upper jiao, so Yuebi tang (Eppito) moves yang and transforms heat, and zhu is added to overcome the water.

## Notes

A clause using Yuebi jia Zhu tang for interior water with yellow swelling of the whole body and face, a deep pulse, and inhibited urination.

- The phrase "if urination is spontaneously free, the fluids are lost, hence thirst" does not fit the context, and many regard it as a displaced passage.
- The formula of Yuebi jia Zhu tang is in unit 22 below.
