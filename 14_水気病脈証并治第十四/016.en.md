# Clause 16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Spleen water: the abdomen is enlarged, the limbs suffer from heaviness, fluids are not generated, one suffers only from shallow breathing, and urination is difficult.

## Notes

The signs of spleen water. Xu Bin's commentary is after the next unit.

- The base reads 但若少気; it is emended to 苦 because all other sources so read (see the emendation note).
