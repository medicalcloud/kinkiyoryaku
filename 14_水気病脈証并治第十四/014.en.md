# Clause 14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Liver water: the abdomen is enlarged, one cannot turn over by oneself, the area below the ribs and the abdomen hurt, at times a little fluid arises, and urination passes intermittently.

## Notes

The signs of liver water. Xu Bin's commentary is after unit 17.

- The base reads 転則; it is emended to 転側 because all other sources so read (see the emendation note).
