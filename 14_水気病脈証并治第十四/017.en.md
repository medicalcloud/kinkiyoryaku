# Clause 17 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Kidney water: the abdomen is enlarged, the umbilicus swollen, the lower back aches, one cannot urinate, the area below the genitals is damp like the sweat on an ox's nose, the feet are counterflow cold, and the face, on the contrary, is thin.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

This too is generalized edema, finely distinguished as a basis for treatment. When a person has water disease for a long time, it passes from one to another and all are generally ill; but at the beginning there are cases caused by deficiency of the heart alone. When water comes from the heart, it is heart water. The heart is the sovereign fire, governing the yang of the whole body. When water afflicts it, the sovereign fire cannot extend, and the yang of the whole body has nothing to draw on, so it cannot be light and vigorous, and is heavy. Fire is the source of qi; when fire is afflicted, qi is short. When water counterflows and the wei-defensive qi cannot enter the yin, one cannot lie down. The more the sovereign fire is depressed, the more the yin fire stirs, so there is restlessness. Heart and kidney originally interact; now the heart is suppressed by water and cannot interact with the kidney, and what interacts is the surplus dampness outside the heart, so the genitals swell — that is, swelling of the penis.

There are cases caused by deficiency of the liver alone. When water comes from the liver, it is liver water. Wood cannot drain water to help earth, so the spleen is damaged and the abdomen enlarged. Wood qi extends upward; when ill it runs rampant sideways and becomes rigid, so one cannot turn over by oneself. The residence of the liver is in the ribsides, and its qi connects with the lesser abdomen, so below the ribs and the abdomen hurt. The large intestine governs the fluids; when liver wood insults earth, earth declines and the water is turbid and rough. But it is not an original disease of the large intestine; when the liver qi is somewhat relieved, the Yangming qi flows freely, a little fluid arises, and urination passes intermittently, because the liver governs free coursing. This is how the liver alone differs from spleen and kidney.

There are cases caused by deficiency of the lung alone. When water comes from the lung, it is lung water. The lung governs qi and moves it through the whole body; when ill, the healthy qi does not spread, so the body swells. Urination must come out through qi transformation; the qi does not transform, so urination is difficult. When the lung qi is ill, it cannot receive what the spleen qi conveys upward; lung and spleen are both afflicted, causing duck-stool diarrhea — clear and unformed like duck droppings.

There are cases caused by spleen deficiency. When water comes from the spleen, it is spleen water. The spleen is extreme yin and governs the abdomen, so when the spleen is ill the abdomen is enlarged. The limbs belong to the spleen; the spleen is afflicted, so they suffer from heaviness. The spleen is Taiyin damp earth; it transforms and generates with dampness, yet dislikes dampness and likes dryness. Now water afflicts it, so earth is depressed and fluids are not generated, and one suffers only from shallow breathing. When spleen earth cannot restrain water, water overflows sideways and does not follow its old path, so urination is difficult.

There are cases caused by deficiency of the kidney alone. When water comes from the kidney, it is kidney water. The kidney is originally the master of water; with water disease it becomes double yin, and the abdomen is enlarged. The lower half of the body is governed by the kidney, so the umbilicus swells and the lower back aches. When the kidney is ill, opening and closing lose their authority, clear and turbid are not separated, heart fire has no restraint, metal is damaged and cannot transform qi, so one cannot urinate. The kidney contains true fire, while the genuine qi of the zang organ belongs to cold water; when dampness afflicts it, the dragon fire is depressed and presses the cold outward, so below the genitals it is damp like the sweat on an ox's nose, cold dampness never drying. But the kidney yin is actually deficient, so the feet are counterflow cold. The kidney qi is blocked by water and cannot nourish above, so unlike the water diseases of other zang organs, in which the face and eyes are fresh and lustrous, the face alone is thin.
