# Clause 22 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wind edema with aversion to wind, swelling of the whole body, a floating pulse without thirst, continuous spontaneous sweating, and no great heat: Yuebi tang (Maidservant from Yue Decoction) governs it.

Yuebi tang formula

Mahuang 6 liang; shigao half a jin; shengjiang 3 liang; dazao 15 pieces; gancao 2 liang.

Of the five ingredients above, with 6 sheng of water first boil the mahuang, remove the froth on top, add the other medicinals, boil down to 3 sheng, and take warm in three doses. For aversion to wind, add one fuzi (blast-fried); for wind edema, add 4 liang of zhu (*Gujin lu yan*).

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

In the previous pattern the body is heavy, so dampness predominates. Here the whole body is swollen, so wind predominates and the qi is strong. Wind is a yang pathogen, and a floating pulse means heat. Further, the sweat does not come out suddenly but continuously and spontaneously, as if steamed out by the qi. But with no great heat outside, the exterior part is small and the internal heat large. So Yuebi tang governs it: mahuang releases the yang, shigao clears the heat, gancao harmonizes the center, and shengjiang and dazao free the ying-nutrient and wei-defense and diffuse the yang qi. This formula is especially heavy, because compared with the previous one there is more wind and more qi, hence more heat, and it is acute wind, so it aims to clear it away with one prescription. If there is aversion to cold, we know the inside is deficient, so fuzi is added. The *Gujin lu yan* adds zhu to drive out dampness as well.

## Notes

A clause using Yuebi tang for wind edema with generalized swelling, a floating pulse, spontaneous sweating, and no great heat.

- Yuebi tang, of five ingredients — mahuang, shigao, shengjiang, dazao, gancao — removes exterior water by sweating and clears internal heat with shigao.
- The base reads 急風 ("acute wind"); it is emended to 悪風 ("aversion to wind") because all other sources so read (see the emendation note).
