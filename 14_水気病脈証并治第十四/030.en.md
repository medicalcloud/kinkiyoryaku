# Clause 30 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qi aspect, with hardness below the heart as large as a plate, its rim like a circling cup, [due to] water-fluid retention: the following formula governs it.

Gui Jiang Cao Zao Huang Xin Fu tang (Cinnamon Twig, Fresh Ginger, Licorice, Jujube, Ephedra, Asarum, and Aconite Decoction) formula

Guizhi (keishi) and shengjiang (shokyo), 3 liang [each]; gancao (kanzo) 2 liang; dazao (taiso) 12 pieces; mahuang (mao) and xixin (saishin), 2 liang each; fuzi (bushi) 1 piece (blast-fried).

Of the seven ingredients above, with 7 sheng of water boil the mahuang, remove the froth on top, add the other medicinals, boil down to 2 sheng, and take warm in three doses. When sweat comes out with a feeling like insects crawling in the skin, recovery follows.

## Notes

A clause using Guizhi qu Shaoyao (shakuyaku) jia Mahuang Xixin Fuzi tang (Maosaishinbushito) for qi-aspect disease with a hard mass below the heart as large as a plate with a round rim like a circling cup. This unit has no commentary by Xu Bin (the commentary of the next unit refers to "the previous formula treating heart and kidney together").

- The formula is Guizhi tang (Keishito) minus shaoyao plus mahuang, xixin, and fuzi (Mahuang Xixin Fuzi tang), said to move yang qi and disperse water.
- For "the following formula," the other sources read "due to water-fluid retention; Guizhi qu Shaoyao jia Ma Xin Fuzi tang (Bushito) governs it." The heading Gui Jiang Cao Zao Huang Xin Fu tang is an abbreviation of it.
