# Clause 24 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Interior water: Yuebi jia Zhu tang (Eppikajutsuto) governs it; Gancao Mahuang tang (Kanzomaoto; Licorice and Ephedra Decoction) also governs it.

Yuebi jia Zhu tang (see above: add 4 liang of baizhu (byakujutsu) to [Yuebi tang (Eppito)]; also appears under leg qi)

Gancao Mahuang tang

Gancao (kanzo) 2 liang; mahuang (mao) 4 liang.

Of the two ingredients above, with 5 sheng of water first boil the mahuang, remove the froth on top, add the gancao, boil down to 3 sheng, and take 1 sheng warm. Cover heavily to induce sweating; if there is no sweat, take another dose. Be careful of wind and cold.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

Interior water is the earlier generalized edema with "the whole body, face, and eyes yellow and swollen, a deep pulse, and thirst." The explanation of the Yuebi tang formula appears earlier. "Gancao Mahuang tang also governs it" because mahuang releases the yang and gancao harmonizes it; then the yang moves and the water leaves, and any interior heat clears by itself without treatment. It also guards against those of weak constitution who cannot bear shigao (sekko).

## Notes

A clause using Yuebi jia Zhu tang or Gancao Mahuang tang for interior water.

- Yuebi jia Zhu tang is Yuebi tang plus baizhu, also found as an appended formula (Yuebi jia Zhu tang from the *Qianjin fang*) in the chapter on wind attack and joint wind. Gancao Mahuang tang induces sweating with two ingredients, mahuang and gancao.
