# Clause 12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Question: After diarrhea, one is thirsty and drinks water, urination is inhibited, and the abdomen becomes full and then swollen; why is this? Answer: By rule this should become water disease. If urination becomes spontaneously free or there is sweating, it should recover by itself.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

This speaks of a case after diarrhea that can become water but is easily cured. Thirst after diarrhea means sudden loss of fluids. Earth is weak and the qi does not transform, so urination is, on the contrary, inhibited. Further, drinking water freely damages spleen earth, so there is intake without output; the abdomen therefore becomes full, and the qi floats, causing swelling. When water enters and does not exit, fullness is usual, but swelling is puzzling; hence the question. [The questioner] did not know that when the stomach qi is vacuous, water insults earth; earth governs the flesh, and when earth is vacuous and water overflows, there is always swelling. Hence "by rule this should become water disease." But since it comes after diarrhea, it is not comparable to binding of the three yin, so when urination flows and sweat comes out, it recovers by itself at once.

## Notes

A question-and-answer clause stating that when after diarrhea one is thirsty and drinks water, with inhibited urination, abdominal fullness, and swelling, water disease results, but it recovers by itself if urination or sweating occurs.

- The base lacks 水 after 渇飲; it is supplied because all other sources have it (see the emendation note).
