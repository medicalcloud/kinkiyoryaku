# Clause 7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The anterior tibial (Fuyang) pulse should be hidden, but now it is instead rapid; this person originally has heat, disperses grain, and urinates frequently, but now, on the contrary, urination is inhibited; water is about to form.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

These two clauses say that people with water disease may have other old illnesses, differing from person to person, and should be distinguished by the anterior tibial (Fuyang) pulse and the signs of the old illness. When a person has water disease, water and cold contend, and the anterior tibial (Fuyang) pulse should be hidden. Now, having contracted water disease, the anterior tibial (Fuyang) pulse is instead tight, because there was originally cold *shan*, conglomeration, and abdominal pain, adding tightness to the pulse. Treatment should also attend to the cold, but if the physician purges instead, the original qi is damaged, the water disease is not removed, pathogenic cold rises, and the ancestral qi in the chest is too weak to resist it, producing chest fullness and shortness of breath. Or the anterior tibial (Fuyang) pulse should be hidden but is instead rapid: this person originally has heat and should disperse grain and urinate frequently, but now urination is inhibited. This is a person with heat and vigorous movement whose qi fails to transform because of water; so we know the pathogen is bound in the three yin. Hence "this [is about to form water]."

## Notes

A clause stating that when the anterior tibial (Fuyang) pulse is instead rapid in a person who originally has heat, eats much, and urinates often, but now has inhibited urination, it is a sign that water is about to form.
