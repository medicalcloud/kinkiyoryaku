# Clause 15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Lung water: the body is swollen, urination is difficult, and at times there is duck-stool diarrhea.

## Notes

The signs of lung water. Xu Bin's commentary is after unit 17.

- The base reads 脈水 and 時下; these are emended to 肺水 and 時時 because all other sources so read (see the emendation notes).
