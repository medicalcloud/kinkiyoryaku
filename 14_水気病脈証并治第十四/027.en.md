# Clause 27 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Question: Yellow sweating disease: the body is swollen (one version reads "heavy"), there is fever with sweating and thirst, it resembles wind edema, the sweat soaks the clothes and is pure yellow like the juice of a medicinal [other sources: phellodendron], and the pulse is deep of itself. From what is it contracted? The master said: It is contracted by bathing in water while sweating, the water entering through the sweat pores. Qi Shao Gui Jiu tang (Astragalus, Peony, Cinnamon Twig, and Vinegar Decoction) is appropriate and governs it.

Huangqi (ogi) [Shaoyao (shakuyaku)] Gui[zhi] Kujiu tang

Huangqi 5 liang; shaoyao 3 liang; guizhi (keishi) 3 liang.

Boil the three ingredients above in 1 sheng of vinegar (*kujiu*) mixed with 7 sheng of water down to 3 sheng, and take 1 sheng warm. There should be vexation of the heart; after taking it for six or seven days it resolves. If the vexation does not stop, it is because the vinegar is obstructing (one formula uses good vinegar in place of *kujiu*).

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

This section directly states the cause and treatment of yellow sweating. If the body is swollen like skin edema, with fever, sweating, and thirst like wind edema, the pulse should not be deep, yet it is deep of itself; if it were not wind and dampness contending, how could this be? So [the questioner] asked where it was contracted, surmising that it was not merely wind. So Zhongjing answered, "contracted by bathing in water while sweating, the water entering through the sweat pores," meaning that when sweating, the interstices are loose, and the qi of guest water enters through the pores and damages the heart; water and fire steam each other, so the color is yellow, and water qi binds, so the pulse is deep. This pattern also arises from exposure to wind while sweating after drinking alcohol; even without external water, the sweat that has come out is driven back inward by wind, and this too is water. But here only those who bathed in water are spoken of; the principle should be understood by comparison. The medicines huangqi, shaoyao, gui, and wine (vinegar) are used because gui and shaoyao are sacred medicines for driving out wind; with huangqi and wine they run throughout the flesh, and without treating dampness the dampness leaves, since wind can overcome dampness. But when the heart receives qi-supplementing, warming medicines, it is temporarily vexed, and only when the disease leaves does it resolve; hence "there should be vexation of the heart; after six or seven days it resolves." But it does not aggravate the disease; so it says only "because the vinegar is obstructing." Huangqi Shaoyao Guizhi Kujiu tang formula.

## Notes

A question-and-answer clause on the cause and treatment of yellow sweating disease (yellow sweating that stains the clothes), attributed to bathing in water while sweating so that water enters through the sweat pores, and using Huangqi Shaoyao Guizhi Kujiu tang (Qi Shao Gui Jiu tang).

- *Kujiu* is taken to be vinegar. After taking it there is temporary vexation, which resolves in six or seven days.
- For 薬 in 如薬汁, other sources read 柏 (phellodendron) or 蘗.
- The formula heading in the base reads Huangqi Gui Kujiu tang, lacking the character for shaoyao.
