# Clause 26 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Reversal (cold limbs) with skin water: Puhui san governs it (the formula appears above).

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

Note: for skin water there was earlier the main text "its pulse is also floating," the extended statement "no aversion to cold, like generalized impediment," and the text "twitching of the limbs," all leading to the Fangji Fuling tang formula. Here it again says "reversal with skin water." This section follows the meaning of "a sunken pulse belongs to shaoyin": skin water originally belongs to the skin, but with reversal the disease seems rooted in the kidney; so Puhui san is given separately to govern it. Puhui san is the ash of cattail mats with huashi, taking its resolving, disinhibiting, cool, and slippery qualities to discharge the kidney evil; it is said specifically of shaoyin water combined with skin water that cannot bear excessive warming.

Discussion: Skin water is originally wind entering the skin, but this comes after the discussion of shaoyin water because of the reversal. Interior water, not being wind water, is regular water; because wind has entered the interior it is not comparable to stone water nor to wind water, so its name was specially changed to "interior." How clear, like a hanging mirror, is the subtlety of pattern identification in the wording and naming! As for the use of medicines, for pregnancy with water qi, heavy body, difficult urination, shivering aversion to cold, and dizziness on rising, Kuizi Fuling tang is used; it seems usable for regular and stone water too, but it is not made the mainstay, meaning that when the flesh swells and the momentum is like a prairie fire, petty percolating and lubricating cannot accomplish anything. Later worthies' moxibustion at Water Divide, Yuyuliang wan, and Cheniu Bawei wan as aftercare all hit the mark every time and may be said to supplement what earlier people did not reach. But when the water bursts its banks sideways, it is like heaven and earth sinking, and one cannot cling to ordinary principles; so Zihe had Shenyou wan and Daoshui wan, used in the hope of a lucky chance. Always, black lips mean damage to the liver, a flattened supraclavicular fossa damage to the heart, a protruding umbilicus damage to the spleen, a flat back damage to the lung, and flat, full soles damage to the kidney; the five damages are incurable, though occasionally some recover. But how can one rely on these as the mainstay? So although Zhongjing, at critical moments at the bedside, did not necessarily refrain from drastic formulas, in writing his book and giving formulas he generally did not include them. His establishment of methods is rigorous.

## Notes

A clause using Puhui san for skin water accompanied by reversal (cold limbs). The formula is in the chapter on wasting thirst, inhibited urination, and strangury.

- For the note "appears above," most other sources read "appears under wasting thirst."
- Xu Bin's "Discussion" addresses the treatment of water disease in general, mentioning later moxibustion, Yuyuliang wan, and the drastic purgatives of Zhang Congzheng (Zihe).
