# Clause 11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Appended formulas

Sanwu Huangqin tang from the *Qianjin*: treats a woman on the straw mat [of childbirth] who has uncovered herself and caught wind, with the four limbs suffering vexing heat. If there is headache, give Xiao Chaihu tang; if there is no headache but only vexation, this decoction governs it.

Huangqin 1 liang; kushen 2 liang; gandihuang 4 liang.

Of the three ingredients above, with 8 sheng of water boil down to 2 sheng and take 1 sheng warm. Often worms come out through vomiting or purging.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

This says that a parturient woman may briefly contract slight wind — either in the half-exterior half-interior, or in the lower burner where wind and dampness combine, or generating worms — and in all cases signs of vexing heat in the limbs can appear, distinguished only by whether there is headache. "On the straw mat" means she has not yet left the place of delivery. "Uncovered herself and caught wind" means she lifted her clothes and bedding and, being a little careless, briefly contracted it. After childbirth yin is vacuous; after blood loss yang qi alone is exuberant in the limbs, and contracting slight wind brings suffering from vexing heat. But when the exterior predominates, it enters above and causes headache, so the upper burner should be considered primary, and Xiao Chaihu tang to harmonize and resolve is the mainstay. If it is received from below and damp-heat binds below, worms will surely be generated, with no headache. So huangqin, clearing heat, is the sovereign; kushen, dispelling wind and killing worms, is the minister; and dihuang, supplementing the original yin, is the assistant. "Often worms come out through vomiting or purging" means that worms receiving kushen will surely be restless, and whether they come out above or below cannot be known.

## Notes

An appended formula of this chapter. For vexing heat of the limbs after catching wind during the lying-in period, Xiao Chaihu tang is used if there is headache, and Sanwu Huangqin tang if there is no headache but only vexation.

- Daizhige and Xu Bin's text give the water as 6 sheng.
