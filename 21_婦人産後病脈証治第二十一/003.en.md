# Clause 3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Pain in the abdomen after childbirth: Danggui Shengjiang Yangrou tang (Tokishokyoyonikuto) governs it. It also treats cold mounting in the abdomen and deficiency/consumption insufficiency.

Danggui Shengjiang Yangrou tang (Angelica, Fresh Ginger, and Mutton Decoction) formula (appears under cold [mounting])

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

㽲痛 means slow, gentle pain. It generally belongs to obstruction by visiting cold. So danggui frees stagnation in the blood aspect and shengjiang (shokyo) moves cold in the qi aspect. But before birth one blames excess, so in Danggui Shaoyao san (Tokishakuyakusan) fuling (bukuryo) and zexie (takusha) are added to drain water-damp; after birth one generally blames deficiency, so mutton is made sovereign — what is called "insufficiency of form is supplemented with flavor." Mutton supplements qi, and 㽲痛 belongs to weakness of qi, so it is suitable. This formula applies attack and supplementation together, so it also treats cold mounting and deficiency detriment.

## Notes

A clause using Danggui Shengjiang Yangrou tang for postpartum abdominal pain.

- The formula is in the chapter on abdominal fullness, cold mounting, and abiding food. For its location, "寒病中" is "寒疝中" in all other sources.
