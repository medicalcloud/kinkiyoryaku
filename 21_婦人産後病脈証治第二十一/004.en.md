# Clause 4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Abdominal pain after childbirth, with vexation and fullness and inability to lie down: Zhishi Shaoyao san (Kijitsushakuyakusan) governs it.

Zhishi Shaoyao san (Unripe Bitter Orange and Peony Powder) formula

Zhishi (kijitsu; charred black, not overdone) and shaoyao (shakuyaku), equal amounts.

Pound the two ingredients above into powder; [take] a square-inch spoonful three times a day. It also governs abscess pus. Wash it down with wheat gruel.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

Pain is generally due to obstruction of qi. Abdominal pain is spleen deficiency and weak qi causing obstruction. When the spleen is deficient and the healthy qi is not contained, there is fullness; when qi is obstructed and congested with fire above, there is vexation; when congestion is extreme and Yangming counterflows and cannot follow its path, one cannot lie down. So zhishi frees the qi — what is called "when there is free flow, there is no pain." Shaoyao supplements the spleen and contracts qi to dissolve the fullness. When qi flows smoothly and there is no pain, there is no vexation and one can lie down. Freeing qi and contracting blood regulate qi and blood by themselves, so it also governs abscess pus. Washing it down with wheat gruel harmonizes liver qi and nourishes the heart and spleen.

[Xu Bin's instructions:] Pound the two ingredients above into powder and take a square-inch spoonful three times a day. It also governs abscess pus. With wheat gruel [wash it down].

## Notes

A clause using Zhishi Shaoyao san for postpartum abdominal pain with vexation, fullness, and inability to lie down.
