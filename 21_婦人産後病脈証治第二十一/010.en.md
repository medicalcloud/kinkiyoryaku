# Clause 10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Postpartum diarrhea with extreme deficiency: Baitouweng jia Gancao Ejiao tang governs it.

Baitouweng jia Gancao Ejiao tang (Pulsatilla Decoction plus Licorice and Ass-Hide Glue) formula

Baitouweng, gancao, and ejiao, 2 liang each; qinpi, huanglian, and bopi (phellodendron bark), 3 liang each.

Of the six ingredients above, with 7 sheng of water boil down to 2.5 sheng, add the glue and let it dissolve completely, and take warm in three doses.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

Zhongjing takes Baitouweng tang to treat heat diarrhea with heaviness below. Baitouweng, purely bitter, can firm the kidney, so it is the sovereign medicine for driving out bound qi of wind-heat in the lower jiao. As ministers, huanglian clears heart fire, qinpi clears liver heat, and bopi clears kidney heat. All four are bitter and cold, so they suit heat diarrhea with heaviness below. In postpartum diarrhea the damp-heat should be the same as in [other] people, so Baitouweng tang is appropriate. If deficiency is extreme, supplementation cannot be omitted, but other ingredients such as renshen and baizhu are not suitable, for their congesting and drying are disliked; nor can it be treated with the bland percolation of fuling and zexie, for fear of damaging fluids. Only gancao's sweet coolness, clearing the center, is the way to supplement the center, and ejiao's enriching moisture, dispelling wind, is the way to harmonize the blood. Treating the disease with these is itself making a great supplementing formula. One should know that in treating dysentery, damp-heat cannot be removed without bitter cold, so gathering four bitter cold ingredients is not excessive; and for harmonizing the blood and calming the center, gancao and ejiao alone are more than enough. Those who like to use renshen and baizhu in treating dysentery simply have not fully understood this principle.

[Part of Xu Bin's formula:] baitouweng 2 liang; gancao 2 liang; ejiao 2 liang; qinpi 3 liang; huanglian 2 liang; bopi 3 liang.

## Notes

A clause using Baitouweng jia Gancao Ejiao tang for postpartum diarrhea with extreme deficiency.

- It is Baitouweng tang (chapter 17, unit 42) plus gancao and ejiao, treating heat diarrhea with bitter cold while supplementing the center and harmonizing the blood with gancao and ejiao.
