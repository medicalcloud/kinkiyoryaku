# Clause 9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When a woman is nursing, with vacuity of the center, vexation and agitation, and retching counterflow: calm the center and boost qi; Zhupi Da wan governs it.

Zhupi Da wan (Major Bamboo Shavings Pill) formula

Fresh zhuru 2 *fen*; shigao 2 *fen*; guizhi 1 *fen*; gancao 7 *fen*; baiwei (cynanchum) 1 *fen*.

Powder the five ingredients above and make pills with jujube flesh, the size of a pellet. Take one pill with a drink, three times in the day and twice at night. For heat, double the baiwei; for vexation and panting, add 1 *fen* of baishi (arborvitae seed).

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

乳 means a woman nursing a child. Her liver qi is originally insufficient. Vacuity of the center means the center qi is greatly vacuous. When spleen earth is further distressed and weak, fire congesting above causes vexation and qi surging upward causes retching. Vexation with agitation is extreme vexation; retching with counterflow is extreme retching. The disease originally stems entirely from vacuity of the center, yet the medicines are only zhuru, guizhi, gancao, shigao, and baiwei, because when vacuity of the center reaches retching and vexation, the gallbladder fu has received evil, and vexation and retching are the principal disease; so zhuru, which removes vexation and stops retching, is the sovereign. The yang qi in the chest is not working, so guizhi and gancao, supporting yang and transforming the counterflow qi, are the ministers. Shigao, cooling vacuity heat in the qi aspect of the upper burner, is the assistant, and baiwei, removing floating heat in the exterior, is the courier. One should know that with vexation, agitation, and retching counterflow but no abdominal pain, diarrhea, or the like, though there is vacuity there is no cold to suspect. The subtlety lies in adding guizhi to a cooling prescription, and especially in raw gancao alone being plentiful: dispersing accumulated evil and restoring the qi of clear yang, the center calms by itself and the qi is boosted by itself. So, without a single supplementing medicinal, it nonetheless notes the original intent of the formula: "calm the center and boost qi: Zhupi Da wan." Marvelous!

For panting, baishi is added. The arborvitae always faces west and receives the qi of the west most deeply, so it can boost metal, moisten liver wood, and quiet the heart; then the lung is not scorched and the panting calms by itself. It is like what the ancients called a medicine for the qi aspect of the liver: arborvitae is yin, can boost liver yin and gather its overflowing qi, and has great merit in moistening the liver. For heat, baiwei is doubled, because baiwei can remove floating heat. The *Xiaopin* says of Guizhi jia Longgu Muli tang, "For much sweating with floating heat, remove guizhi and add baiwei and fuzi, 3 *fen* each; it is called Erjia Longgu tang"; from this one knows that baiwei can remove floating heat.

[Part of Xu Bin's formula:] baiwei 1 *fen*.

... twice. For heat, double the baiwei; for vexation and panting, add 1 *fen* of baishi.

## Notes

A clause using Zhupi Da wan for a nursing woman with vacuous center qi, vexation, agitation, and retching counterflow.

- Zhuru removes vexation and stops retching, shigao and baiwei clear heat, and guizhi and gancao support yang. Xu Bin emphasizes the large amount of gancao and the addition of guizhi to a cooling prescription.
