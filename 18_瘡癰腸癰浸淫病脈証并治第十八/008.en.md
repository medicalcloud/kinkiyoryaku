# Clause 8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Spreading sores: Huanglian fen (Coptis Powder) governs it (the formula has not been seen).

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

Spreading sores are sores that keep spreading. Though they belong to diseases of the flesh, they actually move along with the zang-fu. So earlier Zhongjing cited them as an analogy for what is curable or not, entering the fu from the zang or the zang from the fu. Here Huanglian fen governs it: this is originally heat-toxin evil qi deepening gradually from the outside, so huanglian, clearing the evil heat, is the mainstay. The original formula was lost, so it is not recorded. But by my own estimation, it is nothing more than huanglian alone. Hence it says:

## Notes

A clause stating that spreading sores are treated with Huanglian fen. The formula has not survived.
