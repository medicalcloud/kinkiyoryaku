# Clause 7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yellow-water sore: those that flow from the mouth toward the four limbs can be treated; those that flow from the four limbs and enter [base: 人] the mouth cannot be treated.

## Notes

A clause stating that yellow-water sore (oozing skin lesions that spread) are curable when they spread from the mouth to the limbs and hard to cure when they spread from the limbs to the mouth. Nearly the same clause appears in chapter 1 (unit 12).

- This paragraph is missing from the base because of a lost leaf and has been supplied from Wikisource (*Jingui yaolüe fanglun*). For 人 in 人口, most other sources read 入 ("enter"), which the translation follows.
- Xu Bin's commentary is attached together after the next unit.
