# Clause 1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In all cases of a floating and rapid pulse, there should be fever; if instead there is shivering aversion to cold, and there is a painful spot, one should effuse the abscess.

## Notes

A clause stating that when the pulse is floating and rapid and there should be fever but instead there is aversion to cold with a painful spot, it is because an abscess (a suppurating swelling) is forming.

- "当発其癰" is usually read "an abscess will develop," but Xu Bin takes 発 as the treatment of effusing and dispersing the bound qi.
- Xu Bin's commentary is attached together after the next unit.
