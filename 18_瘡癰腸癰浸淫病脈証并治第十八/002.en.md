# Clause 2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The master said: For all abscesses and swellings, to know whether there is pus or not, cover the top of the swelling with the hand; if it is hot, there is pus; if it is not hot, there is no pus.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

[On the previous unit:] When sores and abscesses arise, at first they resemble external contraction. But examining the pattern, it is the opposite of an exterior pulse. Floating and rapid is originally a pulse of wind-heat, and with wind-heat there should be fever; if instead there is shivering aversion to cold with a painful spot, clearly there is congested and bound toxin inside, and the wei-defensive qi, assailed by internal heat, does not move to the exterior, so outside there is instead shivering aversion to cold. Naturally one should effuse and disperse the bound qi, and then the abscess opens by itself.

If there is already an abscess or swelling, when it is not hot the pus has not yet formed; when hot, toxin has gathered. So cover the swollen spot with the hand: hot means pus, not hot means no pus. No formula is given because 癰 (abscess) means 壅 (congestion); freeing the congestion brings recovery, so the single word 発 ("effuse") says it all.

## Notes

A clause stating that whether pus has formed in an abscess is distinguished by placing the hand on it and feeling whether it is hot.

- The first part of Xu Bin's commentary concerns the previous unit.
