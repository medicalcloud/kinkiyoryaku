# Clause 5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Lily bulb disease that has not undergone vomiting, purging, or sweating, with the disease form as at the beginning: Baihe Dihuang tang (Lily Bulb and Rehmannia Decoction) governs it.

Baihe Dihuang tang formula

Baihe 7 pieces (split); juice of fresh dihuang (rehmannia) 1 sheng.

Of the above, wash the baihe with water and soak it overnight; white froth should come out. Discard that water, then decoct with 2 sheng of spring water down to 1 sheng, remove the dregs, add the dihuang juice, decoct down to 1.5 sheng, and take warm in two doses. When it hits the disease, do not take more. The stool will always be like lacquer.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

If it has not undergone vomiting, purging, or sweating, there is no concern about damage to yin or yang. But the "beginning" in "the disease form as at the beginning" is what the *Shanghan lun* calls Taiyang disease. Remaining as at the beginning without resolving means the distress of the yang meridians has reached its extreme and the yin qi too has been exhausted. The heart is the master of the five zang organs, so fresh dihuang, which cools the blood and supplements the heart, is used with baihe and spring water to nourish the yin and transform the long-standing pathogen in the yang meridians.

## Notes

The clause of Baihe Dihuang tang, the proper formula for lily bulb disease that has not been mistreated; it is regarded as the representative formula for lily bulb disease.

- Baihe moistens the lung, and fresh dihuang juice clears heat and cools the blood.
- The stool turning black like lacquer after taking it is due to the color of dihuang and is no cause for concern. For 常 ("always") in 大便常如漆, most other sources read 当 ("will").
