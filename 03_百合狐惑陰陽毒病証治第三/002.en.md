# Clause 2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Lily bulb disease after sweating has been induced: Baihe Zhimu tang (Lily Bulb and Anemarrhena Decoction) governs it.

Baihe Zhimu tang formula

Baihe (lily bulb) 7 pieces (split); zhimu 3 liang (sliced).

Of the above, first wash the baihe with water and soak it overnight; white froth should come out. Discard that water, then decoct with 2 sheng of spring water down to 1 sheng and remove the dregs. Separately, decoct the zhimu with 2 sheng of spring water down to 1 sheng and remove the dregs. Afterward combine them, decoct down to 1.5 sheng, and take warm in two doses.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

[Xu Bin's formula:] baihe 7 pieces; zhimu 3 liang.

... decoct with 2 sheng of water down to 1 sheng; separately decoct the huashi with 2 sheng of spring water, remove the dregs, and take 1 sheng; afterward combine them, decoct again, and take warm in divided doses.

## Notes

A clause using Baihe Zhimu tang after lily bulb disease has been mistakenly treated by sweating.

- Sweating has damaged the fluids and heat remains; baihe moistens the lung and zhimu clears heat.
- The base writes 知毋 in the formula heading and the formula, an error for 知母 (the clause itself reads 知母, as do the other sources). The translation uses zhimu (知母).
- Xu Bin's commentary for this unit consists only of part of his formula; its preparation instructions mention huashi (talc) and seem mixed with the following Huashi Daizhe tang formula. Xu Bin's explanation of Baihe Zhimu tang is included in the commentary of the next unit.
