# Clause 12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yang toxin disease: the face is red, mottled like the pattern of brocade, the throat is sore, and pus and blood are spat. At five days it can be treated; at seven days it cannot. Shengma Biejia tang (Shomabekkoto; Cimicifuga and Turtle Shell Decoction) governs it.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

The *Neijing* says, "Those damaged by cold all develop heat disease." But when pathogen is in the yang meridians for a long time and becomes blazing, it becomes toxin. Hence there is yang toxin disease. In this disease, heat soaks into the ying-nutrient and wei-defense, binds in the stomach, and rises to the throat; it is entirely yang heat, so it blazes in the upper jiao, and the yin of the liver and spleen does not interact. The face is where the qi of Yangming pours, so with exuberant fire-heat the face is red and mottled like brocade. The pharynx and larynx are distinguished as yin and yang, but where a great fire strikes, jade and stone are not distinguished, so pharynx and larynx both hurt. The heat of the yang meridians is exuberant and heart fire joins it; the heart governs the blood, so [the blood] transforms into pus. The disease is in the upper jiao, so it is spat out.

Although yang toxin disease is severe, it is not comparable to the transmission through the meridians in cold damage. But the meridians of the body circulate in sequence; at five days the meridian qi has not yet completed a cycle, so it can be treated; at seven days the yin and yang meridian qi has already completed a cycle and moves again, so it cannot be treated. The medicine used is Shengma Biejia tang. Here heat contends with qi and blood and cannot be broken directly, so shengma (cimicifuga) with raw gancao (kanzo), which raise and disperse heat toxin, is the sovereign; xionghuang (yuo), which resolves toxin, is the minister; biejia (bekko; turtle shell) and danggui, which regulate the liver yin, are the assistants; and shujiao (shokusho) (Sichuan pepper), which conducts the heat qi, is the envoy. Biejia is not used because the yang toxin has, on the contrary, arisen in the yin meridians. Rather, the method of treating disease is that when disease is in the yang, one must also harmonize the yin — the military method of "attacking Wei to rescue Zhao." This too is what is called "when disease appears in the yang, rescue it by the yin method." But it is not supplementation.

## Notes

A clause describing the signs (red mottling of the face, sore throat, spitting pus and blood) and prognosis of yang toxin, using Shengma Biejia tang. The formula is placed after the next unit on yin toxin.

- What yang toxin and yin toxin correspond to has long been debated; suggestions include infectious diseases with rash (such as scarlet fever) and collagen diseases (systemic lupus erythematosus).
- That it can be treated at five days but not at seven shows how quickly the disease progresses.
- 班班 is the same as 斑斑 ("mottled"), which the other sources read.
