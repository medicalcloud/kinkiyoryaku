# Clause 7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Lily disease in which the thirst does not recover: the following formula governs it.

Gualou Muli san (Trichosanthes Root and Oyster Shell Powder) formula

Gualougen; muli (oyster shell) (dry-fried), equal amounts.

Grind the above into a fine powder and take a square-inch spoonful with a drink, three times a day.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

That the thirst does not recover means washing with the baihe decoction has not helped. Clearly the yin qi within has not recovered, and the yin qi has not recovered because yang is hyperactive. So gualougen clears heat in the chest and muli clears heat in the lower burner. It is the same method as the above, calming yang to rescue yin, except that here it is treated from within. Therefore baihe is not used and it is made into a powder.

## Notes

A clause using Gualou Muli san when thirst does not recover even after the Baihe washing formula.

- Gualougen generates fluids to stop thirst, and muli draws down floating yang (vacuity heat).
- A square-inch spoon (*fangcunbi*) is a measure using a spoon one inch square.
- For "the following formula governs it," the other sources give the formula name (Gualou Muli san, etc.).
