# Clause 4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Lily disease after vomiting has been induced: the following formula governs it.

Baihe Jizi tang (Lily Bulb and Egg Yolk Decoction) formula

Baihe 7 pieces (split); jizihuang (egg yolk) 1.

Of the above, first wash the baihe with water and soak it overnight; white froth should come out. Discard that water, then decoct with 2 sheng of spring water down to 1 sheng, remove the dregs, add the egg yolk, stir evenly, decoct to five-tenths (*fen*), and take warm.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

Vomiting damages the original qi, and the yin essence no longer rises to support [the upper body]. So in lily disease after vomiting, one must use egg yolk, which nourishes yin, with spring water to enrich the original yin, cooperating with baihe to move the lung qi. Then qi and blood are regulated and yin and yang become balanced by themselves.

## Notes

A clause using Baihe Jizi tang after lily disease has been mistakenly treated by vomiting.

- Egg yolk nourishes yin-blood where vomiting has damaged the stomach yin.
- For "the following formula governs it," icntcm and Xu Bin's edition read "Baihe Jizi tang governs it."
- Some suggest that 煎五分 ("decoct to five *fen*") means "five *ge*," judging from the other formulas' wording, but this is uncertain. The translation follows the characters.
