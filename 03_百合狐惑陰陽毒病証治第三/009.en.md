# Clause 9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In lily bulb disease, when it appears in the yin, rescue it by the yang method; when it appears in the yang, rescue it by the yin method. To see yang and attack the yin, and then again induce sweating, is a counterflow (mistreatment). To see yin and attack the yang, and then again purge, is also a counterflow.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

This section sums up the whole chapter. Lily bulb disease is in every case the internal qi combined with residual pathogen of cold damage, lingering without end; the concern is not that it will grow but that it will drag on. So disease after purging, and disease that turns into thirst with unceasing thirst, are what is called "appearing in the yin." It will inevitably extend to the yang, and when the yang too is ill, nothing can be done. So huashi thoroughly opens the blocked pores, baihe disinhibits the yang of the skin and hair, and when the yang within is dry, gualou and muli nourish the yang within the abdomen. When the yang is balanced, even if the yin pathogen tries to pass to it, it does not receive it, and the pathogen in the yin meridians gradually disappears. This is what is called "rescuing it by the yang method."

Disease after sweating or after purging, disease whose form is as at the beginning, and disease that turns into fever are all what is called "appearing in the yang." It will inevitably extend to the yin, and when the yin too is ill, nothing can be done. So zhimu [nourishes] the yin of (lacuna), egg yolk nourishes the yin of the blood aspect, fresh dihuang strengthens its (lacuna); for what arises in the flesh and exterior, huashi harmonizes the yin of the intestines and stomach (lacuna); even if the pathogen tries to pass to it, it does not receive it, and the pathogen within the yang gradually disappears. This is what is called "rescuing it by the yin method."

But this is rescuing, not attacking. If the methods of sweating and purging were used, that would be attacking. So if one sees yang and attacks the yin, the yin becomes deficient and the yang is about to invade it; how can this be called rescuing? Yet the yang's tendency to invade at once is not because the yang is strong. Hence "then again inducing sweating is a counterflow": the first error lies in attacking the yin, and this further error lies in treating the yang. If one sees yin and attacks the yang, the yang becomes deficient and the yin is about to invade it; how can this be called rescuing? Yet the yin's tendency to invade at once is not because the yin is strong. Hence "then again purging is also a counterflow": the first error lies in attacking the yang, and this further error lies in treating the yin.

Discussion: The yang method and the yin method are the methods of harmonizing yin and harmonizing yang. Rescuing with them is the idea of harmonizing what is not yet ill — what the *Neijing* calls "use yin to harmonize yang, use yang to harmonize yin." So all the methods of treatment use baihe to supplement the lung and make the qi flow to the bowels — what is called "the qi returns to the balance, and by the balance it is leveled." All use spring water to clear pathogenic heat and let the lung metal receive and complete it — what is called "the blazing and steaming obtain clarity and the myriad things are accommodated and leveled." Only, when the disease appears in the yang, one or two ingredients are added to harmonize the yin; when it appears in the yin, one or two ingredients are added to harmonize the yang.

Someone asks: Huashi also belongs to yin medicinals; why is it taken as a medicinal that harmonizes yang? Answer: Qi belongs to yang; when the orifices are open the yang flows, and when urination is free the qi transforms. Huashi is white in color and bland in flavor, a yang medicinal within yin. When it frees the orifices and opens urination, the qi becomes unimpeded, and when the qi is unimpeded the yang harmonizes by itself.

Someone asks: Then since huashi was used to harmonize yang, why, when [the disease] later turns into fever, is it used again to harmonize yin? Answer: When lily bulb disease reaches fever, the yin disease has not stopped and the yang becomes ill along with it, unlike a disease of the yang alone. So since the outside is already hot, one first calms the inside, using huashi, which is cool, cold, moistening, and descending, as the mainstay. But one does not dare to use it together with spring water and so greatly damage the yin. Then the inner yin harmonizes by itself and the outer yang does not resist. This too is what is called rescuing it by the yin method. When thirst does not recover, it is lily bulb disease turned into thirst; if harmonizing the yang of the skin and hair has brought no response, the yang within the yin must be dry. Huafen (trichosanthes root) and muli are both light in flavor and white in color, yang medicinals within yin; they drive back the yin fire and restore the original yang, so they too can harmonize yang.

## Notes

A clause summarizing the principles of treating lily bulb disease.

- When the disease appears in the yin (yin aspect, interior), rescue by the yang method; when in the yang, by the yin method — that is, the principle is to harmonize yin and yang; aggressive treatments such as sweating or purging are counterflow (mistreatment).
- Interpretations of "see yang and attack the yin" and "see yin and attack the yang" vary; Xu Bin takes them as double errors, such as mistakenly attacking (purging) the yin despite seeing yang signs and then also inducing sweating.
- Xu Bin applies the formulas of the preceding clauses to the yang and yin methods. The lacunae (闕) are in the Siku quanshu edition.
