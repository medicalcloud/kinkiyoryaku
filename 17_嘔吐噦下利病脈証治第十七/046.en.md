# Clause 46 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qi diarrhea: Helile san governs it.

Helile san (Chebule Powder) formula

Helile (chebule) 10 pieces.

Make the one ingredient above into a powder, mix with rice-gruel liquid, and take in a single dose (probably not Zhongjing's formula).

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

Earlier it was already said, "Diarrhea with [passing of] gas: one should disinhibit the urine." "Qi diarrhea" here seems to be that diarrhea with gas, yet it is governed by helile. Qi diarrhea arises from congested qi, and congested qi arises from collected drool. Helile can open drool, and being astringent by nature it can also secure qi; so it governs this. [He]lile san formula.

Make the one ingredient above into a powder, mix with rice-gruel liquid, and take in a single dose.

## Notes

A clause using Helile san for qi diarrhea.

- Xu Bin regards it as the same as the "diarrhea with gas" of unit 30. Helile is an astringent medicinal that stops diarrhea.
- Most other sources add "roasted in ashes" after "helile 10 pieces." A note after the formula says "probably not Zhongjing's formula."
