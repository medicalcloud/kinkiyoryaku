# Clause 41 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Diarrhea with pus and blood in the stool: Taohua tang governs it.

Taohua tang (Peach Blossom Decoction)

Chishizhi (halloysite) 1 sheng (half crushed, half sifted into powder); ganjiang 1 liang; jingmi (non-glutinous rice) 1 sheng.

Of the three ingredients above, with 7 sheng of water boil the rice until cooked, remove the dregs, warm 7 ge [most other sources: "take 7 ge warm"], add a square-inch spoonful of chishizhi powder, and take three times a day. If one dose heals it, do not take the rest.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

Diarrhea with pus and blood in the stool arises from depressed cold turning into damp-heat, which therefore stirs the blood. But diarrhea reaching the blood means it first damaged the center qi and later the blood aspect. So ganjiang disperses the root cold and plunders the branch heat, combined with jingmi to regulate the center, and the sweet-sour, warm, astringent chishizhi enters the blood aspect to gather dampness and secure desertion. The *Bencao* says it can nourish heart blood; this too takes its entering the blood aspect to regulate it.

## Notes

A clause using Taohua tang for diarrhea with pus and blood. The Shaoyin chapter of the *Shanghan lun* (corresponding to clause 306) has "Shaoyin disease with diarrhea and pus and blood in the stool: Taohua tang governs it."

- A formula that astringes and secures the intestines with chishizhi, warms with ganjiang, and protects the center with jingmi. Characteristically, half the chishizhi is powdered and added at the time of taking.
- Three other sources give chishizhi as 1 jin.
