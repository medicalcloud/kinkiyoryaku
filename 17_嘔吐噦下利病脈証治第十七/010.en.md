# Clause 10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Retching with rumbling intestines and stuffiness below the heart: use the following formula.

Banxia Xiexin tang (Hangeshashinto; Pinellia Heart-Draining Decoction)

Banxia (hange) half a sheng (washed); huangqin (ogon), ganjiang (kankyo), and renshen (ninjin), 3 liang each; huanglian (oren) 1 liang; dazao (taiso) 12 pieces; gancao (kanzo) 3 liang (mix-fried).

Of the seven ingredients above, with 1 dou of water boil down to 6 sheng, remove the dregs, boil again down to 3 sheng, and take 1 sheng warm, three times a day.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

Retching originally belongs to heat. But rumbling intestines mean cold below, and deficient stuffiness means yin pathogen binds with retained fluid below the heart — what the *Shanghan lun* calls "the stomach's mouth not harmonious, thunderous rumbling in the abdomen." So Banxia Xiexin tang governs it: renshen, gancao, and dazao supplement the center, ganjiang warms the stomach and discharges fullness, banxia opens phlegm-fluid retention, and huangqin and huanglian dissolve stuffiness; their bitter cold can also discharge fullness. I personally saw a wet nurse who had vomited for five days and whom a hundred medicines could not stop; afterward she took only ganjiang and huanglian and it stopped at once. This is the meaning of this formula.

[Part of Xu Bin's formula:] banxia half a jin (washed); huangqin 3 liang; ganjiang 3 liang; renshen 3 liang.

[Part of the next, Huangqin jia Banxia Shengjiang tang (Ogonkahangeshokyoto):] banxia half a jin; shengjiang (shokyo) 3 liang.

## Notes

A clause using Banxia Xiexin tang for retching, intestinal rumbling, and stuffiness below the heart. It is the same as Banxia Xiexin tang in the lower Taiyang chapter of the *Shanghan lun*.

- Mixed cold and heat causing stuffiness in the middle jiao is opened with acrid (banxia, ganjiang) and bitter (huangqin, huanglian) medicinals, with renshen, gancao, and dazao supplementing.
- For "use the following formula," the other sources read "Banxia Xiexin tang governs it."
