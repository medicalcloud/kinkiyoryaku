# Clause 8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Retching with fullness of the chest: use the following formula.

Zhuyu tang (Evodia Decoction)

Wuzhuyu 1 sheng; renshen 3 liang; shengjiang 6 liang; dazao 12 pieces.

Of the four ingredients above, with 5 sheng of water boil down to 3 sheng and take 7 ge warm, three times a day.

## Notes

A clause using Wuzhuyu tang for retching with chest fullness. Xu Bin's commentary is attached after the next unit.

- Wuzhuyu tang warms cold and brings down turbid yin with wuzhuyu, supplements with renshen and dazao, and stops retching with shengjiang. It also appears in the yangming, shaoyin, and jueyin chapters of the *Shanghan lun*.
- For "use the following formula," the other sources read "Zhuyu tang governs it."
