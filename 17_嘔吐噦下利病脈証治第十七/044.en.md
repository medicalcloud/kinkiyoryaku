# Clause 44 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Diarrhea with clear grain, cold inside and heat outside, sweating and reverse-flow: Tongmai Sini tang governs it.

Tongmai Sini tang (Vessel-Freeing Counterflow Cold Decoction) formula

Fuzi, one large piece (used raw); ganjiang 3 liang (4 liang for a strong person); gancao 2 liang (mix-fried).

Of the three ingredients above, with 3 sheng of water boil down to 1 sheng 1 ge [Daizhige and Xu Bin's text: 1 sheng 2 ge], remove the dregs, and take warm in two doses.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

Stool and water come out mixed, and the color is not very yellow; this is the so-called diarrhea with clear grain, visiting cold entering the interior so that the intestines and stomach are not regulated. But there are cases where the original qi is still strong, the healthy qi grows fuller daily, the pathogenic qi drains by itself, and no cold signs appear at all. If there is cold inside and heat outside, with sweating outside and reverse-flow inside, yin cold is blocking yang outside. Originally one should treat the interior first, but yin and yang are not harmonized, and outside and inside are hostile like Wu and Yue, so the disease qi pull against each other and it is hard to heal. So freeing the vessels is the mainstay, and it is called "Tongmai Sini": it is the ganjiang, fuzi, and gancao of Sini tang, except that ganjiang is increased by half, and in the *Shanghan lun* modifications are further provided. For a red face add 9 stalks of scallion; for abdominal pain remove the scallion and add shaoyao; for retching add shengjiang; for sore throat remove the shaoyao and add jiegeng; if the diarrhea stops but the pulse does not emerge, remove the jiegeng and add renshen. Though these are not all recorded here, one must know them. The meaning of the two words "free the vessels," together with the modifications, is not limited to warming the interior.

## Notes

A clause using Tongmai Sini tang for diarrhea with clear grain, cold inside and heat outside, sweating, and cold limbs. The same clause appears in the Jueyin chapter of the *Shanghan lun* (corresponding to clause 370).

- Tongmai Sini tang has the same three ingredients as Sini tang, with a large fuzi and double the ganjiang. It is used when exuberant yin cold drives yang outward (cold inside, heat outside).
- For "4 liang for a strong person," all other sources read "a strong person may use 4 liang."
