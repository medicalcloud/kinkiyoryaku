# Clause 16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Stomach reflux with retching and vomiting: Da Banxia tang (Daihangeto) governs it (the *Qianjin* says: "it treats stomach reflux with inability to keep food down, vomiting much as soon as food enters." The *Waitai* says: "it treats retching with stuffiness and hardness below the heart").

Da Banxia tang (Major Pinellia Decoction) formula

Banxia (hange) 2 sheng (washed, used whole); renshen (ninjin) 3 liang; white honey 1 sheng.

Of the three ingredients above, mix the honey into 1 dou 2 sheng of water and ladle it up 240 times; boil down to 2.5 sheng, take 1 sheng warm, and take the rest in two doses.

Vomiting as soon as one has finished eating: use the following formula (the *Waitai* says it treats vomiting of water).

Dahuang Gancao tang (Daiokanzoto; Rhubarb and Licorice Decoction) formula

Dahuang (daio; rhubarb) 4 liang; gancao (kanzo) 1 liang.

Of the two ingredients above, with 3 sheng of water boil down to 1 sheng and take warm in two doses.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

Everything before this discussed retching; even where vomiting was mentioned too, it was only a little brought up after eating and drinking. If food is entirely brought up long after eating, this is stomach deficiency unable to digest grain, which therefore counterflows upward, causing stomach reflux. After reflux, fire counterflows with retching and vomiting, combined with dryness. So banxia, bringing down counterflow and phlegm-drool, is the mainstay; renshen is added to nourish the healthy qi, and white honey moistens the dryness; moreover the water is ladled up 240 times to make it go down quickly. The *Qianjin* treats "inability to keep food down," and the *Waitai* treats "retching with stuffiness and hardness below the heart." One must understand that inability to keep food down is deficiency, and stuffiness and hardness are also deficiency.

## Notes

A clause using Da Banxia tang for vomiting due to stomach reflux (bringing up food some time after eating), and Dahuang Gancao tang for vomiting immediately after eating.

- Da Banxia tang brings down counterflow with banxia, supplements with renshen, and moistens with white honey. The method of mixing honey into the water and ladling it up repeatedly is characteristic.
- Dahuang Gancao tang is said to be for heat in the stomach, where food is rejected and vomited at once. Xu Bin has no commentary on Dahuang Gancao tang.
- 巳 in "食巳" is read as 已 ("finished").
- For "use the following formula," the other sources read "Dahuang Gancao tang governs it."
