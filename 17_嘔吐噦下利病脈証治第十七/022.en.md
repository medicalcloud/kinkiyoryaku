# Clause 22 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Hiccup counterflow: Jupi Zhuru tang governs it.

Jupi Zhuru tang (Tangerine Peel and Bamboo Shavings Decoction)

Jupi 2 sheng; zhuru (bamboo shavings) 2 sheng; dazao 30 pieces; shengjiang half a jin; gancao 5 liang; renshen 1 liang.

Of the six ingredients above, with 1 dou of water boil down to 3 sheng and take 1 sheng warm, three times a day.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

This does not mention retching together with it, so it is hiccup purely from stomach deficiency with surging counterflow. But it is not comparable to decline and collapse of the true origin. So renshen and gancao cultivate the original qi in the stomach, and jupi and zhuru, one cold and one warm, bring down the upward counterflowing qi. It also arises from the yang qi of the upper jiao being insufficient to resist it, so the hiccup does not stop; therefore dazao and shengjiang diffuse the upper jiao, letting the yang in the chest gradually flow freely and reach downward. That is, the upper jiao certainly receives qi from the middle jiao, but the middle jiao also receives from the upper jiao; once the upper jiao is diffused, the middle qi regulates itself.

## Notes

A clause using Jupi Zhuru tang for hiccup counterflow.

- For hiccup due to stomach deficiency, renshen and gancao supplement, jupi and zhuru bring down counterflow qi, and shengjiang and dazao diffuse the upper jiao.
- For the amount of jupi, Daizhige reads "2 jin" and Xu Bin's text "2 jin" (written 觔).
