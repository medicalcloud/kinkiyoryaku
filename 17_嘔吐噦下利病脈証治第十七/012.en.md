# Clause 12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

All kinds of retching and vomiting in which grain cannot go down: Xiao Banxia tang governs it (the formula appears under phlegm-fluid retention).

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

Retching certainly belongs to fire. But if there were no phlegm in the stomach, food could go down somewhat. When grain cannot go down at all, what is it if not phlegm obstructing it? Phlegm always arises from counterflow of qi; so banxia and shengjiang bring down counterflow and open phlegm.

## Notes

A clause using Xiao Banxia tang for vomiting with food unable to go down. The formula is in the chapter on phlegm-fluid retention and cough.
