# Clause 45 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Diarrhea with lung pain: Zishen tang governs it.

Zishen tang (Bistort Decoction) formula

Zishen 0.5 jin; gancao 3 liang.

Of the two ingredients above, with 5 sheng of water first boil the zishen down to 2 sheng, add the gancao, boil down to 1.5 sheng, and take warm in three doses (probably not Zhongjing's formula).

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

Diarrhea with lung pain is qi stagnation. Zishen is bitter and cold by nature and can free blood and qi. The *Bencao* says it governs accumulations of the heart and abdomen and cold-heat pathogenic qi, and Haogu says it treats bloody dysentery. So it is used to disperse stasis and stop pain. But it is very bitter and cold, so gancao is used to moderate it, which also supplements deficiency and boosts qi.

[Part of Xu Bin's formula:] zishen 0.5 jin; gancao 3 liang.

## Notes

A clause using Zishen tang for diarrhea with pain of the lung (chest).

- For "lung pain," Wikisource (*Jingui yaolüe fanglun*) reads "lung abscess."
- A note after the formula says "probably not Zhongjing's formula."
