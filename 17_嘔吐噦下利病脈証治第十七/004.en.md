# Clause 4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The pulse at the inch opening is faint and rapid. Faint means there is no qi; with no qi the nutritive is vacuous; with the nutritive vacuous the blood is insufficient; with the blood insufficient the chest is cold.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

This section traces the cause of vacuity cold in the stomach and verifies it by the inch pulse. The inch opening governs the upper burner; faint means little original yang qi in the chest. The nutritive qi follows the defensive qi, and blood is the nutritive formed into a stream. With no qi to draw and fill the nutritive qi, the nutritive is vacuous; vacuous, the blood is scant and insufficient, unable to be full as in a healthy person. Though yin fire flames and shows a rapid picture, the nutritive and defensive in the chest are actually vacuous and the original yang greatly deficient; how could it not be cold?

## Notes

A clause stating that a faint, rapid inch pulse shows insufficient qi leading to insufficient nutritive blood and cold in the chest, tracing the cause of the previous clause (stomach vacuity cold).
