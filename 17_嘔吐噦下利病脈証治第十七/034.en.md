# Clause 34 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

After diarrhea, the pulse expires and the hands and feet are reverse-cold. If within a full day the pulse returns and the hands and feet warm, the person lives; if the pulse does not return, the person dies.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

This says that diarrhea reaching expiry of the pulse and reverse-cold of the hands and feet is an extremely dangerous pattern, but if the pulse returns and the hands and feet warm, the healthy qi is gradually recovering, so there is life. Suppose the hands and feet warm but the pulse does not return: there is still death. One sees that the pulse should be taken as primary.

## Notes

A clause stating that if, after diarrhea, the pulse expires and the limbs grow cold, the person lives if the pulse returns and the limbs warm within a full day, and dies if the pulse does not return. The same clause appears in the Jueyin chapter of the *Shanghan lun* (corresponding to clause 368).
