# Clause 29 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Diarrhea where the pulse is instead wiry, with fever and sweating of the body: it heals by itself.

## Notes

A clause stating that diarrhea where the pulse becomes wiry instead, with fever and sweating, heals naturally.

- Xu Bin's commentary is attached together after unit 31. He takes wiry as a yang pulse, so yang prevails and there is recovery.
