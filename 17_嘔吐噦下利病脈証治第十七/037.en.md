# Clause 37 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Diarrhea with a slow and slippery pulse: this is repletion; the diarrhea is not about to stop. Purge it urgently; Da Chengqi tang is suitable.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

This says that diarrhea with replete evil, regardless of vacuity or repletion, long or short duration, should always be removed; one must not delay and nurture the trouble. But how is replete evil distinguished? For example, in diarrhea with the pulses at all three positions normal, there should be no disease in the chest. But hardness below the heart on pressure means something with form lies across the inside; it has not yet stirred the qi and blood, so it does not appear in the pulse, but the disease qi is gradually about to reach the pulse. So it is purged urgently to block its progress.

If in diarrhea the pulse is slow, it seems as if the true qi is depleted and the pulse cannot circulate on schedule. But slippery is also seen, and slippery is a pulse of evil; clearly there is evil, and an image of slowness and stagnation appears. Hence "this is repletion." Repletion is replete evil; how would the diarrhea be willing to stop? So it is suitable to purge urgently to drive out the bandit.

If in diarrhea the pulse is not slow but only slippery, one knows that something with form is obstructing. Hence "there should be something to remove; only then will it heal."

If diarrhea has healed and recurs at the same year, month, day, and hour, how could there be a principle of contracting evil in accordance with the time? Clearly the root of the disease has not been pulled out. Earlier, the zang qi was damaged on this day, so when this day comes, the zang qi is timid again; when it is timid, the evil stirs again by itself and takes advantage. Hence "because the disease has not been exhausted." It should be purged to cut off the root. In all the above, Da Chengqi tang is used: zhishi, houpo, mangxiao, and dahuang move and do not stay, stopping as soon as the disease is removed, unlike accumulation-dispersing medicinals, which instead risk damaging and paring the zang-fu.

## Notes

A clause stating that diarrhea with a slow and slippery pulse is repletion and should be purged urgently with Da Chengqi tang.

- Xu Bin discusses together the four clauses from the previous unit to unit 39, holding that where there is replete evil, it should be purged and removed even in diarrhea.
