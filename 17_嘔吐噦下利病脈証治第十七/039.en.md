# Clause 39 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When diarrhea has already got better and recurs at the same year, month, day, and hour, it is because the disease has not been exhausted. It should be purged; Da Chengqi tang (Daijokito) is suitable.

Da Chengqi tang (Major Order-Restoring Decoction) (appears under convulsive disease)

## Notes

A clause stating that diarrhea which recurs at the same time after healing does so because the disease remains, and should be purged with Da Chengqi tang.

- 巳 is read as 已 ("already"); Daizhige and Xu Bin's text read 已.
- The formula is in the chapter on convulsions, dampness, and summer heat. Xu Bin's commentary is attached together after unit 37.
