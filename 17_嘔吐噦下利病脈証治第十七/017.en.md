# Clause 17 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Stomach reflux with vomiting and thirst, wishing to drink water: use the following formula.

Fuling Zexie tang (Poria and Alisma Decoction) (the *Waitai* treats wasting and thirst with an expiring pulse and stomach reflux with vomiting of food, and includes 1 sheng of wheat): fuling 0.5 jin; zexie 4 liang; gancao 2 liang; guizhi 2 liang; baizhu 3 liang; shengjiang 4 liang.

Of the six ingredients above, with 1 dou of water boil down to 3 sheng, add the zexie, boil again down to 2.5 sheng, and take 8 ge warm, three times a day.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

This is Wuling san minus zhuling, plus gancao and shengjiang. Wuling san was originally devised as a formula resolving both exterior and interior, for Taiyang exterior pathogen that has invaded the bladder fu, with dryness, thirst, drinking, and dampness lodged in the central palace. Here, with stomach reflux and vomiting, water comes out with the vomiting, and there is thirst with no water qi inside; so zhuling is removed, and only fuling, zexie, guizhi, and baizhu resolve the deficiency pathogen of both exterior and interior, with shengjiang and gancao added to harmonize the center and stop vomiting.

## Notes

A clause using Fuling Zexie tang for stomach reflux with vomiting, thirst, and wishing to drink water.

- As Xu Bin notes, it is Wuling san with zhuling removed and gancao and shengjiang added.
- For "use the following formula," the other sources read "Fuling Zexie tang governs it."
