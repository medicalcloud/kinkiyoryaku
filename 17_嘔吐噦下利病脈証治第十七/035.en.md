# Clause 35 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Diarrhea with abdominal distension and fullness and pain of the body: first warm the interior, then attack the exterior. For warming the interior Sini tang is suitable; for attacking the exterior Guizhi tang is suitable.

Sini tang (Counterflow Cold Decoction) (the formula appears above)

Guizhi tang (Cinnamon Twig Decoction)

Guizhi 3 liang (bark removed); shaoyao 3 liang; gancao 3 liang (mix-fried); shengjiang 3 liang; dazao 12 pieces.

Of the five ingredients above, chop coarsely, and with 7 sheng of water boil over a gentle fire down to 3 sheng, remove the dregs, adjust the temperature, and take 1 sheng. Shortly after taking it, sip 1 sheng of thin gruel to [base: 巳] assist the medicine's power, and cover warmly for about one double-hour; it is best if the whole body is moist [base: "hot hot"] with what seems a slight sweat. Do not let it drip like water. If one dose produces sweat and the disease gets better, discontinue the remaining doses.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

The *Neijing* says, "When the stomach is cold, fullness disease arises." All the more with diarrhea, the cold is even more certain. But pain of the body, like body heat, shows without doubt that there is an exterior. Since both arise at once, the interior should be taken as urgent; hence "first warm the interior, then attack the exterior," wishing people to know the order of first and later. As for the formulas, Sini tang and Guizhi tang govern it. Sini tang is ganjiang, gancao, and fuzi, and since raw fuzi must be used, warming the interior carries a sense of dispersal. Guizhi tang contains gancao and shaoyao, and also carries the sense of securing the interior.

[Fragments of formula amounts:] shigao 5 liang; xingren 10 pieces; dazao 12 pieces.

Dazao 10 pieces.

[Part of the Guizhi tang instructions:] ... about one double-hour; it is best if the whole body is moist with what seems a slight sweat. Do not let it drip like water. If one dose produces sweat and the disease gets better, discontinue the remaining doses.

## Notes

A clause stating that diarrhea with abdominal distension and fullness accompanied by body pain is treated first by warming the interior with Sini tang, and then by attacking the exterior with Guizhi tang. The same clause appears in the jueyin chapter of the *Shanghan lun* (corresponding to clause 372).

- It shows the principle of order in treatment: when interior vacuity cold and an exterior pattern occur together, the interior comes first.
- 熱熱 ("hot hot") appears to correspond to 漐漐 ("moist with sweat") in the Guizhi tang instructions of the *Shanghan lun* and in the fragment appended to Xu Bin's commentary, and is translated accordingly. For 巳, all other sources read 以 ("to"), and the base has been emended accordingly.
- Two other sources give gancao as 2 liang.
- It is unclear which formula the fragments of amounts after Xu Bin's commentary belong to.
