# Clause 11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Dry retching with diarrhea: use the following formula.

Huangqin jia Banxia Shengjiang tang (Ogonkahangeshokyoto; Scutellaria Decoction plus Pinellia and Fresh Ginger) formula

Huangqin (ogon) 3 liang; gancao (kanzo) 2 liang (mix-fried); shaoyao (shakuyaku) 3 liang; banxia (hange) half a jin; shengjiang (shokyo) 4 liang; dazao (taiso) 20 pieces.

Of the six ingredients above, with 1 dou of water boil down to 3 sheng, remove the dregs, and take 1 sheng warm, twice in the day and once at night.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

In the *Shanghan lun*, the four ingredients huangqin, gancao, dazao, and shaoyao form Huangqin tang (Ogonto), treating combined Taiyang–Shaoyang disease. When the pathogens of Taiyang and Shaoyang combine and enter inward, they carry heat and cause diarrhea, so huangqin is the mainstay. But once the pathogen has entered, it may also bind with retained fluid, of which much retching is clear evidence; so banxia and shengjiang are added.

## Notes

A clause using Huangqin jia Banxia Shengjiang tang for dry retching with diarrhea. It also appears in the lower Taiyang chapter of the *Shanghan lun* (corresponding to clause 172).

- Huangqin tang (huangqin, shaoyao, gancao, dazao) treats diarrhea due to heat, and banxia and shengjiang stop retching.
- Some amounts differ from other sources (most give banxia as half a sheng and shengjiang as 3 liang, and dazao apparently as 12 pieces).
