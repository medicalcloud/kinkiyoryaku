# Clause 20 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When a patient's chest seems to pant but does not pant, seems to retch but does not retch, seems to hiccup but does not hiccup, and it reaches through into the heart with confusion [base: 憤憤 "vexation"] that cannot be helped, [other sources: "Shengjiang Banxia tang (Shokyohangeto) governs it"]

Shengjiang Banxia tang (Fresh Ginger and Pinellia Decoction)

Banxia (hange) half a jin; fresh ginger juice 1 sheng.

Of the two ingredients above, with 3 sheng of water boil the banxia down to 2 sheng, add the ginger juice, boil down to 1.5 sheng, let it cool a little, and take in four doses, three in the day and one at night. When it stops, discontinue the remaining doses.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

Dyspnea, retching, and hiccup are all images of something coming out upward. Now there is the image without the substance. This is the region above the diaphragm receiving pathogen that has not yet attacked the lung and does not come from the stomach; hence "in the chest." It also says "reaches through into the heart with confusion that cannot be helped." 徹 means to pass through: the pathogen in the chest has become heavy and so descends to reach the heart, making it uneasy and confused beyond help. The diffusing and dispersing power of fresh ginger acts as soon as it enters the mouth, so its treatment reaches highest and can clear pathogen above the diaphragm; combined with banxia, it can also bring down turbid drool; so it governs this. It differs from wuzhuyu (goshuyu) bringing down turbid yin and ganjiang (kankyo) regulating cold in the center: those are deficiency cold counterflowing upward, whereas this is only visiting pathogen binding with retained fluid at the highest level. Now this is Xiao Banxia tang (Shohangeto); but there shengjiang (shokyo) is added and decocted, while here the juice is used, in a larger amount. A medicinal used raw goes upward. Precisely because the pathogen is high, the juice is used and decocted only briefly; so the name of the decoction is changed to show that shengjiang is the sovereign.

[Part of Xu Bin's formula:] banxia half a sheng; shengjiang 1 jin.

## Notes

A clause using Shengjiang Banxia tang when the chest seems to have dyspnea, retching, and hiccup but does not, with distress reaching into the heart.

- It has the same two ingredients as Xiao Banxia tang but uses fresh ginger as juice and in a larger amount (see Xu Bin's commentary).
- For the final 者, the base reads "use the following formula," but all other sources read 者, so it has been emended (see the emendations). The other sources then continue "Shengjiang Banxia tang governs it."
- For 憒憒 ("confused"), the base reads 憤憤 ("vexed"); most other sources and Xu Bin's commentary read 憒憒, so it has been emended (see the emendation notes). Most other sources give banxia as half a sheng.
