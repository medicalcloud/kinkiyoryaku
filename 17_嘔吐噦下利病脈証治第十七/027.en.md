# Clause 27 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Diarrhea with a rapid pulse, slight heat, and sweating: it will soon heal by itself. If the pulse is tight, it has not yet resolved.

## Notes

A clause stating that diarrhea with a rapid pulse, slight fever, and sweating will heal naturally, but not yet if the pulse is tight. Nearly the same clause appears in the Jueyin chapter of the *Shanghan lun* (corresponding to clause 361).
