# Clause 6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When a sick person wants to vomit, he must not be purged.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

Since the text above discussed vomiting, this extends it. The method of treating disease values following the momentum and guiding it advantageously; so the *Neijing* says, "What is above, make it go out over the top; what is below, draw it out to exhaustion." That is, when the disease wants to come up as vomiting, one must not force it down. All diseases are like this; hence "when a sick person wants to vomit, he must not be purged." This is a general statement, not only about stomach reflux, though stomach reflux is included.

## Notes

A clause stating the principle of following the momentum of the disease: a patient who wants to vomit must not be purged. It is close to a clause in the yangming chapter of the *Shanghan lun* (corresponding to clause 204, "In cold damage with much retching, even with yangming signs, one must not attack").
