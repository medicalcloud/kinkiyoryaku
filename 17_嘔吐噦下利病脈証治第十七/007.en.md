# Clause 7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Hiccup with abdominal fullness: look at the front and back (urine and stool) to know which part is not free; free it, and recovery follows at once.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

The following several clauses all discuss retching; this first one probably also discusses retching. Retching is a disease of the middle and upper jiao and should not appear together with abdominal fullness. But abdominal fullness clearly means accumulated stagnation in the abdomen, which steams upward into the stomach, making it uneasy, so there is retching. When pathogen is in the abdomen, purging is appropriate; hence "look at the front and back parts." Front and back are urine and stool. Freeing what is not free, the disease diminishes with the freeing and recovers.

## Notes

A clause stating that hiccup with abdominal fullness is cured by seeing whether urine or stool is blocked and freeing it. The same clause appears in the Jueyin chapter of the *Shanghan lun* (corresponding to clause 381).

- Xu Bin suspects 噦 ("hiccup") is an error for 嘔 ("retching") (an interlinear note in Xu Bin's edition also says so), and explains it as retching.
