# Clause 38 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Diarrhea where the pulse is instead slippery: there must be something to remove; purge and it will heal. Da Chengqi tang (Daijokito) is suitable.

## Notes

A clause stating that diarrhea with a slippery pulse instead indicates something excess to be removed, and purging with Da Chengqi tang will heal it.

- Xu Bin's commentary is attached together after the previous unit.
