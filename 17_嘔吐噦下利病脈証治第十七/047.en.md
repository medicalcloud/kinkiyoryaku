# Clause 47 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Appended formulas

Xiao Chengqi tang from the *Qianjin yi*: treats constipation with frequent hiccup and delirious speech (the formula appears above). Huangqin tang (Scutellaria Decoction) from the *Waitai*: treats dry retching with diarrhea.

Huangqin, renshen, and ganjiang, 3 liang each; guizhi 1 liang; dazao 12 pieces; banxia half a sheng.

Of the six ingredients above, with 7 sheng of water boil down to 3 sheng and take warm in three doses.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

[On Xiao Chengqi tang:] This formula seems to be appended because one with hiccup and delirious speech amid diarrhea belongs to stomach repletion, to prepare for distinguishing the disease mechanism. Now it says "constipation," which I fear is an error.

[On Huangqin tang:] Among the retching patterns, it was already said, "Dry retching with diarrhea: Huangqin jia Banxia Shengjiang tang governs it"; Huangqin tang is the main formula for combined taiyang–shaoyang disease, with shengjiang and banxia added because of retching. But in this condition there are cases belonging to stomach vacuity, with the taiyang and shaoyang evils in the center unable to disperse. So huangqin, banxia, and dazao are the mainstay, with renshen and ganjiang added to warm the center qi, for if the center qi does not move, the evil has no way out. Guizhi is also added to drive out the combined evil of taiyang and shaoyang. Gancao, shaoyao, and shengjiang are not used, meaning that once the center qi is warmed and supplemented, there is no need further to diffuse the diaphragm and harmonize the spleen.

## Notes

The appended formulas of this chapter: Xiao Chengqi tang from the *Qianjin yifang* and Huangqin tang from the *Waitai miyao*.

- Xu Bin suspects that "constipation" in the Xiao Chengqi tang entry is an error, as it does not fit an appended formula for the chapter on diarrhea.
- The *Waitai* Huangqin tang differs from the *Shanghan lun* Huangqin tang (huangqin, shaoyao, gancao, dazao): it lacks shaoyao and gancao and contains renshen, ganjiang, guizhi, and banxia. Xu Bin explains it by comparison with Huangqin jia Banxia Shengjiang tang of unit 11.
