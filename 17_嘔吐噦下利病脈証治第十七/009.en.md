# Clause 9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Dry retching, spitting of foamy drool, and headache: Zhuyu tang governs it (the formula appears above).

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

[On the previous unit:] The chest is the position of yang, and retching is yin evil. If the yang qi of the chest were sufficient to resist it, there need not be retching, and even with retching the chest would be unharmed. Retching with fullness of the chest means there is evil within that takes advantage of vacuity to attack the chest, not merely disharmony of the stomach. Vacuity evil belongs to yin, so wuzhuyu, bitter and warm and good at driving out turbid yin, is the sovereign, renshen supplementing vacuity is the assistant, and shengjiang and dazao diffuse the right qi of the upper burner.

Dry retching means sound without matter. Though there is no matter, foamy drool is spat. Zhongjing said, "When there is cold in the upper burner, there is much drool in the mouth." The upper burner already has cold; cold is a yin evil and blocks the yang above, so there is headache. Compared with retching with chest fullness, there seem to be differences of severity and exterior and interior, but the evil must take advantage of vacuity, so Zhuyu tang is again used, warming and supplementing together to drive out turbid yin. That is, though retching varies, the cold is the same.

## Notes

A clause using Wuzhuyu tang for dry retching, spitting of foamy drool, and headache. The same clause appears in the jueyin chapter of the *Shanghan lun* (corresponding to clause 378).

- The first part of Xu Bin's commentary concerns the previous unit (retching with chest fullness).
