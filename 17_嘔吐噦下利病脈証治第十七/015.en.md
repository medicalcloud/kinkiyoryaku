# Clause 15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Retching with fever: use the following formula.

Xiao Chaihu tang formula

Chaihu half a jin; huangqin 3 liang; renshen 3 liang; gancao 2 liang; banxia half a jin; shengjiang 3 liang; dazao 12 pieces.

Of the seven ingredients above, with 1 dou 2 sheng of water boil down to 6 sheng, remove the dregs, decoct again down to 3 sheng, and take 1 sheng warm, three times a day.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

In the previous section, slight heat with reverse-flow meant heavy cold, so it was blamed on shaoyin. If there is no reverse-flow and the fever is not slight, then since the shaoyang pattern originally includes retching, it is treated outright as shaoyang. So Xiao Chaihu tang harmonizes and resolves it; it contains banxia and shengjiang, which also treat retching.

[Part of Xu Bin's formula:] banxia 1 sheng; shengjiang 3 liang; dazao 12 pieces.

## Notes

A clause using Xiao Chaihu tang for retching with fever. The same clause appears in the jueyin chapter of the *Shanghan lun* (corresponding to clause 379).

- For "use the following formula," the other sources read "Xiao Chaihu tang governs it." Most other sources give gancao as 3 liang.
