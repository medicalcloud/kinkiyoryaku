# Clause 32 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Diarrhea with clear grain: one must not attack the exterior. If sweat comes out, there will surely be distension and fullness.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

This is spontaneous diarrhea not due to mistaken purgation; there is already an exterior, and the internal cold is also severe, so both are seen. Attacking the exterior must be avoided. If the exterior is attacked, yang becomes vacuous and yin all the more exuberant, and exuberance brings distension and fullness; hence "if sweat comes out, there will surely be distension and fullness."

## Notes

A clause stating that diarrhea with clear grain must not be treated by sweating, which would cause abdominal distension and fullness. The same clause appears in the jueyin chapter of the *Shanghan lun* (corresponding to clause 364).
