# Clause 26 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Diarrhea with slight heat and thirst and a weak pulse: it will soon heal by itself.

## Notes

A clause stating that diarrhea with slight fever, thirst, and a weak pulse will heal naturally. The same clause appears in the jueyin chapter of the *Shanghan lun* (corresponding to clause 360).

- Xu Bin's commentary on the six clauses of units 26–31 is attached together after unit 31.
- For 今 ("soon"), Xu Bin's text reads 令 ("causes it to").
