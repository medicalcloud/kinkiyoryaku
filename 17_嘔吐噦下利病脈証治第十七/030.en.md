# Clause 30 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Diarrhea with [passing of] gas: one should disinhibit the urine.

## Notes

A clause stating that diarrhea with frequent passing of gas is treated by promoting urination.

- Xu Bin's commentary is attached together after unit 31. He explains that the qi is stagnant and disordered, and when urination is freed the qi transforms and is no longer disordered.
