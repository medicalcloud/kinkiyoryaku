# Clause 28 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Diarrhea with a rapid pulse and thirst: it will soon heal by itself. If it does not get better, there will surely be passing of pus and blood, because there is heat.

## Notes

A clause stating that diarrhea with a rapid pulse and thirst will heal naturally, but if it does not, heat will cause stools with pus and blood. The same clause appears in the Jueyin chapter of the *Shanghan lun* (corresponding to clause 367).

- 清 here stands for 圊 ("privy," hence "to pass stool"); Wikisource (*Jingui yaolüe*) and Xu Bin's text read 圊.
- Xu Bin's commentary is attached together after unit 31.
