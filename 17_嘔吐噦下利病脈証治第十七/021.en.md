# Clause 21 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Dry retching and hiccup; if the hands and feet are reverse-cold, Jupi tang governs it.

Jupi tang (Tangerine Peel Decoction)

Jupi (tangerine peel) 4 liang; shengjiang half a jin.

Of the two ingredients above, with 7 sheng of water boil down to 3 sheng and take 1 sheng warm. As soon as it goes down the throat, there is recovery.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

When hiccup is mentioned together with retching, the hiccup is the heavier. Hiccup due to collapse of the original qi is kidney vacuity on the verge of expiry. If it comes from dry retching, then even with reverse-cold of the hands and feet, it is clearly cold qi of the stomach binding and not reaching the four limbs. So jupi, warming the stomach, is the mainstay, combined with shengjiang to diffuse and disperse the counterflow qi.

## Notes

A clause using Jupi tang for dry retching and hiccup with cold hands and feet.

- Xu Bin interprets the cold limbs here not as collapse of the original qi but as cold qi of the stomach binding and failing to reach the limbs.
