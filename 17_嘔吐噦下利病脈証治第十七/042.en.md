# Clause 42 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Heat diarrhea with heavy discharge [other sources: "heaviness below"]: Baitouweng tang (Hakutooto) governs it.

Baitouweng tang (Pulsatilla Decoction)

Baitouweng (hakutoo; pulsatilla) 2 liang; huanglian (oren), huangbai (obaku; phellodendron), and qinpi (ash bark), 3 liang each.

Of the four ingredients above, with 7 sheng of water boil down to 2 sheng, remove the dregs, and take 1 sheng warm. If it does not heal, take more.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

Heat diarrhea with heaviness below means heat damages the yin qi of the stomach, so it sinks down and is heavy. Sinking down damages the kidney, so four bitter cold ingredients are used to firm it. Baitouweng clears blood heat of Yangming, huanglian clears the heart and spleen, qinpi harmonizes the liver, and huangbai calms the kidney, so they have the merit of achieving effect together. Not using a single medicinal that regulates or raises qi despite the heaviness below is because the disease has already invaded the blood aspect and is not solely in the qi. Note: in the *Shanghan lun* this formula also governs "diarrhea with wanting to drink water," explained as "because there is heat." Wanting to drink water differs from thirst: thirst is merely dried fluids, while wanting to drink water means the yin aspect is dried by fire heat, so bitter cold clearing and descending medicinals are likewise needed to wash it away, unlike the acrid cool used to relieve thirst of the upper jiao.

[Part of Xu Bin's formula:] baitouweng 3 liang; huanglian 3 liang; huangbai 3 liang; qinpi 3 liang.

## Notes

A clause using Baitouweng tang for heat diarrhea with tenesmus. The Jueyin chapter of the *Shanghan lun* (corresponding to clause 371) has "Heat diarrhea with heaviness below: Baitouweng tang governs it."

- The base's 重下 is 下重 ("heaviness below") in most other sources, and Xu Bin's commentary also reads 下重.
- A formula clearing heat and firming the intestines with four bitter cold ingredients: baitouweng, huanglian, huangbai, and qinpi.
