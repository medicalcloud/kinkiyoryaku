# Clause 36 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Diarrhea where the pulses at all three positions are normal, but on pressure the area below the heart is hard: purge it urgently; Da Chengqi tang [base: "Da Cheng tang"] is suitable.

## Notes

A clause stating that when there is diarrhea but the pulse is normal and the area below the heart is hard on pressure, one should purge urgently with Da Chengqi tang.

- Xu Bin's commentary on the four clauses of units 36–39 is attached together after the next unit.
- The base reads "Da Cheng tang," lacking 気, but all other sources read "Da Chengqi tang" (Wikisource [*Jingui yaolüe fanglun*] has the miswritten 大承気揚).
