# Clause 25 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the tenth month, do not eat pepper; it harms the heart and damages the heart vessel.

## Notes

A clause forbidding pepper in the tenth month.

- The base lacks 椒 ("pepper"; a blank space is left), but all other sources have it, so it has been supplied (see the emendations).
