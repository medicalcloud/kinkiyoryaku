# Clause 78 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Applying lepidium seed to sores on the head: the medicine forms and enters the brain, killing the person.

## Notes

A clause stating that applying lepidium seed to head sores lets the medicine enter the brain, which is fatal.

- Xu Bin's text has the note 恐是或字 ("probably the character 或") after 薬成.
