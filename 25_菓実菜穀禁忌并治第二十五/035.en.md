# Clause 35 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Eating raw scallion with rooster, pheasant, or white dog meat makes the seven orifices bleed for years.

## Notes

A clause stating that eating raw scallion with rooster, pheasant, or white dog meat causes bleeding from the seven orifices.

- For 白犬 ("white dog"), the base reads 大, but all other sources read 白犬, so it has been emended (see the emendations).
