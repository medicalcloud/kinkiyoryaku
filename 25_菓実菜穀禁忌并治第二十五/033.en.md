# Clause 33 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Raw scallion must not be eaten with honey; it kills people. Single-clove garlic is even more strictly forbidden.

## Notes

A clause stating that raw scallion must not be eaten with honey, and single-clove garlic even less so.
