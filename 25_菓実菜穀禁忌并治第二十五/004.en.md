# Clause 4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Eating many peaches makes one hot; one must then not bathe in water, or it causes dribbling urination and cold-heat disease.

## Notes

A clause stating that eating many peaches causes heat, and bathing afterward causes strangury and cold-heat disease.
