# Clause 13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Eating many fresh jujubes causes heat and thirst and distension of qi; those with cold and heat and emaciation should all the more not eat them; they harm people.

Formula to treat poisoning from eating fruit

Pig bone [other sources add "burned to ash" or the like].

Powder the one ingredient above and take a square-inch spoonful with water. It also treats poisoning from horse liver, leak-soaked dried meat, and the like.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

[Part of Xu Bin's formula:] pig bone (burned).

## Notes

A clause forbidding eating many fresh jujubes, with a formula for fruit poisoning.
