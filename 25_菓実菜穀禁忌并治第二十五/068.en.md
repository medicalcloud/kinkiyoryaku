# Clause 68 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Drinking wine, eating pork, and lying in sorghum or rice straw brings on jaundice.

## Notes

A clause stating that drinking wine, eating pork, and sleeping in straw brings on jaundice.

- For 秫 ("sorghum"), the base reads 禾, but all other sources read 秫, so it has been emended (see the emendations).
