# Clause 75 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Another formula

A rhinoceros-horn decoction is also good.

Formula to treat gluttonous overeating that does not digest, with hardness, fullness, and pain of the heart and abdomen

Salt 1 sheng; water 3 sheng.

Boil the two ingredients above until the salt dissolves and divide into three doses; the food should be vomited out, and there is immediate improvement.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

[Xu Bin's formula:] Salt 1 sheng; water 2 sheng. Boil the two ingredients above to three boilings and likewise divide into three doses; the food should be vomited out, and there is immediate improvement.

## Notes

Another formula for food poisoning, and a formula treating overeating by inducing vomiting with salt water.

- Xu Bin's text gives the water as 2 sheng.
