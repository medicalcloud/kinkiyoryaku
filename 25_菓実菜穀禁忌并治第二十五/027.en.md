# Clause 27 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the four seasonal junctures, do not eat raw mallow; it keeps food and drink from being digested and brings on a hundred diseases. Not only in food but also in medicines it must never be used; one should be very careful about this.

## Notes

A clause stating that raw mallow must not be eaten in the seasonal junctures, nor used in medicines.

- For 百 ("hundred"), the base reads 日, but all other sources read 百, so it has been emended (see the emendations).
