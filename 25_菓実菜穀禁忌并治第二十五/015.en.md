# Clause 15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Mushrooms whose caps curl upward, or that are red, must not be eaten.

Formula to treat poisoning from eating mushrooms, with oppression and agitation, near death

Drink 1 sheng of human fecal juice; drink one or two sheng of earth water; drink soybeans boiled into a strong liquid; take any emetic or purgative medicine. All resolve it.

For eating maple-trunk mushrooms with unceasing weeping, treat with the preceding formulas.

Formula to treat mistakenly eating wild taro, with vexation and poisoning [other sources: restlessness], near death (use the preceding formulas) (the root of wild taro is called "kui taro" by people east of the mountains; if planted taro is not harvested for three years, it too becomes wild taro; both kill people).

## Notes

Prohibitions on mushrooms and formulas for poisoning from mushrooms and wild taro.
