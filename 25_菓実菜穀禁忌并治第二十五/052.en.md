# Clause 52 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Eating much small garlic harms the strength of the heart.

Formula for agitation after eating

Fermented soybeans.

Boil the above into a strong liquid and drink it.

Gouwen resembles celery; eaten by mistake, it kills. Formula to resolve it (the *Zhouhou* says: it resembles evodia and edible mustard)

Jinian (adenophora) 8 liang.

Boil the one ingredient above in 6 sheng of water down to 2 sheng and take warm in two doses (where gouwen grows, there are no other plants beside it [base: 仿], and its stems are hairy; distinguish it by this).

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

[Part of Xu Bin's text:] Fermented soybeans.

Boil the one ingredient above in 6 sheng of water down to 2 sheng and take warm in two portions (where gouwen grows, there are no other plants beside it, and its stems are hairy).

## Notes

A clause forbidding eating much small garlic, with a formula for agitation after eating and a formula for resolving gouwen (a poisonous plant) poisoning.

- For the base's 仿, most other sources read 傍無 ("beside it there are no"), which the translation follows.
