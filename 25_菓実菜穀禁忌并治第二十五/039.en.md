# Clause 39 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Chinese chive bulb must not be made into lamb [other sources: broth] with beef and eaten; it causes conglomeration disease. Chives likewise.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

[Xu Bin's text (the clause of the next unit) with note:] Much ailing [probably the character 食 "eating"] of water shield stirs hemorrhoids.

## Notes

A clause stating that eating xie or chives in a broth with beef causes conglomeration disease.

- The text by Xu Bin attached to this unit is the clause of the next unit with an interlinear note.
