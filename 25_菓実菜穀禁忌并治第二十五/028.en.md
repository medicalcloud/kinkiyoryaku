# Clause 28 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

If, after a seasonal illness has improved but before full health, one eats raw vegetables, the hands and feet will surely swell.

## Notes

A clause stating that eating raw vegetables during convalescence from a seasonal illness causes swelling of the limbs.
