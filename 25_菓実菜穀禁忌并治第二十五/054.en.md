# Clause 54 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In spring and autumn, dragons carrying their essence enter celery, and if people happen to eat it they fall ill. When it strikes, the hands [other sources: the hands are blue]

back and abdomen are full and painful beyond endurance; it is called flood-dragon disease. Formula to treat it:

Hard sugar, two or three sheng.

Take the one ingredient above twice a day, and three to five things like lizards will be vomited out.

Formula to treat poisoning from eating bitter gourd

Lirang (millet stalks).

Boil the above and take the liquid several times; it resolves.

## Notes

A formula for flood-dragon disease (apparently a parasitic disease) from eating celery, and a formula for poisoning from bitter gourd.

- For 名 ("called"), the base reads 各, and for 瓠 ("gourd"), the base reads 瓜; all other sources read the emended characters, so they have been emended (see the emendations).
- The paragraph break after "hands" follows a line break in the base. Most other sources read 手青 ("the hands are blue") for 手背.
