# Clause 74 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Food or drink that foams when stirred with rhinoceros-horn chopsticks, or that swells up when poured on the ground, kills a person who eats it.

Formula to treat food poisoning with vexation and fullness

Kushen 3 liang; vinegar 1.5 sheng.

Boil the two ingredients above to three boilings, raising and lowering three times, and take it; when the food is vomited out, there is immediate improvement. Boiling with water also works.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

[Part of Xu Bin's formula:] kushen 3 liang; vinegar 1.5 sheng.

## Notes

How to recognize poisonous food and drink, and a formula for food poisoning.
