# Clause 40 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Much ailing [probably "eating"] of water shield stirs hemorrhoids.

## Notes

A clause stating that eating much water shield aggravates hemorrhoids.

- For 病 ("ailing"), Daizhige reads 食 ("eating"), and Xu Bin's text has the note 恐是食字 ("probably the character 食").
