# Clause 12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Walnuts must not be eaten in quantity; they stir phlegm-fluid retention.

## Notes

A clause stating that eating many walnuts stirs phlegm-fluid retention.

- For 痰 ("phlegm"), the base reads 疾, but all other sources read 痰, so it has been emended (see the emendations).
