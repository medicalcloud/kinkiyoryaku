# Clause 9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Pears must not be eaten in quantity; they cause cold in the center. Those with incised wounds and parturient women should also not eat them.

## Notes

A clause stating that eating many pears chills the center, and that the wounded and parturient women should not eat them.
