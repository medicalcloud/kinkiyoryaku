# Clause 50 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Eating smartweed mixed with raw fish robs one of qi and causes pain in the genitals.

## Notes

A clause stating that eating smartweed with raw fish robs one of qi and causes genital pain.

- For 陰咳, Daizhige reads 陰核.
