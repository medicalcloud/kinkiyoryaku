# Clause 30 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the tenth month, do not eat raw vegetables that have been frosted; they make the face lusterless, the eyes rough, the heart painful, and the lower back ache, or bring on heart malaria; when the malaria strikes, all ten fingernails and toenails turn blue-green and one is exhausted and limp.

## Notes

A clause forbidding frosted raw vegetables in the tenth month.
