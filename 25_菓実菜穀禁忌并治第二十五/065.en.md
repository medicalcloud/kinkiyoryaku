# Clause 65 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When very drunk and streaming with sweat in summer, one must not wash the body with cold water or use a fan; illness results at once.

## Notes

A clause stating that washing with cold water or fanning oneself when very drunk and sweating in summer causes illness.
