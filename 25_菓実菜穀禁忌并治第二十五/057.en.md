# Clause 57 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

After eating soybeans and the like, avoid eating pork.

## Notes

A clause stating that pork should be avoided after eating soybeans.

- For 等 ("and the like"), Daizhige and Xu Bin's text read 屑 ("powder").
