# Clause 16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Sichuan pepper with closed mouths is poisonous. If eaten by mistake, it pricks the throat, the qi is afflicted to the point of expiring, or there is vomiting

and purging of white foam, with numbness and cold of the body. Formula for urgent treatment:

Drink a decoction of rougui; or eat garlic; or drink earth water; or drink fermented soybeans boiled into a strong liquid. All resolve it.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

[Xu Bin's text:] Drink a decoction of rougui; drink plenty of cold water, one or two sheng; or eat garlic; drink earth water; or drink fermented soybeans boiled into a strong liquid. All resolve it.

## Notes

The poison of closed-mouthed Sichuan pepper and formulas for treating the poisoning.

- The other sources have "(plenty of) cold water, one or two sheng" before "or eat garlic."
- The paragraph break after "or there is vomiting" follows a line break in the base.
