# Clause 11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Pomegranates must not be eaten in quantity; they harm the lung.

## Notes

A clause stating that eating many pomegranates harms the lung.

- For 肺 ("lung"), Xu Bin's text reads 腹 ("abdomen").
