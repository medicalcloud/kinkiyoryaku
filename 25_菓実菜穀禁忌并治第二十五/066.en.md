# Clause 66 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

After drinking wine, moxibustion on the abdomen or back is strictly forbidden; it makes the intestines bind.

## Notes

A clause strictly forbidding moxibustion on the abdomen or back after drinking wine.

- For 灸 ("moxibustion"), the base reads 炙, but all other sources read 灸, so it has been emended (see the emendations).
