# Clause 44 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The heart of mallow must not be eaten; it harms people. The leaves are especially cold. Do not eat those with yellow backs and red stems.

## Notes

A clause stating that the heart of mallow, or leaves with yellow backs and red stems, must not be eaten.
