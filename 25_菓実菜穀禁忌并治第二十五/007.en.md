# Clause 7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Damsons must not be eaten in quantity; they cause abdominal distension. Crabapples must not be eaten in quantity; they weaken all the vessels.

## Notes

A clause stating that damsons and crabapples must not be eaten in quantity.
