# Clause 69 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Eating malt sugar and drinking much wine is strictly forbidden.

## Notes

A clause strictly forbidding drinking much wine after eating malt sugar.

- For 飴 ("malt sugar"), the base reads 治, but all other sources read 飴, so it has been emended (see the emendations).
