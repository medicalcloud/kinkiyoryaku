# Clause 79 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When mercury enters the ears of people or of the six domestic animals, they all die. If gold or silver is placed beside the ear, the mercury is spat out.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

[Xu Bin's note:] (吐 "spat" is perhaps 出 "comes out".)

## Notes

A clause on the harm of mercury entering the ear and on drawing it out by placing gold or silver beside the ear.
