# Clause 53 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Among vegetables there is water henbane, with round, glossy leaves; it is poisonous. Eaten by mistake, it makes one deranged, appearing

like wind strike, or vomiting blood. Formula to treat it:

Gancao.

Boil the above and take the liquid; it resolves at once.

## Notes

A formula for poisoning from mistakenly eating water henbane.

- For 光 ("glossy"), the base reads 先, but all other sources read 光, so it has been emended (see the emendations).
- The paragraph break after "appearing" follows a line break in the base.
