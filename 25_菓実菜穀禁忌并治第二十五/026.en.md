# Clause 26 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the eleventh and twelfth months, do not eat Chinese chive bulbs; they cause much snivel and spittle.

## Notes

A clause forbidding xie (Chinese chive bulb) in the eleventh and twelfth months.
