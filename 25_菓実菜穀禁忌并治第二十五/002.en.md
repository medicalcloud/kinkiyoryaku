# Clause 2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Fruit that has fallen to the ground overnight and been eaten by insects or ants is strictly forbidden for people to eat.

## Notes

A clause strictly forbidding fruit that has lain on the ground overnight and been eaten by insects or ants.
