# Clause 58 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Eating barley for a long time causes [base: 㿍; most other sources: "produces ..."; Xu Bin's text: "produces ringworm"].

## Notes

A clause stating that long-term eating of barley produces a skin disease.

- For the character 㿍, the other sources read 作 ("produces") and Xu Bin's text 作癬 ("produces ringworm"); the meaning of the base's character is unclear.
