# Clause 4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In taiyang disease, excessive sweating therefore causes tetany.

## Notes

This and the next two units describe tetany caused by mistaken treatment. Xu Bin's commentary is attached after the third (the clause on sore sufferers).

- Excessive sweating loses fluids and blood, so the sinews are not nourished and tetany results.
