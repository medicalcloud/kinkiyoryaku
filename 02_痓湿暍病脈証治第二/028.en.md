# Clause 28 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang summer heat stroke with a hot, aching, heavy body and a feeble, weak pulse: this is caused by being damaged by cold water in the summer months, the water moving into the skin. Yiwu Guadi tang (Single-Ingredient Melon Stalk Decoction) governs it.

Yiwu Guadi tang formula

Guadi (katei; melon stalk) "two sevens" (i.e., 14) pieces.

Cut the above, boil with 1 sheng of water down to 5 ge, remove the dregs, and take in a single dose.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

This too is of the kind struck by summer heat while at rest. (lacuna) yin (lacuna) the body receives (lacuna) blocking summer heat in the collaterals causes damage; no (lacuna) slow. Such (lacuna) the body hot, aching, and heavy (lacuna) qi (lacuna). But the central qi (lacuna) cold water, the water moves (lacuna) heavy. Water (lacuna) and the internal qi again (lacuna) what has form, and not (lacuna) qi; one should only regulate and supplement what is formless (lacuna) we know. Is this why no formula was set out?

Yiwu Guadi tang formula: guadi 20 pieces.

## Notes

A clause using Yiwu Guadi tang for summer heat stroke caused by water-damp lodging in the skin, as from bathing in cold water in summer.

- Guadi is known as an emetic, but here it is said to be used to remove the water qi in the skin.
- 二七箇 means fourteen pieces. Wikisource and icntcm read "twenty," and Xu Bin's edition also has 二十箇.
- 苽蒂 in the formula name is the same as 瓜蒂.
- Xu Bin's commentary has many lacunae (闕) in its base text (the Siku quanshu edition), and the meaning cannot be made out. The translation only indicates the surviving words.
