# Clause 8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Sudden distension and enlargement of the abdomen means it is about to resolve. If the pulse is as before, and instead hidden and wiry, it is tetany.

## Notes

A continuation of the previous clause, giving judgments on the course of tetany.

- Why sudden abdominal distension should be a sign of imminent resolution (the evil moving inward and the exterior tetany easing) is unclear, and commentators differ; some suspect errors or lacunae.
- If the pulse remains hidden and wiry, the tetany has not yet resolved.
- Xu Bin's edition reads 其 before "the pulse is as before."
- Xu Bin's commentary is attached after the next unit.
