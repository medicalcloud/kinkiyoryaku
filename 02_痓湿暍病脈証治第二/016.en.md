# Clause 16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The disease of those with dampness is aching all over the body (one version says aching and vexation), fever, and a body color like smoked yellow.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

This speaks of [disease that is] entirely dampness, long depressed and turned to heat. When dampness carries wind, the wind runs to the empty spaces, so the pain stays only in the joints. If dampness alone causes the disease, it soaks the whole body, and the whole body aches, not only the joints. But when dampness lasts long it becomes depressed, and depression produces heat, so there is fever. When the heat lasts long, the qi steams to the skin and hair; so where there is aching there is dampness, and where there is dampness there is heat, and the color becomes like smoked yellow. "Smoked": dampness is turbid yin, and when depressed it becomes hot and dry, so the color is yellow and also tinged with a scorched black, not bright.

## Notes

A clause describing dampness disease that has spread through the body and become depressed, producing heat. The same clause appears in the *Shanghan lun*'s chapter on tetany, dampness, and summerheat stroke.

- Aching of the whole body, fever, and a smoked (dull) yellow skin color are characteristic. It is distinguished from the bright yellow (yang jaundice) of the later chapter on jaundice.
