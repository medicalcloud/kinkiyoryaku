# Clause 11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The *Maijing* says: In those with tetany, the pulse is hidden and hard, straight up and down.

## Notes

The same *Maijing* quotation as in the interlinear note of unit 9, written as a line of main text. It is probably a note added by a later hand (such as the Song editors) that entered the main text.
