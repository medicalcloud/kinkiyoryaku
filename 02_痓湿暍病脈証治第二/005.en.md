# Clause 5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In wind disease, purging causes tetany; if sweating is then also induced, there will surely be hypertonicity.

## Notes

It states that mistakenly purging a wind (exterior) disease causes tetany, and that inducing sweating in addition damages both yin and yang, causing hypertonicity of the sinews.
