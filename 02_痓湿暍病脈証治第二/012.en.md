# Clause 12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease, with its signs complete, the body stiff and rigid (*jiji*), and the pulse deep and slow instead: this is tetany, and Gualou Guizhi tang (Trichosanthes and Cinnamon Twig Decoction) governs it.

Gualou Guizhi tang formula

Gualougen (trichosanthes root) 2 liang; guizhi (cinnamon twig) 3 liang; shaoyao (peony) 3 liang; gancao (licorice) 2 liang; shengjiang (fresh ginger) 3 liang; dazao (jujube) 12 pieces.

Of the six ingredients above, with 9 sheng of water boil down to 3 sheng, and take warm in three doses, to obtain a slight sweat. If no sweat comes out, after the time of a meal sip hot porridge to bring it out.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

This is the main formula for tetany with sweating and no aversion to cold [soft tetany]. "Taiyang disease with its signs complete" means a hot body, headache, and sweating. "The body stiff" is another way of saying the back arches. *Jiji* is the appearance of a stiff neck and nape. "The pulse deep and slow instead" means a yang pattern with a yin pulse; this is how the tetany pulse differs from true cold damage. Its cause is that the sinews have long been unnourished, and dampness further carries wind to dry them. So Guizhi tang is used as the main treatment for wind damaging the wei-defense [aspect], and gualougen is added to clear heat in the qi aspect and greatly moisten the already depleted fluids of the Taiyang meridian. Then the meridian qi flows freely, the pathogenic wind resolves by itself, the damp qi moves by itself, the sinews are no longer dry, and the tetany is cured.

[Xu Bin's formula:] gualougen 3 liang; guizhi 3 liang (remove the bark); gancao 2 liang (mix-fried); shaoyao 3 liang.

Of the six ingredients above, pound them, and with 7 sheng of water boil over a low fire down to 3 sheng; remove the dregs and adjust to a suitable temperature.

## Notes

The clause of Gualou Guizhi tang, the main formula for soft tetany.

- Tetany is when the Taiyang signs (fever, sweating, etc.) are all present, the body is stiff, and the pulse is deep and slow rather than floating.
- Gualou Guizhi tang is Guizhi tang with gualougen added: Guizhi tang resolves the exterior, and gualougen generates fluids to moisten the sinews.
- The base writes the formula name 括楼 in the clause and 括蔞 in the formula heading. The usual writing is 栝蔞 (栝楼).
- The formula at the end of Xu Bin's commentary is part of the formula in Xu Bin's edition that has entered the commentary; it lacks shengjiang and dazao (four ingredients though it says six), and the amount of water and other details differ from the base.
- *Jiji* also appears in the *Shanghan lun* clauses of Guizhi jia Gegen tang and Gegen tang.
