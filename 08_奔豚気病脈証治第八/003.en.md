# Clause 3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Running piglet qi surging up into the chest, with abdominal pain and alternating cold and heat: Bentun tang (Running Piglet Decoction) governs it.

Bentun tang formula

Gancao, xiongqiong, and danggui, 2 liang each; banxia 4 liang; huangqin 2 liang; shengge (fresh pueraria) 5 liang; shaoyao 2 liang; shengjiang 4 liang; ganligen baipi (white bark of sweet plum root) 1 sheng.

Of the nine ingredients above, with 2 dou of water boil down to 5 sheng and take 1 sheng warm, three times in the day and once at night.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

This is running piglet qi meeting an external evil in the exterior. So its appearance is like running piglet, with qi surging into the chest; though it does not reach the throat, it is like running piglet arising from fright. But the accompanying abdominal pain means a guest evil is in the abdomen, and the alternating cold and heat mean a guest evil is half exterior and half interior. So Guizhi tang and Xiao Chaihu tang are combined, without guizhi and chaihu, using the method for combined taiyang–shaoyang disease to harmonize the guest evil combined within. Because the liver qi is not regulated, the acrid, warm xiongqiong and danggui are added; because the inside is cold, painful, and counterflowing, the sweet, warm shengge and ligen are added. That is, when the guest evil is gone and the liver qi flows freely, the running piglet stops by itself without being treated. Gui is the precise medicinal for running piglet, yet it is not used because of the internal tension.

[Part of Xu Bin's formula:] gancao 2 liang; xiongqiong 2 liang; danggui 2 liang; banxia 4 liang.

## Notes

A clause using Bentun tang for running piglet with qi surging into the chest, abdominal pain, and alternating cold and heat.

- Bentun tang is characterized by ganligen baipi (sweet plum root bark); danggui, xiongqiong, and shaoyao nourish liver blood, huangqin and gegen clear heat, and banxia and shengjiang bring down counterflow qi. It is often regarded as a formula for running piglet due to upward counterflow of liver qi.
- Xu Bin explains it as Guizhi tang combined with Xiao Chaihu tang, without guizhi and chaihu.
