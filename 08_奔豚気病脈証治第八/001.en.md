# Clause 1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The master said: Among diseases there are running piglet, vomiting of pus, fright and fear, and fire evil. These four kinds of disease are all contracted through the outbreak of fright.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

One who treats disease, whether internal damage or external contraction, should, when a disease is suddenly added, inquire deeply into its cause. For example, when external evil has already done damage, there may further be fright that enters the heart, and in severe cases fright that stirs the kidney qi. Though the signs differ, one should know the source from which the disease was received; then distinguishing which is shallow and which deep and treating accordingly is not difficult. So running piglet, vomiting of pus, fright palpitations, and fire evil are made the four kinds of disease. Running piglet is a disease of the kidney. Vomiting of pus, fright palpitations, and fire evil are all diseases of the heart aspect of the upper burner. Zhongjing has treatments for each: for vomiting of pus he says, "Retching and vomiting with pus and blood must not be treated; when the pus has been retched out, it recovers by itself"; for heart palpitations he uses Banxia Mahuang wan; for fire evil he uses Guizhi qu Shaoyao jia Longgu Muli tang. How do we know? Tracing their source, all alike are contracted through the outbreak of fright; that is, apart from the original disease, these again arise from fright. He first speaks of the four kinds together to show how fright can cause these various diseases. But this section discusses only running piglet, so afterward he speaks only of the patterns and treatment of running piglet.

## Notes

The opening clause of the chapter on running piglet qi. Four diseases — running piglet, vomiting of pus, fright and fear, and fire evil — are all said to arise from fright.

- Apart from running piglet (attacks of qi surging up from the lower abdomen), the other three are not discussed in this chapter; related clauses appear in the chapter on retching, vomiting, hiccup, and diarrhea (vomiting of pus) and the chapter on fright palpitations, bleeding, and blood in the stool (fright and fire evil).
- Xu Bin's "Banxia Mahuang wan for palpitations" and "Guizhi qu Shaoyao jia Longgu Muli tang for fire evil" refer to formulas in the chapter on fright palpitations and bleeding (the latter is called Guizhi Jiuni tang in the text).
