# Clause 2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The master said: Running piglet disease starts from the lesser abdomen and surges up to the throat; during an attack one feels near death, then it returns and stops. It is always contracted through fright and fear.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

This describes the main signs of running piglet. There is something formless whose appearance is like a piglet. The pig is a water animal; rushing up from below, it is named "running." That it starts from the lesser abdomen is because the kidney evil stirs. That it surges up to the throat means there is no longer anything blocking it in the middle and upper burners. Evil arising from a viscus differs from that in a channel or bowel, so during an attack one feels near death. Kidney water fears earth, so when the spleen qi recovers a little, it returns and stops. Tracing its cause, external evil cannot enter directly like this; it is due to the qi of fright damaging the heart and the qi of fear damaging the kidney. The qi of heart and kidney originally interact and communicate; now the evil acts as agent, and there is no longer any limit or restraint. Hence ["it is always contracted through fright and fear"].

Discussion: Zhongjing says, "Jueyin disease: qi surges up to the heart"; he says, "The accumulation of the kidney is running piglet"; and here again he says, "Running piglet qi surges from the lesser abdomen up to the throat, always contracted through fright and fear." Fright enters the heart. Then to what does this pattern ultimately belong? Answer: it involves heart, liver, and kidney alike. Formerly Dongyuan said, "The body has seven surging gates above and below, all surging from below and above so that the inhaled qi cannot descend and return to the spleen and kidney." But what Dongyuan meant by surging is the true qi filling and acting as locks for one another, so that outer qi cannot enter inward and lower yin cannot flee upward; from the Corporeal Soul Gate (anus) through the Screen Gate, the Pylorus, the Cardia, the Throat Gate, the Inhaling Gate, up to the Flying Gate (lips), yang qi always rises and yin qi lies low. In a person this means no disease; in the realm, peace.

Now the evil of fright and fear suddenly damages the heart qi. Fright makes the qi descend. The heart is the sovereign; when [the sovereign] leaves the hall and flees and the fences are all removed, the yin lying low in the lower burner — since *yi* and *gui* (liver and kidney) share a source — the kidney evil carries the liver qi and enters upward. It is like An Lushan, having broken through Tong Pass, driving far ahead unopposed; without the restoring army from Fengxiang, Chang'an would not easily have been recovered. So in this pattern the accumulation arises in the kidney, the qi surges in disorder together with jueyin, and the abandonment of defense lies in the heart; what doubt can there be?

## Notes

A clause on the main signs and cause of running piglet disease.

- Qi surges from the lower abdomen up to the throat in attacks of near-fatal distress, then subsides. It is said to be caused by fright or fear. Some compare it to modern panic attacks.
- Xu Bin's "Discussion" cites Li Dongyuan's theory of the seven surging gates and, using the analogy of An Lushan's rebellion, explains that when fright and fear disorder the heart qi, the kidney evil carries the liver qi and surges upward.
- The base reads 小腹; it is emended to 少腹 because all other sources so read (see the emendation note).
