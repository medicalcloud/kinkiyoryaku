# Clause 3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

It is also said: Nosebleed from spring to summer is [due to] taiyang; nosebleed from autumn to winter is [due to] yangming.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

Nosebleed is blood of the yang channels flaming upward with fire and coming out wildly through the nasal orifices. In spring and summer, yang is outside; nosebleed under pressure then comes from taiyang, since taiyang governs the yang of the channels. In autumn and winter, yang is inside; with no heat pressing from outside, it comes from yangming, since yangming governs the yang of central earth. Why is this? Nosebleed is blood of the clear passages of the yang channels and is never governed by the yin channels. The foot shaoyang vessel starts at the outer canthus, rises to the head, follows the corner of the forehead, descends behind the ear, and runs in front of the hand shaoyang; the branch of the hand shaoyang too only enters the ear and rises to the corner of the ear; neither can go from the governing vessel through Wind Mansion over the vertex down into the nose. So taiyang and yangming are assigned to the four seasons.

## Notes

A clause attributing nosebleed in spring and summer to taiyang and in autumn and winter to yangming.
