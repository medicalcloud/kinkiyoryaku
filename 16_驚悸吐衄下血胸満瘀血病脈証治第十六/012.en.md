# Clause 12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Palpitations below the heart: Banxia Mahuang wan (Pinellia and Ephedra Pill) governs it.

Banxia Mahuang wan formula

Banxia and mahuang, equal amounts.

Powder the two ingredients above, make refined-honey pills the size of aduki beans, and take three pills with a drink, three times a day.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

Palpitation differs greatly from fright. In fright, evil is bound and the spirit light cannot bear it, so the pulse is stirring. In palpitation, one is afflicted by yin evil and the heart qi is insufficient, so the pulse is only weak. The yin evil is phlegm-rheum. So banxia is the mainstay, combined with mahuang, since old phlegm cannot be removed without mahuang. Three pills per dose, three times a day, remove it gradually; quietly hidden phlegm cannot be expelled suddenly. Palpitations may also arise from vacuity detriment, but since there are no other vacuity signs here, the blame is placed solely on phlegm. This is just the phlegm-rheum section's "mild cases have shortness of breath, severe cases palpitations."

## Notes

A clause using Banxia Mahuang wan for palpitations below the heart, regarded as palpitations due to water-rheum (phlegm-rheum).

- Banxia removes water-rheum and mahuang frees the yang qi; small doses remove it gradually.
