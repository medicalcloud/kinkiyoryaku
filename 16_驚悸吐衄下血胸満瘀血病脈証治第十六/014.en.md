# Clause 14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bleeding below, with stool first and blood after: this is distant blood. Huangtu tang (Yellow Earth Decoction) governs it.

Huangtu tang formula (also governs vomiting of blood and nosebleed)

Gancao; gandihuang; baizhu; fuzi (blast-fried); ejiao; huangqin [base: 黄苓]; 3 liang each; yellow earth from the stove, half a jin.

Of the seven ingredients above, with 8 sheng of water boil down to 3 sheng and take warm in two doses.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

Bleeding below, compared with vomiting of blood, moves with the current and does not counterflow; the disease is not in the qi, so it should be sought in the abdomen. From stool first and blood after, we know that before defecation the blood aspect does not stir, and only after straining at stool does blood come down. This is internal cold unable to warm the spleen, and the spleen's origin insufficient to command the blood. The spleen dwells in central earth, which, spoken of from the lower jiao, is distant. So fuzi warms the kidney yang; fearing excessive dryness, ejiao and dihuang strengthen yin as assistants; baizhu fortifies the spleen qi. The spleen also likes coolness, so huangqin and gancao clear heat; and fire-baked yellow earth, of a kind with the spleen, leads them into the spleen, giving it warm qi, like the yang qi in the earth in winter, the root of growth. Truly a marvelous formula. Spleen and kidney are the roots of the postnatal and prenatal; when they are regulated, the ying-nutrient and wei-defense work together and blood does not come out wildly; so it also governs vomiting of blood and nosebleed. In my view it is especially suited to vomiting of blood with spontaneous diarrhea.

[Xu Bin's formula:] gancao 3 liang; dihuang 3 liang; baizhu 3 liang; fuzi 3 liang (blast-fried); ejiao 3 liang; huangqin 3 liang; yellow earth from the stove, half a jin.

## Notes

A clause using Huangtu tang for "distant blood" (bleeding after defecation, said to be from the stomach, small intestine, and the like).

- Huangtu tang centers on baked earth from the stove (*fulonggan*), warms the spleen with baizhu and fuzi, nourishes blood with dihuang and ejiao, and clears heat with huangqin. It is regarded as the representative formula for bleeding due to spleen deficiency cold unable to command the blood.
- 黄苓 is an error for 黄芩 (huangqin), which the other sources read.
