# Clause 7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When a heavy drinker coughs, it will surely lead to vomiting of blood; this is caused by drinking to excess.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

This says that vomiting of blood is not always due to qi failing to contain blood, nor always to yin vacuity with exuberant fire. When a heavy drinker develops a cough, the lung is already extremely damaged and is further shaken by coughing, so it must lead to vomiting of blood. This is not an internal cause; hence "caused by drinking to excess." So we know that treatment should center on clearing the heat of alcohol.

## Notes

A clause stating that cough in a heavy drinker leads to vomiting of blood, attributing it to excessive drinking.
