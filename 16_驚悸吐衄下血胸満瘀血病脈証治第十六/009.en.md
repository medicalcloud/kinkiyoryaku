# Clause 9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In blood loss, the exterior must not be released; if sweat comes out, there will be chills, shivering, and trembling.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

This says that those with blood loss must not be made to sweat even if there is exterior evil. If made to sweat, the original yin, already vacuous from blood loss, is joined by vacuity of the yang in the exterior through sweating; then there is nothing to guard within and nothing to secure without. So the vacuity is extreme as if cold, and there are chills and shivering — no yang to defend itself. Trembling means being too vacuous to control oneself.

## Notes

A clause stating that those who have lost blood must not be made to sweat even with exterior signs, and that sweating causes chills and trembling. The same content appears in the middle taiyang chapter of the *Shanghan lun* (corresponding to clause 87).
