# Clause 10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

A sick person with fullness of the chest, withered lips, a green-blue tongue, and a dry mouth, who only wants to rinse the mouth with water but not swallow it, without cold or heat, with a feeble, large pulse that comes slowly, whose abdomen is not full but who says, "I feel full": there is static blood.

## Notes

A clause on the signs of static blood: chest fullness, lusterless lips, a bluish-purple tongue, and a dry mouth (wanting to rinse but not drink); characteristically the abdomen does not look distended though the patient complains of fullness. Xu Bin's commentary is attached after the next unit.

- For 嗽水, most other sources read 漱水 ("rinse the mouth with water").
