# Clause 32 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Appended formula

Fuling Yin (Poria Drink) from the *Waitai*: treats lodged phlegm and abiding water in the heart and chest, which, after one has spontaneously vomited water, leaves the heart and chest deficient, with qi fullness and inability to eat. It dissolves phlegm qi and enables eating.

Fuling, renshen, and baizhu, 3 liang each; zhishi 2 liang; jupi 2.5 liang; shengjiang 4 liang.

Of the six ingredients above, with 6 sheng of water boil down to 1 sheng 8 ge and take warm in three doses, taking the next dose after about the time it takes to walk eight or nine li.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

This is the most reliable formula for treating the aftermath of phlegm-fluid retention. The heart and chest are deficient because of great vomiting, so renshen is added; if there was no great vomiting, renshen may be omitted and zhishi reduced. Vulgar physicians say using chenpi reduces the power of renshen, but this not only uses chenpi but also adds 2 liang of zhishi, supplementing and draining together. How excellent!

## Notes

An appended formula, Fuling Yin from the *Waitai*, for distension and inability to eat after phlegm-fluid retention has been vomited, leaving the chest deficient.

- Renshen, baizhu, and fuling supplement the spleen and stomach; zhishi, jupi, and shengjiang move qi and dissolve phlegm. It was often used later for stomach disorders.
