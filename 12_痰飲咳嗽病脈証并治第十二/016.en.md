# Clause 16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Phlegm-fluid retention below the heart with propping fullness of the chest and ribsides and dizziness: use the following formula.

Fu Gui Gan Bai tang (Poria, Cinnamon Twig, Licorice, and White Atractylodes Decoction) formula

Fuling (bukuryo) 4 liang; guizhi (keishi) and baizhu (byakujutsu), 3 liang each; gancao (kanzo) 2 liang.

Of the four ingredients above, with 6 sheng of water boil down to 3 sheng and take warm in three doses. Urination will become free.

## Notes

A clause using Ling Gui Zhu Gan tang (Ryokeijutsukanto) for phlegm-fluid retention below the heart with propping fullness of the chest and ribsides and dizziness. It is the same as Ling Gui Zhu Gan tang in the middle Taiyang chapter of the *Shanghan lun*. Xu Bin's commentary is after the next unit.

- The base names the formula Fu Gui Gan Bai tang; the other sources read Ling Gui Zhu Gan tang.
- For "use the following formula," the other sources read "Ling Gui Zhu Gan tang governs it."
