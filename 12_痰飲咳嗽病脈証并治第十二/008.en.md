# Clause 8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When there is prolonged fluid retention below the heart, the person's back is cold, [a patch] the size of a hand [base: 水 "water"; other sources: 手 "hand" or 掌 "palm"].

## Notes

A clause stating that prolonged fluid retention below the heart causes a palm-sized cold area on the back. Xu Bin's commentary is attached after unit 10.

- 水 in 如水大 is 手 ("hand") or 掌 ("palm") in the other sources and appears to be an error in the base; the translation follows the other sources.
