# Clause 40 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the water has gone and the retching has stopped, if the person's body is swollen, add xingren (kyonin) to govern it. The pattern calls for adding mahuang (mao), but because the person already has numbness, it is not added. If, contrary to this, it is added, there will surely be cold limbs. The reason is that the person's blood is deficient and mahuang would release the yang.

Ling Gan [Wuwei jia] Jiang Xin [Ban]xia [Xing]ren tang [base: Ling Shao Jiang Wei Xin Xia Ren tang]

Fuling (bukuryo) 4 liang; gancao (kanzo) 3 liang; wuwei[zi] half a sheng; ganjiang (kankyo) 3 liang; xixin (saishin) 3 liang; banxia (hange) half a sheng; xingren half a sheng (remove the skins and tips).

Of the seven ingredients above, with 1 dou of water boil down to 3 sheng, remove the dregs, and take half a sheng warm, three times a day.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

"The body is swollen" means swelling of the body. The lung qi is already deficient and cannot spread everywhere, so it stagnates and swells; so xingren disinhibits it. When the qi does not stagnate, the swelling disperses by itself. "The pattern calls for adding mahuang" because the chapter on water swelling says, "Deficient swelling without water is called qi water; inducing sweating stops it by itself," and mahuang is suitable for inducing sweat. "The person already has numbness" is the earlier numbness of the hands and feet. Cough should not cause numbness, yet there is numbness; hence "contrary." "Adding it contrary to this" means mistakenly using mahuang, so that yin and yang are both deficient and there are cold limbs. But since the meaning of "surely cold limbs" is not yet clear, it says the reason is that, because of blood deficiency, the blood cannot attach to the qi, so the qi moves roughly and there is numbness; if further the yang qi is released and drained with a mahuang decoction, there is blood loss followed by sweating, the warm qi departs and cold qi prevails — how could there not be cold limbs? This is just like cold limbs from blood deficiency in a new mother who has lost blood and then sweated. Ling Gan Wuwei jia Jiang Xin Banxia Xingren tang formula.

## Notes

A clause adding xingren when the water has gone and vomiting stopped but the body is swollen. Normally one would induce sweating with mahuang, but because of blood deficiency with numb limbs, mahuang is not used; using it would release the yang and cause cold limbs.

- The base names the formula Ling Shao Jiang Wei Xin Xia Ren tang, but it contains no shaoyao (shakuyaku) (芍); most other sources read Ling Gan Wuwei jia Jiang Xin Banxia Xingren tang, which the translation follows.
