# Clause 14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Propping rheum also has panting with inability to lie down, plus shortness of breath, yet the pulse is even.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

Above it was said, "A pulse wiry on one side means rheum." But the lung is far from the pathways of the pulse; when rheum is in the lung itself, the lung itself is ill, producing panting, and obstructs the qi so that it does not spread, producing shortness of breath. This is a disease of the lung's form that does not affect the pulse, so it is not wiry. Propping rheum belongs to replete evil and is one-sided, producing panting, inability to lie down, and shortness of breath; this is rheum evil lodged in the diaphragm with yangming qi counterflowing, yet it may not affect the pulse, and the pulse is not wiry. Hence "even." He feared that people, because the pulse is not wiry, would doubt all three signs — panting, shortness of breath, and inability to lie down — and think it was not rheum. Since the rheum pulse is originally wiry, he cites these two exceptional cases.

## Notes

A clause stating that propping rheum with panting, inability to lie down, and shortness of breath may have a normal pulse. With the previous clause, it gives examples of rheum without a wiry pulse.
