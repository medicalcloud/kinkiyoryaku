# Clause 14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Thoracic fluid retention also has dyspnea with inability to lie down, plus shortness of breath, yet the pulse is even.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

Above it was said, "A pulse wiry on one side means retained fluid." But the lung is far from the pathways of the pulse; when retained fluid is in the lung itself, the lung itself is ill, producing dyspnea, and obstructs the qi so that it does not spread, producing shortness of breath. This is a disease of the lung's form that does not affect the pulse, so it is not wiry. Thoracic fluid retention belongs to excess pathogen and is one-sided, producing dyspnea, inability to lie down, and shortness of breath; this is retained fluid pathogen lodged in the diaphragm with Yangming qi counterflowing, yet it may not affect the pulse, and the pulse is not wiry. Hence "even." He feared that people, because the pulse is not wiry, would doubt all three signs — dyspnea, shortness of breath, and inability to lie down — and think it was not retained fluid. Since the retained fluid pulse is originally wiry, he cites these two exceptional cases.

## Notes

A clause stating that thoracic fluid retention with dyspnea, inability to lie down, and shortness of breath may have a normal pulse. With the previous clause, it gives examples of retained fluid without a wiry pulse.
