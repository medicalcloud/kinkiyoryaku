# Clause 15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Those who suffer from phlegm-fluid retention should be harmonized with warm medicinals.

## Notes

A clause stating the great principle of treating phlegm-fluid retention. Since retained fluid is a yin pathogen, warm medicinals move the yang qi and transform it. It is often quoted later. Xu Bin's commentary is attached after unit 17.
