# Clause 27 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Propping rheum with inability to breathe: Tingli Dazao Xiefei tang governs it (the formula appears under lung abscess).

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

Speaking of propping rheum, it is not lung rheum. But inability to breathe means the lung is filled by propping rheum and its qi is closed. One exhalation and one inhalation is a breath; inability to breathe means the qi is closed and the spreading of lung qi cannot follow its normal measure. Tingli is bitter and cold, light in substance, and images yang, so it can discharge the closure in the lung in the yang aspect; precisely because it discharges closure, it is good at expelling water. Now qi and water disturb each other and the lung is replete with evil, so tingli discharges it; hence "drains the lung." Dazao is taken because its sweetness can supplement the stomach, and it also restrains the bitterness of tingli so that it does not damage the stomach.

## Notes

A clause using Tingli Dazao Xiefei tang for propping rheum with severe dyspnea. The formula is in the chapter on lung atrophy, lung abscess, cough, and ascending qi.
