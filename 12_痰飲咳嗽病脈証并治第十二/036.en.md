# Clause 36 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cough with counterflow, leaning to breathe, and inability to lie down: Xiao Qinglong tang governs it (the formula appears above under lung abscess).

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

Cough with counterflow, leaning to breathe, and inability to lie down are the definite signs of thoracic fluid retention above. Xiao Qinglong tang rather than Shizao tang is used because it must carry an exterior [pattern]. But if the disease has not long broken out and one cannot lie down, the heat too is very urgent. So for the time being guizhi and mahuang treat the exterior, and ganjiang and banxia treat the retained fluid.

## Notes

A clause using Xiao Qinglong tang for the typical signs of thoracic fluid retention (cough, leaning to breathe, inability to lie down) with an exterior pattern.

- The interlinear note "the formula appears above under lung abscess" appears to be an error, since the Xiao Qinglong tang formula is in the spillage-retained fluid clause of this chapter (unit 23). Xu Bin's edition reads "the formula appears earlier."
- From this clause on (units 36–42) is a series of clauses describing in order the changes after taking Xiao Qinglong tang and how to deal with them.
