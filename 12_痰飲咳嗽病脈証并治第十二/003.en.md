# Clause 3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When water is in the heart, below the heart is hard and pounding, the breath is short, and one dislikes water and does not want to drink.

## Notes

The first of five clauses describing the signs when water is in each of the five viscera. Xu Bin's commentary is attached after unit 7.
