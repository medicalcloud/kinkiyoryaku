# Clause 9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In lingering rheum, pain below the ribs draws into the supraclavicular fossa, and coughing stops it each time (one version reads "worsens it").

## Notes

A clause on lingering rheum with pain below the ribs radiating to the supraclavicular fossa. Whether coughing stops it or worsens it is opposite between the text and the interlinear note. Xu Bin's commentary is after unit 10.

- The base reads 欠盃; it is emended to 欠盆 because all other sources so read (see the emendation note).
