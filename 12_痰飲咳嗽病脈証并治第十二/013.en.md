# Clause 13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Lung rheum is not wiry [in the pulse]; one suffers only from panting and shortness of breath.

## Notes

A clause stating that rheum in the lung does not make the pulse wiry, with only panting and shortness of breath. Xu Bin's commentary is attached after the next unit.
