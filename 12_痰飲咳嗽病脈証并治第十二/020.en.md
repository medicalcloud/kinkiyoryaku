# Clause 20 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

A wiry, rapid pulse with cold rheum is difficult to treat in winter and summer.

## Notes

A wiry, rapid pulse means yin (cold rheum) within yang; in the seasons of great cold and great heat the disease is affected by them and hard to treat. Xu Bin's commentary is after unit 22.

- For 弦数 ("wiry and rapid"), Xu Bin's edition reads 弦索 ("wiry like a rope").
