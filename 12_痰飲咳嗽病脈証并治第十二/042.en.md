# Clause 42 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

First thirst, then retching: water is lodged below the heart; this belongs to those with retained fluid. Xiao Banxia [jia] Fuling tang (Bukuryoto) governs it (the formula appears above).

## Notes

A clause stating that one who is first thirsty and drinks much, then retches, has water lodged below the heart and belongs to those with retained fluid, treated with Xiao Banxia jia Fuling tang (Shohangekabukuryoto). It is the last clause of the chapter on phlegm-fluid retention and cough.

- Xu Bin's commentary has been placed after unit 30 (the Xiao Banxia jia Fuling tang clause).
- Most other sources name the formula Xiao Banxia jia Fuling tang.
