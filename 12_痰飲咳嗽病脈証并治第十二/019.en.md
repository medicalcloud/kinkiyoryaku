# Clause 19 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

A floating, thready, slippery pulse means damage by retained fluid.

## Notes

A clause on the pulse of sudden damage by water entering from outside ("damage by retained fluid"). Xu Bin's commentary is attached after unit 22.
