# Clause 24 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Propping rheum in the diaphragm, with panting and fullness, glomus and hardness below the heart, a soot-black complexion, and a sunken, tight pulse, contracted for several tens of days, not cured though the physician has induced vomiting and purged: Mufangji tang (Woody Fangji Decoction) governs it. Vacuous cases recover at once; replete cases recur after three days, and if given it again [base: 愈] without recovery, Mufangji tang minus shigao plus fuling and mangxiao should govern it.

Mu [base: 朮] Fangji tang formula

Mufangji 3 liang; shigao, twelve pieces the size of a hen's egg; guizhi 2 liang; renshen 4 liang.

Of the four ingredients above, with 6 sheng of water boil down to 2 sheng and take warm in two doses.

Mufangji [minus Shigao] plus Fuling and Mangxiao tang formula

Mufangji and guizhi, 2 liang each; renshen [4 liang]; mangxiao 3 ge; fuling 4 liang.

Of the five ingredients above, with 6 sheng of water boil down to 2 sheng, remove the dregs, add the mangxiao, decoct [base: 前] slightly again, and take warm in two doses. With slight diarrhea there is recovery.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

The diaphragm is above the membranes, somewhat higher than below the heart. Below the heart corresponds to the upper opening of the stomach duct, and the diaphragm is above it, a place that cannot be pressed. "In the diaphragm" means outside the lung, not lung rheum. But the chest is the residence of the lung, and the qi presses the lung, so there is also panting. The inside of the diaphragm is clear and empty like the sky; rheum qi takes advantage of it, so there is fullness. Glomus and hardness below the heart: because of mistaken vomiting and purging, guest qi stirred the diaphragm, and the glomus blockage is below the heart. A soot-black complexion: the essential splendor of the stomach is [in the face], but the yin evil seizes its right qi, so the face is not nourished and is black; black is the image of yin. Water makes the pulse sunken and cold makes it tight, so the pulse is sunken and tight; there is no doubt the error lay in vomiting and purging. Further, having been contracted for several tens of days, we know there is vacuity. So Mufangji tang governs it. Mufangji is the sovereign, freeing the congestion of water qi; renshen is the assistant, for fear that vacuity cannot move the evil. But the diaphragm belongs to the province of taiyang, and without gui the qi does not transform, so guizhi is added. With glomus, vacuity heat must be depressed in the chest, so shigao is added.

Han fangji can drain damp-heat in the blood and free its congestion and stagnation, so it suits damp swelling of the lower burner and skin water with dribbling, removing accumulated heat of the bladder, but it is forbidden for heat patterns of the qi aspect of the upper burner. Mufangji frees damp congestion and also governs vacuity wind, so it is used with shigao to treat the diaphragm. If there is replete heat within, it can be removed only by the urgent, violent, dispersing force of mangxiao; shigao, cold and slow by nature, cannot remove bound heat in the stomach. Hence "replete cases recur; if given it again without recovery, remove shigao and add mangxiao," meaning replete evil heat differs from vacuity heat of the qi aspect. The later statement under Ji Jiao Li Huang wan, "if there is fluid in the mouth with thirst, add mangxiao," is the same. Fuling is also added to guide out the water.

[Xu Bin's formulas:] mufangji 3 liang; shigao, twelve pieces the size of a hen's egg; guizhi 2 liang; renshen 4 liang.

Mufangji 2 liang; guizhi 2 liang; fuling 4 liang; renshen 4 liang; mangxiao 2 ge.

## Notes

A clause using Mufangji tang for propping rheum in the diaphragm with panting and fullness, glomus and hardness below the heart, a darkened complexion, and a sunken, tight pulse, not cured by vomiting and purging. For cases that recur after improvement (repletion), shigao is removed and fuling and mangxiao added.

- Mufangji tang removes water with fangji, clears heat with shigao, frees the yang with guizhi, and supplements with renshen. It is sometimes related to heart failure and the like.
- The base also writes 朮防巳 in the formula headings and formulas, an error for 木防已; since the other sources read 木, these have been emended as well (see the emendation notes).
- In the formula minus shigao plus fuling and mangxiao, the amount of renshen is missing; some other sources have 4 liang.
- For 愈 in 復愈不愈者, most other sources read 与 ("given again without recovery"), which the translation follows. 前 in 再微前 is an error for 煎 ("decoct").
- The base reads 朮防己湯 and 墨黒 in the clause; these are emended to 木防己湯 and 黧黒 because all other sources so read (see the emendation notes).
