# Clause 21 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

A sunken, wiry pulse means suspended rheum with internal pain.

## Notes

A clause on the pulse of suspended rheum. Sunken indicates water and wiry indicates pain. Xu Bin's commentary is after the next unit.
