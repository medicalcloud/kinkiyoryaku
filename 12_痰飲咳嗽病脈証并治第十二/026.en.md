# Clause 26 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Thoracic fluid retention with fullness of the chest: Houpo Dahuang tang (Magnolia Bark and Rhubarb Decoction) governs it.

Houpo Dahuang tang formula

Houpo 1 chi; dahuang 6 liang; zhishi 4 liang [other sources: 4 pieces].

Of the three ingredients above, with 5 sheng of water boil down to 2 sheng and take warm in two doses.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

Speaking of thoracic fluid retention, it must be somewhat one-sided. But since it does not say there is pulling pain below the ribs, nor propping fullness of the ribsides, but only fullness of the chest, it is one-sided but not very; so it can be driven out directly, using Xiao Chengqi tang. When the qi is smoothed, it descends by itself.

Discussion: This is Xiao Chengqi tang; when it treats abdominal fullness with pain and blockage, it is directly called Sanwu tang. This one emphasizes dispersing bound qi, so houpo is central; that one is named in contrast to Qiwu tang.

## Notes

A clause using Houpo Dahuang tang for thoracic fluid retention with chest fullness.

- Houpo Dahuang tang has the same three ingredients (houpo, dahuang, zhishi) as Xiao Chengqi tang and Houpo Sanwu tang, differing in amounts, with more dahuang.
- For zhishi, the base has 4 liang, most other sources 4 pieces.
