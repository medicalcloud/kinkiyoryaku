# Clause 2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Malaria that breaks out on the first day of the month should recover on the fifteenth. If it does not recover, it should resolve at the end of the month. If it still does not recover, what then [base: 去何]?

## Notes

This forms a single question-and-answer with the next unit ("The master said ..."), describing the course of malaria and the formation of the "malaria mother." Xu Bin's commentary follows the next unit.

- The time of resolution is judged by the phases of the moon (fifteen days, a month).
- For the final 当去何, most other sources read 当云何 ("what then?"), and icntcm reads 当如何. 去 is taken as a graphic error for 云.
