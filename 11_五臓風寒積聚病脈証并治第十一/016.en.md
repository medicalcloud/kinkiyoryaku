# Clause 16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Kidney fixity disease: the person's body is heavy, the lower back is cold as if sitting in water, the appearance is like water [disease], yet there is no thirst, urination is spontaneously free, and eating and drinking are as usual. The disease belongs to the lower burner. From exertion with sweating, the inside of the clothes (one version reads "exterior") being cold and damp, it is contracted over a long time. Below the lower back there is cold pain, and the abdomen is heavy as if carrying five thousand coins. Ganjiang Ling Zhu tang [other sources: Gan Jiang Ling Zhu tang] governs it.

Gancao Ganjiang Fuling Baizhu tang (Licorice, Dried Ginger, Poria, and White Atractylodes Decoction) formula

Gancao and baizhu, 2 liang each; ganjiang and fuling, 4 liang each.

Of the four ingredients above, with 5 sheng of water boil down to 3 sheng and take warm in three doses. The lower back will soon be warm.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

Kidney fixity means sticking and not flowing. Since the defensive qi comes from the lower burner, when there is a fixed evil in the kidney, dampness stagnates the defensive qi, so the body is heavy. The lower back is the residence of the kidney; the true qi does not penetrate, so it is cold as if sitting in water. "The appearance is like water": when the kidney has evil, the girdle vessel at the waist is always ill, so there is a loose sensation as if sitting in water, and the non-functioning part is slightly distended like water. Yet with no thirst, the upper burner is not ill; with free urination and normal eating, the middle burner carries out its orders and transforms qi. So it is summed up as "the disease belongs to the lower burner." Dampness is received from below, so we know it was contracted over a long time from exertion with sweating and cold, damp clothes. It must be said to be due to exertion because the kidney is not vacuous without exertion, and evil cannot take advantage without kidney vacuity. But though called kidney fixity, dampness is a yin evil, and yin evil damages yin, not only the kidney. So it says generally, "below the lower back there is cold pain, and the abdomen is heavy as if carrying five thousand coins," meaning both lower back and abdomen are heavy. In short, kidney fixity is damp evil damaging yin, the kidney included, unlike winter cold striking directly. So the medicine centers on fuling, baizhu, and gancao to support earth and percolate dampness, with the single ingredient ganjiang to warm the center and remove cold. That is, the kidney's origin is not ill; its illness is only in the outer residence of the kidney, so treating the outer cold-damp cures it by itself. If guizhi and fuzi were used, they would instead damage the yin of the kidney.

Discussion: Wind and cold of the kidney viscus are both missing. But seeing that the *Qianjin*'s Sanhuang tang uses duhuo and xixin to treat wind-strike reaching the kidney, describing the condition as "vexing heat with a disordered heart, aversion to cold, no desire to eat all day," and describing wind-strike of the kidney as "squatting with lower back pain," we know that the pattern of kidney wind stirring within, missing from the *Jingui*, is not far from this. Cold striking the kidney is direct strike and should not go beyond the clauses on reverse-flow, diarrhea, and wanting to vomit but not vomiting. What the *Neijing* says — "The appearance of the kidney struck by wind: much sweating, aversion to wind, the face puffy as if swollen, spinal pain with inability to stand straight, a sooty color, impaired sexual function; the diagnosis is on the flesh, the color black" — speaks of wind entering from the exterior and damaging the channel qi of shaoyin, the external picture of the kidney struck by wind.

[Xu Bin's formula:] gancao 2 liang; baizhu 2 liang; ganjiang 4 liang; fuling 4 liang.

## Notes

A clause using Gancao Ganjiang Fuling Baizhu tang (Ling Jiang Zhu Gan tang) for kidney fixity (cold and dampness fixed below the waist), with a cold, heavy lower back and cold pain below it.

- Because there is no thirst, urination is free, and eating is normal, the disease lies not in the kidney viscus itself but in cold-damp of the lower back (the outer residence of the kidney). Ganjiang warms, fuling and baizhu remove dampness.
- The clause names the formula Ganjiang Ling Zhu tang; all other sources read Gan Jiang Ling Zhu tang.
