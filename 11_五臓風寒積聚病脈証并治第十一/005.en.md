# Clause 5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the liver is struck by cold, both arms cannot be raised, the root of the tongue is dry, one often sighs, there is pain in the chest with inability to turn over, and on eating one vomits and sweats (the *Maijing* and *Qianjin* say: at times night sweats, cough, and after eating, vomiting of the juice).

## Notes

A clause on the signs of cold-strike of the liver. Xu Bin's commentary is after unit 6.

- 巳 in the interlinear note is an error for 已.
