# Clause 8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the heart is struck by wind, there is feather-warm fever, inability to rise, hunger in the heart, and vomiting as soon as one eats.

## Notes

The signs of wind-strike of the heart. Xu Bin's commentary is attached after unit 11.
