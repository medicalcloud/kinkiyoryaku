# Clause 4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the liver is struck by wind, the head and eyes twitch, both ribsides ache, one always walks stooped, and one craves sweet things.

## Notes

The signs of wind attack of the liver: twitching of the head and eyes, pain in both ribsides, a stooped gait, and craving for sweet things. Xu Bin's commentary is attached after unit 6.
