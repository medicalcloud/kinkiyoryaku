# Clause 13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the spleen is struck by wind, there is feather-warm fever, the person looks drunk, the abdomen is vexed and heavy, the skin and eyelids twitch, and the breath is short.

## Notes

The signs of wind-strike of the spleen. Xu Bin's commentary is attached after the next unit.
