# Clause 10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In one whose heart is damaged, when he is fatigued, the head and face turn red and the lower body is heavy, there is pain in the heart with vexation, fever, throbbing at the umbilicus, and a wiry pulse. This is caused by damage to the heart viscus.

## Notes

The signs of damage to the heart viscus: with fatigue the face reddens, the lower body is heavy, the chest hurts with vexation, there is fever, and the umbilical area pulsates. Xu Bin's commentary is after unit 11.
