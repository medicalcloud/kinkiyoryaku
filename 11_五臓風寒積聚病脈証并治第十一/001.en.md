# Clause 1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the lung is struck by wind, the mouth is dry with panting, the body sways and is heavy, the head is veiled, and there is swelling and distension.

## Notes

The opening clause of the chapter on wind and cold of the five viscera and accumulations and gatherings. It describes in turn the signs when each of the five viscera is struck by wind or cold and the pulses of the "dead viscus." In wind-strike of the lung there are a dry mouth, panting, a swaying, heavy feeling of the body, a veiled head, and swelling. Xu Bin's commentary is attached after unit 3.

- The five-viscera clauses of this chapter have many gaps; the Song editors also noted that cold-strike of spleen and kidney and others are missing (see unit 14).
