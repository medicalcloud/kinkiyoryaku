# Clause 1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the lung is struck by wind, the mouth is dry with dyspnea, the body sways and is heavy, the head is veiled, and there is swelling and distension.

## Notes

The opening clause of the chapter on wind and cold of the five zang organs and abdominal masses. It describes in turn the signs when each of the five zang organs is struck by wind or cold and the pulses of the "dead zang organ." In wind attack of the lung there are a dry mouth, dyspnea, a swaying, heavy feeling of the body, a veiled head, and swelling. Xu Bin's commentary is attached after unit 3.

- The five-zang organs clauses of this chapter have many gaps; the Song editors also noted that cold-strike of spleen and kidney and others are missing (see unit 14).
