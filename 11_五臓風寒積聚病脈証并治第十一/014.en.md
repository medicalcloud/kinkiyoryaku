# Clause 14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The dead zang organ [pulse] of the spleen: superficially it is large and hard; on pressure it is like an overturned cup, empty and seeming to sway; the patient dies (Your servants [Lin] Yi and others consider: each of the five zang organs should have wind attack and cold-strike, but here for the spleen only wind attack is recorded, and for the kidney neither wind attack nor cold-strike is recorded; this is because the ancient bamboo slips are in great disorder, and being so far from antiquity, there is no text with which to supplement them).

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

The function of fire is that once it shines it spreads everywhere. So when heart fire is fanned by wind, there is at once feather-warm fever. The spleen governs the flesh of the whole body, so when wind enters, the feather-warm heat likewise spreads at once through the whole body. But when the heat of the flesh arises from the organ itself, the essence conveyed upward is depressed, so one is limp as if drunk. The abdomen is governed by the spleen; the pathogen overcomes the right and the right does not function, so it is vexed and heavy. Twitching of the skin and eyelids is wind within. Shortness of breath: the lung relies on the spleen's essence for its qi, so when the spleen is ill, the lung is deficient and the breath short.

The spleen belongs to the central region, and its image is moderate. Large and hard superficially means earth dried and warped, contrary to its stable, solid nature — what is called "like a bird's beak." Like an overturned cup on pressure is like collapsed earth. Seeming to sway means it cannot complete its arrival and is about to tilt and collapse; so its movement is not lively movement and its turning is not round turning. What is this if not the visceral qi about to be cut off? Hence "the patient dies."

Discussion: The *Jingui* lacks cold-strike of the spleen, but it would be no more than spontaneous diarrhea, abdominal pain, abdominal distension, not eating, and the like, and can be inferred. The above signs of wind attack of the spleen are the changed signs in everything in the body that depends on central earth to receive the benefit of its harmonious qi. This details the internal picture of the spleen struck by wind. What the *Neijing* says — "The appearance of the spleen struck by wind: much sweating, aversion to wind, lassitude of the body, unwillingness to move the limbs, a thin, slightly yellow color, no appetite; the diagnosis is above the nose, the color yellow" — speaks of the external picture of the spleen struck by wind.

## Notes

A clause on the dead zang organ pulse of the spleen, with a note by the Song editors (Lin Yi and others).

- A pulse large and hard superficially, empty like an overturned cup on pressure, and seeming to sway indicates exhaustion of the spleen's genuine qi and death.
- The interlinear note attributes the absence of cold-strike of the spleen and of wind- and cold-strike of the kidney to disorder in the ancient bamboo slips.
- Xu Bin's commentary also explains unit 13.
