# Clause 19 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The master said: Heat in the upper burner leads through coughing to lung atrophy. Heat in the middle burner produces hardness. Heat in the lower burner produces blood in the urine, and also strangury and blockage. Cold in the large intestine often produces duck-stool diarrhea; heat produces intestinal grime in the stool. Cold in the small intestine makes the person heavy below with blood in the stool; heat surely produces hemorrhoids.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

Lung atrophy is contracted through repeated loss of fluids, from much sweating, wasting thirst, vomiting, or constipation. When fluids are lost there is always heat; heat causes cough, and long coughing becomes lung atrophy. Hence "heat in the upper burner, with long coughing, becomes lung atrophy." The middle burner is governed by the spleen and stomach. When the qi is harmonious, the stomach is regulated and the spleen robust. When hot, the qi binds into wasting thirst that even water cannot stop, and the blood binds into hard stool that even attacking cannot bring down; all these are of the kind "hardness." The lower burner belongs to yin and is governed by the nutritive. When hot, the blood cannot return to the channels and comes out with the urine; the qi makes it so. But this is heat alone. If dryness of qi is added to heat, urine dribbles and is inhibited, producing strangury; if withering of blood is added, the stool is hard, blocked, and does not pass, producing blockage. All center on heat; hence "also."

*Wu* is the duck. The duck never has a dry dropping in its life; it is always water and residue mixed. The large intestine is the office of conveyance, whence transformation issues. With cold, the transforming qi is not warm, water and grain are not separated, so residue and water come out mixed, like duck stool. Intestinal grime is like the grime scraped from inside a pig's intestine, what is commonly called pus in the stool. The human intestine always has grime, but without heat the original qi is in charge, so conveyance is normal and the grime diminishes with the stool. With heat the original qi wanes and stagnates, so intestinal grime is passed — meaning its color is bad and it is foul with grime.

The small intestine is the office of receiving and containing, whence the transformation of things issues. It is exterior-interior with heart fire, the so-called *bing* small intestine. Carrying fire to aid yin, the yin does not stagnate; carrying qi to transform blood, the blood returns to the channels. With cold, the qi does not pass upward, so there is heaviness below, and the blood has no qi to command it and moves wildly. The rectum is the head of the large intestine; its gate is the anus. When the small intestine has heat, the large intestine conveys its heat, and qi binds at the anus, producing hemorrhoids; hemorrhoids are the heat of the *bing* small intestine lodged there.

Discussion: Lung atrophy can also have spitting of foamy drool without cough, with enuresis and dizziness, said to be due to cold in the lung. Blood in the urine may be due to heart vacuity and insufficiency or to damp-heat of the stomach, among various differences. There are five strangury types, and blockage too can be cold blockage. Yet all are generally ascribed to heat because one should know that these patterns are usually due to heat. Zhongjing speaks only of the usual, meaning that knowing the usual one can cover all variations. As for duck-stool diarrhea, Zhongjing says "in lung water there is frequent duck-stool diarrhea" and also "when the spleen is vacuous there is duck-stool diarrhea," but here he ascribes it only to cold in the large intestine; we know both hand and foot taiyin can shift cold to the large intestine. Zhongjing says "heat diarrhea with heaviness below" and "heaviness below with pus and blood in the stool," yet here he says "cold in the small intestine causes heaviness below with blood in the stool." Blood is the juice of the middle burner transformed and reddened, circulating through the whole body; the small intestine has fire to steam it, so the blood cannot descend. Now with cold, the blood cannot spread in all directions and falls downward. But speaking only of blood, it is not comparable to that with pus; pus is brewed by heat. Hemorrhoids are mostly due to damp-heat of the large intestine, yet here only the small intestine is blamed. The small intestine is the fire organ, governing receiving and containing, while the large intestine merely conveys. What has been received, if the root is hot, the downstream cannot fail to be scorched and festering. Hence "surely produces hemorrhoids," meaning that even damp-heat of the large intestine comes from the small intestine.

## Notes

A clause on diseases due to heat in each of the three burners (upper: lung atrophy; middle: hardness, such as constipation; lower: hematuria, strangury, blockage) and due to cold and heat of the large and small intestines.

- Cold of the large intestine: duck-stool (watery) diarrhea; heat: intestinal grime (mucous stool). Cold of the small intestine: heaviness below with bloody stool; heat: hemorrhoids.
