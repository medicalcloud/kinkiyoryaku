# Clause 9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the heart is struck by cold, the person suffers a feeling in the heart as if he had eaten garlic. In severe cases heart pain penetrates to the back and back pain penetrates to the heart, like being gnawed by worms. If the pulse is floating, spontaneous vomiting brings recovery.

## Notes

The signs of cold-strike of the heart: a burning feeling in the chest as after eating garlic, and in severe cases pain penetrating between chest and back. If the pulse is floating, the pathogen has not yet bound, and vomiting brings recovery. Xu Bin's commentary is after unit 11.

- For 蠱注, Xu Bin's edition reads 虫注 and suggests it may be an error for 蛀 ("gnawed by insects").
