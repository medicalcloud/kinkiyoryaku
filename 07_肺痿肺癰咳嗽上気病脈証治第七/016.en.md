# Clause 16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Appended formulas

Zhi Gancao tang (Shakanzoto) from the *Waitai*: treats atrophy of the lung lobes with much drool and spittle and queasiness with fluid in the heart; see [the formula in deficiency/consumption] (the base has only the single character 覩 "see").

Gancao tang (Kanzoto; Licorice Decoction) from the *Qianjin*

Gancao (kanzo) [other sources: 2 liang].

Boil the single ingredient above in 3 sheng of water until reduced by half, and take warm in three doses.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

(The following six formulas were collected by Sun Zhi [Sun Qi] and others.)

([The formula of Zhi Gancao tang is] in deficiency/consumption.) The atrophy of the lung lobes pattern generally belongs to dried-up fluids with heat and dryness. This formula is Guizhi tang (Keishito) without shaoyao (shakuyaku), plus renshen (ninjin), dihuang, ejiao (akyo), maren, and maimendong (bakumondo). It is not in a hurry to remove heat but focuses only on generating fluids and moistening dryness; when the deficiency recovers, fluids are generated, and when fluids are generated, the heat transforms by itself. Guizhi (keishi) is a hot medicinal, yet its harshness is not disliked because guizhi, combined with gancao, serves precisely to move the heat.

The heat of atrophy of the lung lobes is due to deficiency, so it must not be attacked directly. So by frequently sipping the sweet and cold raw gancao, the heat gradually transforms by itself. My concubine once had this illness; at first the drool and foam filled a bowl, but after taking it for over half a month the phlegm lessened and she recovered. But it is very hard to drink, and within three or four days there is no quick effect.

## Notes

Appended formulas: Zhi Gancao tang from the *Waitai* (the same formula as Zhi Gancao tang from the *Qianjin yi* in the chapter on deficiency/consumption) and Gancao tang from the *Qianjin*, for atrophy of the lung lobes with much drool and spittle.

- The single character 覩 ("see") in the base is hard to interpret. Other sources read 方見虚労中 ("the formula appears in deficiency/consumption") or similar, and it is taken as an abbreviation or error for that.
- The base gives no amount for gancao in the *Qianjin* Gancao tang; Wikisource and others have 2 liang.
- "Sun Zhi" in the interlinear note at the start of Xu Bin's commentary is probably an error for Sun Qi, one of the Song editors.
