# Clause 18 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Guizhi (keishi) qu Shaoyao (shakuyaku) jia Zaojia tang (Cinnamon Twig Decoction minus Peony plus Gleditsia) from the *Qianjin*: treats atrophy of the lung lobes with spitting of foamy drool.

Guizhi and shengjiang (shokyo), 3 liang each; gancao (kanzo) 2 liang; dazao (taiso) 10 pieces; zaojia (sokyo) 2 pieces (remove the skin and seeds, mix-fry until scorched).

Of the five ingredients above, with 7 sheng of water boil over a very low [base: 大 "great"] fire down to 3 sheng and take warm in three doses.

Jiegeng Bai san (Hakusan; Platycodon White Powder) from the *Waitai*: treats cough with fullness in the chest, shivering chills, a rapid pulse, a dry throat without thirst, frequently bringing up turbid, fishy-smelling spittle, and after a long time spitting pus like rice gruel — this is lung abscess.

Jiegeng (kikyo) and beimu (baimo), 3 *fen* each; badou (hazu) 1 *fen* (remove the skin, dry-fry, grind into a paste).

Make the three ingredients above into a powder. A strong person takes half a *qianbi* with a drink; a thin, weak person takes less. If the disease is above the diaphragm, pus and blood are vomited; if below the diaphragm, it is purged out. If the purging is excessive and does not stop, drink a cup of cold water.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

This treats atrophy of the lung lobes with congestion and blockage. So zaojia is added to move the force of guizhi, gancao, shengjiang, and dazao.

[Part of Xu Bin's formula:] zaojia 1 piece (remove the skin and seeds, mix-fry until scorched).

## Notes

Appended formulas: Guizhi qu Shaoyao jia Zaojia tang from the *Qianjin* (atrophy of the lung lobes with spitting of foamy drool) and Jiegeng Bai san from the *Waitai* (lung abscess).

- Jiegeng Bai san is a drastic formula of jiegeng and beimu with badou added, expelling pus by vomiting or purging. If the diarrhea does not stop, cold water is drunk to stop it (badou is said to act more strongly with hot things and to stop with cold).
- In the base the two formulas are written continuously in one paragraph, and the paragraph breaks in the middle of the indication of Jiegeng Bai san.
- For 大 in 微微大煮, most other sources read 火 ("fire"), and 大 appears to be an error. 貝毋 is an error for 貝母 (beimu).
- After the final "drink a cup of cold water," most other sources have 則定 ("then it settles").
- Xu Bin's commentary on Jiegeng Bai san has been placed after unit 13.
