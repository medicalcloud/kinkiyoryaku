# Clause 8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cough with counterflow and ascending qi, frequently spitting turbid [phlegm], able only to sit and unable to sleep: Zaojia wan (Gleditsia Pill) governs it.

Zaojia wan formula

Zaojia (gleditsia fruit) 8 liang (scrape off the skin, mix-fry with ghee).

Powder [base: 未] the single ingredient above, make honey pills the size of firmiana seeds, and take three pills with jujube paste dissolved in hot water, three times in the day and once at night.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

Compared with the water-fowl sound, this is the most severe counterflow among cough with ascending qi. When it reaches inability to sleep, there is not only congestion but also closure. So the single ingredient zaojia opens it, combined with jujube paste to calm the stomach, waiting until it has opened to consider medicinals that protect the lung separately. Zaojia wan formula.

## Notes

A clause using Zaojia wan for severe cough and ascending qi with sticky phlegm, unable to lie down and sleeping only sitting up.

- Zaojia (honey locust fruit) is a strong phlegm-expelling medicinal that removes very sticky phlegm. Because it is harsh, jujube paste protects the stomach.
- For 未之, all other sources read 末之 ("powder it").
