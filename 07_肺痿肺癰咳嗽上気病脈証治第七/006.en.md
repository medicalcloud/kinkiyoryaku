# Clause 6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Lung atrophy with spitting of foamy drool but no cough: the person is not thirsty and will surely have enuresis and frequent urination. The reason is that the upper part is vacuous and cannot restrain the lower. This is cold in the lung; there will surely be dizziness and much drool and spittle. Warm it with Gancao Ganjiang tang (Licorice and Dried Ginger Decoction). If after taking the decoction there is thirst, it belongs to wasting thirst.

Gancao Ganjiang tang formula

Gancao 4 liang (mix-fried); ganjiang 2 liang (blast-fried).

Cut up the above, boil with 3 sheng of water down to 1.5 sheng, remove the dregs, and take warm in two doses.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

Having said earlier that "heat in the upper burner leads through coughing to lung atrophy," he now singles out one clause with cold, to show that among lung atrophy cases there is one that is different. Most lung atrophy with spitting of foamy drool involves coughing, but some have no cough and no thirst; this means there is no heat at all in the lung. Enuresis and frequent urination are because the upper part is vacuous, so urination has nothing to regulate it. Not only is there no heat, but there is also cold; then yin qi must rise to the vertex and insult the yang qi, causing dizziness, and yin qi in the middle congeals and stagnates the fluids, causing spitting of drool. So gancao and ganjiang warm the lung. If it were not heat below and cold above, warming would finally stop it. That thirst appears instead — is it not that heat is bound in the yin aspect, and though the lung cold has gone, the heat below remains, about to become wasting thirst in which one drinks one and urinates two? Hence "if after taking the decoction there is thirst."

## Notes

A clause using Gancao Ganjiang tang for lung atrophy due not to heat but to cold in the lung (vacuity-cold lung atrophy).

- Spitting foamy drool without cough, no thirst, enuresis, frequent urination, and dizziness are attributed to a cold lung, with the upper part vacuous and unable to restrain the lower (bladder).
- Gancao Ganjiang tang also appears in the *Shanghan lun*; it warms the middle burner and the lung.
- Thirst after taking it belongs to wasting thirst, i.e., a different disease, not cold.
- 巳 is an error for 已.
