# Clause 20 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Lung abscess with fullness and distension of the chest, puffy swelling of the whole body, face, and eyes, nasal blockage with clear snivel, inability to smell fragrant, foul, sour, or acrid odors, cough with counterflow and ascending qi, and panting with wheezing and pressing blockage: Tingli Dazao Xiefei tang governs it (the formula appears above. One prescription every three days; it may go up to three or four prescriptions. Here, first take one prescription of Xiao Qinglong tang, then proceed. The formula of Xiao Qinglong tang appears in the section on cough).

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

The earlier Tingli Dazao tang treated lung abscess with panting and inability to lie down, where the congested qi attacked only the inside. Here the congested qi runs into the channels, producing puffy swelling of the whole body, face, and eyes, and attacks the orifices of the lung, producing nasal blockage, clear snivel, and inability to smell fragrant, foul, sour, or acrid odors. Then the exterior and interior are equally ill. So first one prescription of Xiao Qinglong tang is used, and afterward the repletion of the lung is drained exclusively. This too is a clever idea for rescuing from danger.

## Notes

Appended. For lung abscess with chest distension, generalized edema, nasal blockage, loss of smell, and wheezing, Tingli Dazao Xiefei tang is used. The interlinear note says to take Xiao Qinglong tang first.

- The formula is in unit 12 above.
- The "section on cough" in the note is a section name in the *Qianjin fang* and elsewhere; Xiao Qinglong tang also appears in the later chapter on phlegm-rheum and cough.
