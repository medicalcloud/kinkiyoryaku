# Clause 12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Lung abscess with dyspnea and inability to lie down: Tingli Dazao Xiefei tang (Lepidium and Jujube Lung-Draining Decoction) governs it.

Tingli Dazao Xiefei tang formula

Tingli (mix-fried until yellow, pounded, and made into a pill the size of a pellet); dazao 12 pieces.

Of the above, first boil the dazao in 3 sheng of water down to 2 sheng, remove the dazao, add the tingli, boil down to 1 sheng, and take in a single dose.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

Compared with the earlier "ascending qi and inability to sleep," here there is abscess pus in the lung sealing in the lung qi, so one cannot lie down. So tingli discharges the excess of the lung and brings down its rotten turbidity, and dazao calms the stomach so that it can act.

[Xu Bin's formula:] tingli (dry-fried until yellow, pounded, made into a pill the size of a pellet); dazao 12 pieces.

## Notes

A clause using Tingli Dazao Xiefei tang for lung abscess with dyspnea and inability to lie down.

- Tinglizi drains the excess of the lung (phlegm, water), and dazao protects the stomach and moderates the harshness of the medicine. Many say it is used before the pus of lung abscess has formed.
- For 炙 ("mix-fried"), most other sources read 熬 ("dry-fried").
