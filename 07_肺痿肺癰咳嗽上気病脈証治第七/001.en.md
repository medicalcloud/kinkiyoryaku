# Clause 1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Question: When heat is in the upper jiao, coughing leads to atrophy of the lung lobes. From what is atrophy of the lung lobes contracted? The master said: From sweating, or from vomiting, or from wasting-thirst with frequent urination, or from difficult defecation further subjected to harsh purgatives causing diarrhea — the fluids are lost again and again; hence it is contracted.

[Question:] When the pulse at the inch opening is rapid and the person coughs, yet there is turbid spittle and foamy drool in the mouth, what is this?

## Notes

A question-and-answer clause on the causes of atrophy of the lung lobes (*feiwei*: a wasting disease of the lung). The answer continues in the next unit ("The master said ..."). Xu Bin's commentary follows the next unit.

- Repeated loss of fluids — through sweating, vomiting, excessive urination in wasting-thirst (a disease resembling diabetes), or diarrhea from purgatives — is given as the cause of atrophy of the lung lobes.
- The question asks why, though there is heat that should dry the mouth, the patient instead spits turbid spittle and foam.
