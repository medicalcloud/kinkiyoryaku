# Clause 4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Ascending qi with puffy swelling of the face, breathing with the shoulders, and a floating, large pulse: incurable. If diarrhea is added, it is even worse.

## Notes

A clause on severe ascending qi with a poor prognosis. Xu Bin's commentary is attached after the next unit.

- Facial swelling, shoulder breathing, and a floating, large pulse indicate original qi that has lost its root and is floating up and deserting; it is incurable. With diarrhea added, desertion occurs both above and below, which is even worse.
