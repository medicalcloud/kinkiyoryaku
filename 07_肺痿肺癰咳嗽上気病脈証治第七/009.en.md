# Clause 9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cough with a floating pulse: Houpo Mahuang tang (Kobokumaoto; Magnolia Bark and Ephedra Decoction) governs it.

Houpo Mahuang tang formula

Houpo (koboku) 5 liang; mahuang (mao) 4 liang; shigao (sekko) a piece the size of a hen's egg; xingren (kyonin) half a sheng; banxia (hange) half a sheng; ganjiang (kankyo) 2 liang; xixin (saishin) 2 liang; xiaomai (shobaku; wheat) 1 sheng; wuweizi (gomishi) half a sheng.

Of the nine ingredients above, with 1 dou 2 sheng of water first boil the wheat until cooked [base: 熱 "hot"], remove the dregs, add the other medicinals, boil down to 3 sheng, and take 1 sheng warm, three times a day.

## Notes

A clause using Houpo Mahuang tang for cough with a floating pulse (pathogen in the exterior and upper part). It pairs with Zeqi tang (deep pulse) in the next unit. Xu Bin's commentary is attached after the next unit.

- Houpo Mahuang tang is said to resemble Xiao Qinglong tang (Shoseiryuto) without guizhi (keishi), shaoyao (shakuyaku), and gancao (kanzo), with houpo, shigao, xingren, and wheat added.
- For 熱 in 煮小麦熱, most other sources read 熟 ("cooked"), and 熱 appears to be an error.
