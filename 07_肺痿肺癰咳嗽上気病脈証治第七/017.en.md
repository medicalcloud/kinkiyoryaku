# Clause 17 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shengjiang Gancao tang (Fresh Ginger and Licorice Decoction) from the *Qianjin*: treats lung atrophy with unceasing cough and spitting of foamy drool, and a dry throat with thirst.

Shengjiang 5 liang; renshen 2 liang; gancao 4 liang; dazao 15 pieces.

Of the four ingredients above, with 7 sheng of water boil down to 3 sheng and take warm in three doses.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

This decoction broadens the method of the single-ingredient gancao formula. The chest and throat are dried and withered by vacuity heat, so renshen and gancao generate fluids and transform heat, and shengjiang and dazao diffuse the qi of the upper burner; if the yang in the chest is not stagnant, the yin fire subsides by itself. But this too cannot be expected to work in one or two prescriptions.

## Notes

An appended formula, Shengjiang Gancao tang from the *Qianjin*, for lung atrophy with unceasing cough and foamy drool, and a dry throat with thirst.

- The amount of renshen is 2 liang in the base and 3 liang in all other sources.
