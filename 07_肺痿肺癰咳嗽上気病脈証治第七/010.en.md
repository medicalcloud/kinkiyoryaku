# Clause 10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

[Cough with] a deep pulse: Zeqi tang (Sun Spurge Decoction) governs it.

Zeqi tang formula

Banxia half a sheng; zishen (bistort) 5 liang (one version reads ziwan); zeqi (sun spurge) 3 jin (boiled in 5 dou of east-flowing water down to 1 dou 5 [sheng]); shengjiang 5 liang; baiqian (cynanchum) 5 liang; gancao, huangqin, renshen, and guizhi, 3 liang each.

Cut up the nine ingredients above, put them in the zeqi juice, boil down to 5 sheng, and take 5 ge warm, finishing it by nightfall.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

When there is cough with a floating pulse, exterior pathogen predominates. But this is not the exterior of the meridians; the pathogen is in the exterior of the qi aspect of the lung. So three ingredients, guizhi, shaoyao, and gancao, are removed from Xiao Qinglong tang, and houpo is added to bring down qi, shigao to clear heat, and wheat to gather in heart fire and calm the stomach. When there is cough with a deep pulse, interior pathogen predominates. But this is not the interior of the abdomen; the pathogen is in the interior of the ying-nutrient aspect of the lung. So zeqi, whose power to bring down water resembles daji, is the sovereign; moreover, the pathogen is in the ying-nutrient, and zeqi can also break blood. Ziwan protects the lung, baiqian opens binding, and guizhi moves yang and disperses pathogen; so they are the assistants. The other medicinals are Xiao Chaihu tang without chaihu and dazao, merely to harmonize and resolve the qi of the diaphragm.

[Part of Xu Bin's formula:] shengjiang 5 liang; baiqian 5 liang; gancao 3 liang; huangqin 3 liang; renshen 3 liang; guizhi 3 liang.

## Notes

A clause using Zeqi tang for cough with a deep pulse (pathogen in the interior, with water-fluid retention within). It pairs with Houpo Mahuang tang in the previous unit.

- Zeqi is a strong water-expelling medicinal, said to remove fluid accumulated within.
- The text begins with only "a deep pulse," continuing "cough with" from the previous clause; Xu Bin's edition reads "cough with a deep pulse."
- For the amount of zeqi decoction, 斗五, all other sources read 一斗五升 ("1 dou 5 sheng").
- Xu Bin explains using ziwan, the variant reading for zishen.
