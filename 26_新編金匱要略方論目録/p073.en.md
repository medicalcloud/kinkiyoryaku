# §73 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zhu Fangji jia Fuling Mangxiao tang [Mufangji qu Shigao jia Fuling Mangxiao tang (Woody Fangji Decoction minus Gypsum plus Poria and Mirabilite)]

Names of formulas in chapter 12 (Phlegm-Rheum and Cough).

- It corresponds to Mufangji qu Shigao jia Fuling Mangxiao tang in the main text. 巳 is an error for 己.
