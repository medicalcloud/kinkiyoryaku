# §182 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Formula to treat gluttonous overeating that does not digest, with hardness, fullness, and pain of the heart [base: 必] and abdomen

## Notes

Names of formulas in chapter 25 (Prohibitions on Fruits, Vegetables, and Grains).
