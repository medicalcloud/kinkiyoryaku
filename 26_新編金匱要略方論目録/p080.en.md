# §80 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Ling Shao [Gan] Jiang Wei Xin Ren tang; Ling Gan Jiang Wei Xin Xia Ren tang (Poria, Licorice, Dried Ginger, Schisandra, Asarum, Pinellia, and Apricot Kernel Decoction)

## Notes

Names of formulas in chapter 12 (Phlegm-fluid retention and Cough).

- They correspond to Ling Gan Wuwei jia Jiang Xin Banxia Xingren tang and the like in the main text. 茯芍 is perhaps an error for 茯甘.
