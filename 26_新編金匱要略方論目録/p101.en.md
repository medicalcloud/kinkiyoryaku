# §101 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Guizhi (keishi) qu Shaoyao jia Shuqi Muli Longgu Jiuni tang (Kyugyakuto; Counterflow-Rescuing Decoction: Cinnamon Twig Decoction minus Peony plus Dichroa Leaf, Oyster Shell, and Dragon Bone)

## Notes

Names of formulas in chapter 16 (Fright Palpitations, Nosebleed, Bloody Stool, Chest Fullness, and Static Blood).
