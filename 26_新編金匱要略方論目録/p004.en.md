# §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Gualou Guizhi tang (Trichosanthes and Cinnamon Twig Decoction); Gegen tang (Pueraria Decoction); Mahuang Xingren tang (Ephedra and Apricot Kernel Decoction) [Mahuang Xingren Yiyi Gancao tang]

## Notes

Names of formulas in chapter 2 (Convulsions, Dampness, and Summerheat).

- "Mahuang Xingren tang" corresponds to Mahuang Xingren Yiyi Gancao tang in the main text.
