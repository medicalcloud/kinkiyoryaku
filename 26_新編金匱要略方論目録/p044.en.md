# §44 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shengjiang Gancao tang (Fresh Ginger and Licorice Decoction); Shengjiang tang (Fresh Ginger Decoction); Guizhi tang (Cinnamon Twig Decoction)

## Notes

Names of formulas in chapter 7 (Lung Wilting, Lung Abscess, Cough, and Qi Ascent).

- It is unclear which formula "Shengjiang tang" corresponds to among the appended formulas of the main text (perhaps Guizhi qu Shaoyao jia Zaojia tang, or a duplicate of Shengjiang Gancao tang).
