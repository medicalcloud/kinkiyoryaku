# §40 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Houpo Mahuang tang (Kobokumaoto; Magnolia Bark and Ephedra Decoction); Zeqi tang (Sun Spurge Decoction); Maimendong tang (Bakumondoto; Ophiopogon Decoction)

Names of formulas in chapter 7 (Atrophy of the lung lobes, Lung Abscess, Cough, and Qi Ascent).
