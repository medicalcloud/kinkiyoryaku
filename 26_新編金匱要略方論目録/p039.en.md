# §39 (English translation)

Gancao Ganjiang tang (Licorice and Dried Ginger Decoction); Shegan Mahuang tang (Belamcanda and Ephedra Decoction); Zaojia wan (Gleditsia Pill)

## Notes

Names of formulas in chapter 7 (Lung Wilting, Lung Abscess, Cough, and Qi Ascent).
