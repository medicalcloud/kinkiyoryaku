# §39 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Gancao Ganjiang tang (Kanzokankyoto; Licorice and Dried Ginger Decoction); Shegan Mahuang tang (Yakanmaoto; Belamcanda and Ephedra Decoction); Zaojia wan (Sokyogan; Gleditsia Pill)

## Notes

Names of formulas in chapter 7 (Atrophy of the lung lobes, Lung Abscess, Cough, and Qi Ascent).
