# Clause 5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Thirst with desire to drink water, but vomiting as soon as water enters, is called water counterflow; Wuling san (Goreisan) governs it.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

Thirst leads to drinking water; the water is too much and [accumulates] suddenly, so that as soon as water enters there is vomiting. This is a disease within a disease. So it no longer stresses the wasting and thirst but calls it only "water counterflow," showing that the new disease should be treated urgently. But the medicine is still only Wuling san. Wuling san certainly governs resolving both exterior and interior, but its effect in bringing down water is especially swift.

## Notes

A clause using Wuling san for water counterflow, in which water drunk for thirst is vomited at once. The same content appears in the middle Taiyang chapter of the *Shanghan lun* (corresponding to clause 74).
