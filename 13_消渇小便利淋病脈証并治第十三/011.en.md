# Clause 11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Inhibited urination: Puhui san (Cattail Ash Powder) governs it; Huashi Baiyu san (Talc and Silverfish Powder) and Fuling Rongyan tang (Poria and Halite Decoction) also govern it.

Puhui san formula

Puhui (cattail ash) 7 *fen*; huashi (kasseki) 2 *fen* [other sources: 3 *fen*].

Pound the two ingredients above into a powder and take a square-inch spoonful with a drink, three times a day.

Huashi Baiyu san

Huashi 2 *fen*; luanfa (hair) 2 *fen* (burned); baiyu (silverfish) 2 *fen*.

Pound the three ingredients above into a powder and take half a *qian*-spoon with a drink, three times a day.

Fuling Rongyan tang formula

Fuling (bukuryo) half a jin; baizhu (byakujutsu) 2 liang; rongyan (halite) one piece the size of a pellet.

The three ingredients above ... [the rest is missing; Daizhige and others read "first decoct the fuling and baizhu, add the rongyan and decoct again, and take warm in three doses"].

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

Puhui is the ash of burned cattail mats; it can remove damp-heat and disinhibit urination, and huashi can free the nine orifices and remove damp-heat; so they govern it. Baiyu can open [the stomach], bring down qi, and remove water qi. Hair is the surplus of blood and enters the yin, so combined with huashi it removes damp-heat in the yin aspect and disinhibits urination. As for Fuling Rongyan tang, it contains baizhu to fortify the spleen and fuling to percolate dampness; rongyan comes from the shaded earth and rocks of mountain slopes and, unrefined by boiling, enters the kidney, removes yin fire, and also clears heat; so it is the envoy. But this formula involves much more supplementation and nourishment than the previous two.

[Xu Bin's formulas:] puhui half a jin; huashi 1 jin.

Huashi 1 jin; luanfa 1 jin (burned); baiyu 1 jin.

For the three ingredients above, first decoct the fuling and baizhu, add the rongyan and decoct again, and take warm in three doses.

## Notes

A clause giving three formulas for inhibited urination: Puhui san, Huashi Baiyu san, and Fuling Rongyan tang.

- Puhui san consists of cattail ash and huashi; Huashi Baiyu san of huashi, burned hair, and baiyu (silverfish insects); Fuling Rongyan tang of fuling, baizhu, and rongyan (a kind of rock salt).
- In the base the preparation of Fuling Rongyan tang breaks off at "the three ingredients above." Daizhige and Xu Bin's edition have "first decoct the fuling and baizhu, add the rongyan and decoct again, and take warm in three doses."
- For 半銭匙, most other sources read 方寸匕.
- Xu Bin's amounts are in jin, greatly different from the base's *fen*.
