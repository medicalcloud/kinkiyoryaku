# Clause 7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Strangury disease: the urine is like millet grains, the lesser abdomen is taut and tense, and the pain draws into the umbilicus.

## Notes

A clause on the signs of strangury (painful, frequent, difficult urination). The urine contains something like millet grains (such as gravel-like stones). Xu Bin's commentary is attached after unit 9.
