# Clause 8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the anterior tibial (Fuyang) pulse is rapid, there is heat in the stomach: grain is dispersed and one craves food, the stool must be hard, and urination is frequent.

## Notes

A clause stating that when the anterior tibial (Fuyang) pulse is rapid with stomach heat, one eats much, the stool is hard, and urination is frequent. Xu Bin explains it as a wasting and thirst pattern close to strangury. Xu Bin's commentary is after the next unit.

- The base reads 満穀; it is emended to 消穀 because all other sources so read (see the emendation note).
