# Clause 10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Inhibited urination means there is water qi; if the person is thirsty [Daizhige: "suffers from thirst"], the following pill governs it.

Gualou Qumai wan (Trichosanthes Root and Dianthus Pill)

Gualougen 2 liang; fuling and shuyu, 3 liang each; fuzi 1 piece (blast-fried); qumai 1 liang.

Powder the five ingredients above and make refined-honey pills the size of firmiana seeds; take three pills with a drink, three times a day. If there is no effect, increase to seven or eight pills. Free urination and warmth in the abdomen are the signs of effect.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

Inhibited urination means there is heat in the bladder. The bladder connects the waterways of the whole body; when it is rough and difficult to pass, water lodges and counterflows; hence "there is water qi." If there is no thirst, the heat stops at the bladder; if there is thirst, the source of qi transformation is also hot. So qumai and fuling expel water, and gualougen clears heat in the upper jiao. The original qi of spleen and kidney must be nourished, so shanyao cultivates the root. Though the bladder is hot, it is because the kidney is in fact deficient and its opening and closing have failed; so fuzi supplements the original yang. Moreover, the bladder is already afflicted by damp-heat, its qi feeble and not moving, so the great power of fuzi must serve as the vanguard for qumai and fuling.

[Xu Bin's formula:] shuyu 3 liang; gualougen 2 liang; qumai 1 liang; fuzi 1 piece (blast-fried); fuling 3 liang. Powder the five ingredients above and make refined-honey pills the size of firmiana seeds; take two pills with a drink, three times a day. If there is no [effect] ...

## Notes

A clause using Gualou Qumai wan for inhibited urination with water qi and thirst.

- Gualougen stops thirst, qumai and fuling disinhibit water, shanyao supplements spleen and kidney, and fuzi warms the yang of the lower jiao. It is regarded as a formula treating upper-jiao dry heat and lower-jiao cold together.
- For "the following pill," the other sources give the formula name (Gualou Qumai wan). For 若 in 其人若渇, Daizhige reads 苦 ("suffers").
