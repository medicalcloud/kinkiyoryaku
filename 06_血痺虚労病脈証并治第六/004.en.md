# Clause 4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

A man with a deficient, deep, wiry pulse, no cold or heat, shortness of breath, tension in the abdomen, inhibited urination, a white complexion, occasional dimming of vision, nosebleeds, and fullness of the lesser abdomen: this is caused by taxation.

## Notes

A clause listing the signs of deficiency/consumption (shortness of breath, abdominal tension, inhibited urination, pallor, dim vision, nosebleeds, lower abdominal fullness). The absence of cold and heat distinguishes it from externally contracted disease. Xu Bin's commentary is after unit 7.

- The base reads 小腹; it is emended to 少腹 because all other sources so read (see the emendation note).
