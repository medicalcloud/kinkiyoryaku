# Clause 15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Vacuity taxation with all kinds of insufficiency and the hundred illnesses of wind qi: Shuyu wan (Dioscorea Pill) governs it.

Shuyu wan formula

Shuyu (dioscorea) 30 *fen*; danggui, guizhi, qu (medicated leaven), gandihuang, and douhuangjuan (soybean sprouts), 10 *fen* each; gancao 28 *fen*; renshen 7 *fen*; xiongqiong, shaoyao, baizhu, maimendong (ophiopogon), and xingren, 6 *fen* each; chaihu, jiegeng, and fuling, 5 *fen* each; ejiao 7 *fen*; ganjiang 3 *fen*; bailian (ampelopsis) 2 *fen*; fangfeng 6 *fen*; dazao 100 pieces (made into a paste).

Powder the twenty-one ingredients above, mix with refined honey into pills the size of a pellet, and take one pill with wine on an empty stomach. One hundred pills make one course.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

This does not speak only of abdominal tension; signs of insufficiency appear both inside and outside, not just abdominal tension and all kinds of insufficiency. Compared with the pattern of Huangqi Jianzhong tang, the former speaks only of abdominal tension, so Jianzhong tang governs it; this has many illnesses of wind qi, so Shuyu wan governs it. Does this pill not seem to be specifically for wind qi? [Such a view] does not know that vacuity taxation patterns often involve wind qi, and that one must precisely not set out deliberately to treat the wind qi. So Zhongjing uses the Four Gentlemen and Four Agents to nourish qi and blood; maimendong, ejiao, ganjiang, and dazao to supplement the lung and stomach; jiegeng and xingren to open and lift the lung qi; guizhi to move yang; fangfeng to move the spleen; shenqu to open depression; douhuangjuan to diffuse the kidney; chaihu to raise the qi of shaoyang; and bailian to transform the wind that has entered the nutritive. Though there is wind qi, he never treats it specifically, meaning that when the right qi moves, the wind qi leaves by itself. But shuyu is the largest in amount and gives the formula its name because it is neither cold nor hot, neither drying nor slippery, and suits both spleen and kidney. So with it as sovereign, all the medicinals assist one another in putting things in order.

## Notes

A clause using Shuyu wan for vacuity taxation with all kinds of insufficiency together with various illnesses due to wind evil.

- A large formula of twenty-one ingredients centered on shuyu (shanyao), containing the elements of Si Junzi tang (renshen, baizhu, fuling, gancao) and Siwu tang (danggui, shaoyao, dihuang, xiongqiong). It mainly supplements right qi, with a few medicinals added to disperse wind qi.
- Xu Bin's formula lacks bailian and has twenty ingredients.
