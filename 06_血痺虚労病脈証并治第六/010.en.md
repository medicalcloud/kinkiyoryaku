# Clause 10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

A sunken, small, slow pulse is called qi desertion. When such a person walks quickly, he pants and gasps; the hands and feet are counterflow cold, the abdomen is full, and in severe cases there is loose diarrhea with undigested food.

## Notes

A clause on the pulse and signs of vacuity taxation with declining yang qi (qi desertion). Breathlessness on exertion, cold extremities, abdominal distension, and diarrhea with undigested food are attributed to yang vacuity of spleen and kidney. Xu Bin's commentary is after unit 11.
