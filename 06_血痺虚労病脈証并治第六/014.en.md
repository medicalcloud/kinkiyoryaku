# Clause 14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Deficiency/consumption with lumbar pain, tension of the lesser abdomen, and inhibited urination: Bawei Shenqi wan (Eight-Ingredient Kidney Qi Pill) governs it (the formula appears under leg qi).

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

([The formula appears] in [the chapter on] miscellaneous diseases of women.) Lumbar pain, tension of the lesser abdomen, and inhibited urination are all definite signs of the kidney. But they are not comparable to signs such as loss of essence; it is kidney deficiency with impediment. So Liuwei wan (Six-Ingredient Pill) supplements the yin, and still guizhi and fuzi must strengthen the original yang.

## Notes

A clause using Bawei Shenqi wan for deficiency/consumption due to kidney deficiency (lumbar pain, tension of the lower abdomen, inhibited urination).

- The formula is in the appended "Master Cui's Bawei wan" of the earlier chapter on wind attack and joint wind (interlinear note "the formula appears under leg qi"). Daizhige and Xu Bin's edition say it appears in miscellaneous diseases of women, referring to Shenqi wan in that chapter.
- Xu Bin explains Bawei wan as Liuwei wan (dihuang, shanzhuyu, shanyao, zexie, fuling, mudanpi) plus guizhi and fuzi. Liuwei wan is a later formula (by Qian Yi of the Song).
