# Clause 3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

A man whose complexion is thin (pale and lusterless) has thirst and blood loss. If there is sudden panting and palpitations with a floating pulse, the interior is vacuous.

## Notes

A pale, white complexion indicates insufficient blood, accompanied by thirst and blood loss. Sudden panting and palpitations with a floating pulse are attributed to interior vacuity. Xu Bin's commentary is after unit 7.
