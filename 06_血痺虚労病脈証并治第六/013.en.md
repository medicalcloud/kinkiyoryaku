# Clause 13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Deficiency/consumption with abdominal tension and all kinds of insufficiency: Huangqi Jianzhong tang (Ogikenchuto; Astragalus Center-Fortifying Decoction) governs it (add 1.5 liang of huangqi [ogi] to Xiao Jianzhong tang [Shokenchuto]; otherwise follow the method above. For shortness of breath and fullness of the chest, add shengjiang [shokyo]; for abdominal fullness, remove the dazao [taiso] and add 1.5 liang of fuling [bukuryo]; and to treat lung deficiency detriment and insufficiency and to supplement qi, add 3 liang of banxia [hange]).

## Notes

A clause using Huangqi Jianzhong tang (Xiao Jianzhong tang plus huangqi) for deficiency/consumption with abdominal tension and all kinds of insufficiency.

- Huangqi is added to supplement qi and secure the exterior. The interlinear note gives modifications for shortness of breath, chest fullness, abdominal fullness, etc.
- The base reads 腰痛 ("lumbar pain") for 腹満; it is emended because all other sources read 腹満 (see the emendation note).
- Xu Bin's commentary is attached to the previous unit (Xiao Jianzhong tang).
