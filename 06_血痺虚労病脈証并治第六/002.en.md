# Clause 2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In a man who appears healthy, a large pulse indicates taxation; an extremely deficient pulse also indicates taxation.

## Notes

The clauses on deficiency/consumption begin here (deficiency/consumption: a chronic wasting disease with depletion of qi, blood, yin, and yang). This one gives the pulses of deficiency/consumption. Xu Bin's commentary is attached after unit 7.

- A large but forceless pulse (floating deficient yang) and an extremely deficient pulse (decline of essence) are both regarded as deficiency/consumption pulses.
- Many explain the specific mention of "men" by the view that depletion of essence through sexual activity was considered a main cause of deficiency/consumption.
