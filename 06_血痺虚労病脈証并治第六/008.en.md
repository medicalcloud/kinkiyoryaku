# Clause 8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

A man who appears healthy but whose pulse is vacuous, weak, fine, and faint is prone to night sweating.

## Notes

A clause stating that in vacuity taxation with deficiency of both yin and yang, qi and blood, night sweating occurs readily.

- The base reads 経微; it is emended to 細微 because all other sources so read (see the emendation note). For 善 ("prone to"), most other sources read 喜 (same meaning).
