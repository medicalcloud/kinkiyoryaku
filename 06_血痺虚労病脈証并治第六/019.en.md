# Clause 19 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Tagan san (Otter Liver Powder) from the *Zhouhou*: treats cold taxation, and also governs demonic infixation [base: 症] spreading through a household. Mix-fry one otter liver until dry, powder it, and take a square-inch spoonful with water, three times a day.

## Notes

An appended formula, Tagan san from the *Zhouhou fang*, for cold taxation (vacuity taxation of cold type) and "demonic infixation" passing one after another through a household (often identified with contagious diseases such as pulmonary tuberculosis). Xu Bin's commentary is attached to the previous unit.

- For 鬼症, all other sources read 鬼疰, and 症 appears to be a graphic error; the translation reads 鬼疰.
