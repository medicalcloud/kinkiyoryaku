# Clause 6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

A man whose pulse is floating, weak, and rough is childless; his essential qi is clear and cold (one version reads 冷).

## Notes

A clause on male infertility due to decline of essential qi. Xu Bin's commentary is after unit 7.

- The interlinear note "one version reads 冷" is hard to interpret, since the text already reads 清冷.
