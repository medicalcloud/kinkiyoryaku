# Clause 5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taxation disease: the pulse is floating and large, the hands and feet are vexed (hot), it worsens in spring and summer and improves in autumn and winter; the genitals are cold and semen comes out spontaneously, and [the legs] ache and waste so that one cannot walk.

## Notes

A clause on vacuity taxation in which yin vacuity lets vacuous yang float up. It worsens in spring and summer, when yang prevails, and improves in autumn and winter, when yin prevails. Semen leaks, and the legs ache and waste so that walking becomes impossible. Xu Bin's commentary is after unit 7.
