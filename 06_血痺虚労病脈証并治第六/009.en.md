# Clause 9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When a person aged fifty or sixty has a large pulse in illness, with impediment running along both sides of the spine, or rumbling intestines, or saber [lumps] and goiter-flanking [lumps] (scrofula of the neck and armpit), all are contracted through taxation.

## Notes

In an older person, a large pulse is due not to exuberant yang but to deficiency/consumption, with numbness along both sides of the spine, rumbling intestines, and "saber" and "goiter-flanking" lumps (swollen lymph nodes of the neck and armpit). Xu Bin's commentary is attached after unit 11.
