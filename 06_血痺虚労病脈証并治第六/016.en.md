# Clause 16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Deficiency/consumption with deficiency vexation and inability to sleep: Suanzao tang (Sansoto; Sour Jujube Decoction) governs it.

Suanzao tang formula

Suanzaoren (sansonin; sour jujube seed) 2 sheng; gancao (kanzo) 1 liang; zhimu (chimo) 2 liang; fuling (bukuryo) 2 liang; xiongqiong 2 liang (the *Shenshi* formula includes shengjiang (shokyo) 2 liang).

Of the five ingredients above, with 8 sheng of water [first] boil the suanzaoren; when 6 sheng is obtained, add the other medicinals, boil down to 3 sheng, and take warm in three doses.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

Deficiency/consumption is deficiency. With vexation, it carries fire. Inability to sleep means that because of the fire the qi too is not smooth. The fault should be laid at the heart. But the exuberance of heart fire is really due to depressed liver qi and an unsettled ethereal soul; otherwise it could not generate fire. So suanzaoren, which enters the liver and calms the spirit, in the largest amount, is the sovereign; chuanxiong, which frees the depression of liver qi, is the minister; zhimu, which cools the qi of lung and stomach, gancao, which drains excess of heart qi, and fuling, which guides the qi back to the lower jiao, are the assistants. Though it treats deficiency vexation, it never actually supplements the heart.

[Part of Xu Bin's formula:] xiongqiong 1 liang.

## Notes

A clause using Suanzao tang (Suanzaoren tang (Sansoninto)) for deficiency/consumption with vexation and insomnia. It is regarded as a representative formula for insomnia.

- Suanzaoren nourishes liver blood and calms the spirit, zhimu clears deficiency heat, fuling and gancao calm the heart, and chuanxiong frees the liver qi.
- 知毋 is an error for 知母 (zhimu).
- The base reads 1 sheng of suanzaoren; it is emended to 2 sheng because all other sources so read (see the emendation note).
