# Clause 1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The master said: In the pulse one should take note of excess and insufficiency. When yang is faint and yin is wiry, there is chest impediment with pain. The reason is that one must blame its extreme vacuity. Now since the yang is vacuous, we know [the disease] is in the upper burner. The reason for chest impediment with heart pain is that the yin is wiry.

## Notes

The opening clause of the chapter on chest impediment, heart pain, and shortness of breath. It explains the pathology of chest impediment (a disease of blockage and pain in the chest) by the pulse "yang faint, yin wiry." Xu Bin's commentary is attached after the next unit.

- "Yang faint" means the pulse at the inch (yang position) is faint, i.e., vacuity of upper-burner yang qi. "Yin wiry" means the pulse at the cubit (yin position) is wiry, i.e., exuberance of yin-cold evil in the lower burner. Yin cold overwhelming the vacuous upper-burner yang causes chest impediment.
- It is often related to chest pain disorders such as angina pectoris.
