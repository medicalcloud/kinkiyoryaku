# Clause 7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Chest bi-impediment that slackens and tightens: the following formula governs it.

Yiyi Fuzi san (Coix and Aconite Powder) formula

Yiyiren 15 liang; large fuzi 10 pieces (blast-fried).

Pound the two ingredients above into a powder and take a square-inch spoonful, three times a day.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

"Slackening and tightening" means some sinews of the limbs and joints are slack and some tense; the pathogen of chest bi-impediment has spread into the sinews. The liver governs the sinews, and *yi* and *gui* share a source. Clearly the dragon-thunder fire (kidney yang) is insufficient, so the qi that impedes the chest can shift to impede the sinews. So yiyiren, which relaxes the sinews, is combined with fuzi to warm and rouse the lower origin; when the yang returns, the impediment leaves by itself. A powder is used because it is meant to resolve it gradually.

## Notes

A clause using Yiyi Fuzi san for chest bi-impediment whose symptoms alternately ease and intensify.

- Interpretations of "slackening and tightening" (緩急) vary. Many take it as pain that sometimes eases and sometimes becomes acute (occurring in attacks), but Xu Bin takes it as slackness and tension of the sinews of the limbs.
- Fuzi warms yang and disperses cold, and yiyiren removes dampness.
- For 方寸匙 ("square-inch spoon"), most other sources read 方寸匕.
