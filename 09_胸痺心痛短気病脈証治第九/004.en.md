# Clause 4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Chest bi-impediment with inability to lie down and heart pain penetrating through to the back: Gualou Xiebai Banxia tang (Trichosanthes, Chinese Chive, and Pinellia Decoction) governs it.

Gualou Xiebai Banxia tang formula

Gualoushi 1 piece; xiebai 3 liang; banxia half a jin; baijiu 1 dou.

Boil the four ingredients above together down to 4 sheng and take 1 sheng warm, three times a day.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

Since it is headed "chest bi-impediment," signs such as dyspnea may also be present. Inability to lie down is added, an accompanying sign of thoracic fluid retention. Further, heart pain penetrating to the back: thoracic fluid retention originally does not hurt, but the retained fluid hurts because of chest bi-impediment, and the qi responds in the back. So banxia is added to the previous formula to remove retained fluid and bring down counterflow.

## Notes

A clause using Gualou Xiebai Banxia tang for severe chest bi-impediment with inability to lie down and chest pain penetrating to the back.

- It is the previous formula with banxia added to strengthen the removal of phlegm-fluid retention.
- The amount of banxia is half a jin in the base and half a sheng in Xu Bin's edition.
