# Clause 4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Chest impediment with inability to lie down and heart pain penetrating through to the back: Gualou Xiebai Banxia tang (Trichosanthes, Chinese Chive, and Pinellia Decoction) governs it.

Gualou Xiebai Banxia tang formula

Gualoushi 1 piece; xiebai 3 liang; banxia half a jin; baijiu 1 dou.

Boil the four ingredients above together down to 4 sheng and take 1 sheng warm, three times a day.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

Since it is headed "chest impediment," signs such as panting may also be present. Inability to lie down is added, an accompanying sign of propping rheum. Further, heart pain penetrating to the back: propping rheum originally does not hurt, but the rheum hurts because of chest impediment, and the qi responds in the back. So banxia is added to the previous formula to remove rheum and bring down counterflow.

## Notes

A clause using Gualou Xiebai Banxia tang for severe chest impediment with inability to lie down and chest pain penetrating to the back.

- It is the previous formula with banxia added to strengthen the removal of phlegm-rheum.
- The amount of banxia is half a jin in the base and half a sheng in Xu Bin's edition.
