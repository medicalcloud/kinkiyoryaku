# Clause 10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Meat of the six domestic animals from which hot blood does not stop flowing must not be eaten.

## Notes

A clause stating that meat of domestic animals that keeps bleeding must not be eaten. The six domestic animals are the horse, ox, sheep, pig, dog, and chicken.

- For 熱血 ("hot blood"), Xu Bin's text reads 有切.
