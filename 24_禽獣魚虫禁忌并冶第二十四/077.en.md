# Clause 77 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Pickled carp must not be eaten together with adzuki bean leaves; its roe must not be eaten together with pig liver; they harm people.

## Notes

A clause stating that pickled carp must not be eaten with adzuki bean leaves, nor carp roe with pig liver.
