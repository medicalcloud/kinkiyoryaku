# Clause 36 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Beef, mutton, and pork must never be steamed or roasted over paper-mulberry or mulberry wood and eaten; it makes worms grow in the abdomen.

## Notes

A clause stating that cooking beef, mutton, or pork over paper-mulberry or mulberry wood produces worms in the abdomen.
