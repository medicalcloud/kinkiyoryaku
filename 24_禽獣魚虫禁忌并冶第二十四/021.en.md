# Clause 21 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

A horse without night-eyes on its legs must not be eaten.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

[Xu Bin's text (the clause of the next unit) with note:] If one eats sour horse meat without drinking wine, it kills a person (酸 "sour" should be 駿 "steed"; it comes from the story of Duke Mu of Qin and the rustics below Mount Qi; indeed there is no horse meat that is not sour).

## Notes

A clause stating that a horse lacking night-eyes (the horny calluses on the inner side of a horse's legs) must not be eaten. Prohibitions on horse meat follow.

- The text by Xu Bin attached to this unit is the clause of the next unit with an interlinear note on it.
