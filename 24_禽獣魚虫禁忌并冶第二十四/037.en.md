# Clause 37 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The meat of an ox that has eaten snakes kills people. How is it known? An ox that has eaten snakes has its hair lying smoothly toward the rear.

Formula to treat one who has eaten the meat of a snake-eating ox and is about to die

Drink 1 sheng of human breast milk, and there is immediate recovery.

## Notes

A clause stating that the meat of an ox that has eaten snakes is poisonous, with how to recognize it and a formula for the poisoning.
