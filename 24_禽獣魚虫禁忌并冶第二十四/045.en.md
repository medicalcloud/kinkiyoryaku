# Clause 45 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Eating pork together with raw coriander rots the navel.

## Notes

A clause stating that eating pork with raw coriander causes the navel to rot.

- The base lacks 生 ("raw"), but all other sources have it, so it has been supplied (see the emendations).
