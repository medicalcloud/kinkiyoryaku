# Clause 50 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Roe-deer meat must not be eaten together with shrimp, raw vegetables, plums, or damsons; all of these make people ill.

## Notes

A clause stating that roe-deer meat must not be eaten with shrimp, raw vegetables, plums, or damsons.

- For 蝦 ("shrimp"), the base reads 煆, but all other sources read 蝦, so it has been emended (see the emendations). For 麞, Xu Bin's text reads 麋 ("elk").
