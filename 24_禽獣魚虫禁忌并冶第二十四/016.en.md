# Clause 16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the six domestic animals die naturally, they have all died of pestilence and are poisonous; they must not be eaten.

## Notes

A clause stating that domestic animals that died naturally died of pestilence and are poisonous, and must not be eaten.
