# Clause 87 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Eating minced raw fish and drinking milk or curds makes worms grow in the abdomen and form conglomerations.

## Notes

A clause stating that eating minced raw fish and drinking dairy produces worms in the abdomen that form conglomerations.
