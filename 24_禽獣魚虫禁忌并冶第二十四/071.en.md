# Clause 71 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

A fish without intestines or gallbladder must not be eaten; eating it causes impotence for three years, and in women ends childbearing.

## Notes

A clause stating that eating a fish without intestines or gallbladder causes impotence in men and infertility in women.
