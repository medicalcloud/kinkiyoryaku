# Clause 81 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Pickled black carp must not be eaten together with raw coriander, raw mallow, or among wheat. Loach and swamp eel must not be eaten together with the blood of a white dog.

## Notes

A clause forbidding pickled black carp with certain foods, and loach or swamp eel with white dog's blood.
