# Clause 11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Eating the meat of the animal of one's parents' or one's own birth year makes one's spirit and soul uneasy.

## Notes

A clause stating that eating the meat of the zodiac animal of one's parents' or one's own birth year disturbs the spirit.
