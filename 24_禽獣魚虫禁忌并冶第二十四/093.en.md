# Clause 93 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Food on which bees, flies, insects, ants, and the like have gathered in numbers causes fistulas when eaten.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

[The title of the next chapter in Xu Bin's text:] Prohibitions concerning fruits, vegetables, and grains, and their treatment

## Notes

A clause stating that eating food on which many insects have gathered causes fistulas. It is the last clause of this chapter.

- The text by Xu Bin attached to this unit is the title of the next chapter (chapter 25).
