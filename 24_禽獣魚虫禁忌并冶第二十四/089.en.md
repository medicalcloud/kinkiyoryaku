# Clause 89 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Crabs whose eyes face each other, or with spotted legs and red eyes, must not be eaten.

Formula to treat poisoning from eating crab

Zisu (perilla).

Boil the above and drink 3 sheng of the liquid. Drinking the pounded juice of zisuzi (perilla seed) is also good.

## Notes

A clause stating that abnormal crabs must not be eaten, with a formula for crab poisoning.

- For 目 ("eyes"), the base reads 月, but all other sources read 目, so it has been emended (see the emendations).
