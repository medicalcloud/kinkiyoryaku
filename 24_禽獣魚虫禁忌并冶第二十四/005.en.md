# Clause 5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Meat and liver that do not pick up dust or dirt when they fall to the ground must not be eaten.

## Notes

A clause stating that meat or liver that does not pick up dirt when dropped must not be eaten. Clauses forbidding meat with abnormal properties follow.
