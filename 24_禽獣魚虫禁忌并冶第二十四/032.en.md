# Clause 32 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Eating an ox that died of pestilence, with red or yellow eyes, is strictly forbidden.

## Notes

A clause strictly forbidding the eating of an ox that died of pestilence with red or yellow eyes. Prohibitions on oxen follow.
