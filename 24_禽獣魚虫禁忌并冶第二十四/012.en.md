# Clause 12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

After eating fatty meat or hot broth [base: 羔 "lamb"], one must not drink cold water.

## Notes

A clause stating that one must not drink cold water after eating fatty meat or hot broth.

- For 羹 ("broth"), the base reads 羔 ("lamb"); most other sources read 羹, so it has been emended (see the emendation notes).
