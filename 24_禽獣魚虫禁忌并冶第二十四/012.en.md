# Clause 12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

After eating fatty meat or hot lamb [other sources: broth], one must not drink cold water.

## Notes

A clause stating that one must not drink cold water after eating fatty meat or hot broth.

- For the base's 羔 ("lamb"), most other sources read 羹 ("broth").
