# Clause 30 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Another formula

Take a square-inch spoonful of human grime and take it; it is good.

Formula to treat poisoning from eating horse meat when one is about to die

Xiangchi 2 liang; xingren 3 liang.

Steam the two ingredients above for about the time of a meal, pound them well, and take twice a day.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

[Part of Xu Bin's formula:] xiangchi 2 liang; xingren 3 liang.

## Notes

Another formula for horse-liver poisoning and a formula for poisoning from horse meat.

- Xu Bin's text of the "human grime" formula is placed under "Xu Bin's commentary at the head of the chapter" in the chapter description (index).
