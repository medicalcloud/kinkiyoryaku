# Clause 39 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Those with lodged heat must not eat mutton.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

Lodged heat refers to people who have had heat disease from before. Mutton supplements qi; with supplementation the heat increases, so it must not be eaten.

## Notes

A clause stating that people with pre-existing heat must not eat mutton. Prohibitions on sheep follow.
