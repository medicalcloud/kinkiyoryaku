# Clause 66 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

If a pregnant woman eats sparrow, the child will be lewd and shameless.

## Notes

A clause stating that if a pregnant woman eats sparrow, the child will be lewd.

- Daizhige has 飲酒 ("and drinks wine") after "sparrow."
