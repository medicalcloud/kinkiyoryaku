# Clause 83 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

A softshell turtle with sunken eyes, or with the shape of the character 王 under its plastron, must not be eaten.

## Notes

A clause stating that a softshell turtle with sunken eyes or with a 王-shaped pattern under its plastron must not be eaten.

- For 凹 ("sunken"), the base reads 四, but all other sources read 凹, so it has been emended (see the emendations).
