# Clause 69 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

A fish whose head is pure white, with what look like strung pearls reaching up to the spine, kills a person who eats it.

## Notes

A clause stating that eating a fish with a pure white head and pearl-like strings reaching the spine is fatal. Prohibitions on fish follow.
