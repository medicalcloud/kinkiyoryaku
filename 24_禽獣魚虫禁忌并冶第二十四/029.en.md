# Clause 29 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Horse liver and hair must not be eaten carelessly; they poison and harm people.

Formula to treat a person poisoned by horse liver who has not yet died

Powder [base: 求 "seek out"] twice seven pellets of male rat droppings, mix with water, and take twice a day (the pointed droppings are the male ones).

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

[Xu Bin's text:] Powder twice seven pellets of male rat droppings, mix with water, and take twice a day (the pointed droppings are the male ones).

## Notes

A clause forbidding the eating of horse liver and hair, with a formula for horse-liver poisoning.

- For 毛 ("hair"), the base reads 尾 ("tail"), but all other sources read 毛, so it has been emended (see the emendations).
- For 末之 ("powder them"), the base reads 求之 ("seek them out"); most other sources read 末之, so it has been emended (see the emendation notes).
