# Clause 20 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Dried meat stored in a rice jar is poisonous, and eating it after it has passed the summer brings on kidney disease.

Formula to treat poisoning from the meat of domestic animals that died naturally

Pound shavings of huangbai (obaku) and take a square-inch spoonful.

Formula to treat poisoning from musty meat or leak-soaked dried meat (musty meat is meat covered in a sealed vessel overnight; leak-soaked dried meat is that wetted by drips from a leaking thatched roof): burn dog dung and take a square-inch spoonful with wine. Taking human breast milk each time is also good. Drinking 3 sheng of raw chive juice also works.

Formula to treat poisoning from eating dried meat stored in millet

Drink several sheng of soybeans boiled into a strong liquid and it resolves at once. It also treats poisoning from wildcat meat, leak-soaked dried meat, and the like.

Formula to treat poisoning from eating raw meat

Dig the ground three chi deep, take 3 sheng of the earth below, boil it several times in 5 sheng of water, let it settle, and drink 1 sheng of the clear liquid; there is immediate recovery.

Formula to treat poisoning from the livers of domestic animals, birds, or beasts

Soak fermented soybeans in water, squeeze out the liquid, and take several sheng; there will be recovery.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

[Part of Xu Bin's text:] Soak fermented soybeans in water, squeeze out the liquid, and take several sheng; there will be recovery.

## Notes

A clause on the poison of dried meat stored in a rice jar, followed by formulas for treating poisoning from meat.

- For 鬱 (musty meat), the base reads 欎, and for 治 in 治黍米, the base reads 冶; all other sources read the emended characters, so they have been emended (see the emendations).
