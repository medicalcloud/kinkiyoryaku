# Clause 19 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Eating the meat of an ox that died of pestilence causes throughflux diarrhea and can also produce hard accumulations; it should be purged with disinhibiting medicines.

## Notes

A clause stating that eating the meat of an ox that died of pestilence causes severe diarrhea or hard accumulations, which should be purged.
