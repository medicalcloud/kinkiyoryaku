# Clause 68 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Do not eat swallow; [if one does and] enters water, one will be devoured by a flood dragon.

Birds or beasts killed by poisoned arrows have poisonous meat. Formula to resolve it:

Take the liquid of boiled soybeans or salt water, and it resolves.

## Notes

A clause forbidding the eating of swallows, and a formula for resolving the poison in the meat of birds or beasts killed by poisoned arrows.
