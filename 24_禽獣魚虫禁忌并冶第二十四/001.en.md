# Clause 1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Food and drink, with their flavors, serve to nourish life; but if what one eats is unsuitable, it can instead do harm. Unless one practices taking drugs and refining elixirs, how can one not eat and drink? I observe that people of our time do not know how to regulate and take care of themselves, and illnesses arise in competition, as if none arose other than from food. If one hopes to preserve one's life, one must know what to strictly avoid. Among the flavors one eats, some suit the disease and some harm the body; if one obtains what is suitable, it benefits the body; if harmful, it causes illness, and when danger results from this, it is generally hard to cure. Whenever medicines are boiled and the liquid drunk to resolve poisoning, although it is said to be an emergency, it must not be [drunk] hot, [for all poisoning diseases become] worse [with heat]; it should be drunk cold.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

Qi increases on meeting heat and decreases on meeting cold, and toxic qi is the same. Hence "all poisoning diseases become worse with heat." This is also why medicines that resolve toxins must be sweet and cold. In dry cholera, drinking hot water causes death, because the toxin arises from blazing evil heat, so heat makes it worse. I have often seen pig dung and salt water, which are cold by nature, cure it; this too is what is called drinking cold, not merely that the decoction is cool. It is also clear that acrid hot medicines are unsuitable.

## Notes

The general introduction to chapter 24 (prohibitions concerning birds, beasts, fish, and insects, and their treatment). It says that some foods suit the disease or body and others are harmful, so one must know what to strictly avoid, and that medicines for resolving poisoning should be drunk cold.

- After 熱 in the base's 不可熱更甚, all other sources have 飲諸毒病得熱 ("must not be drunk hot; all poisoning diseases become worse with heat"); the base appears to have lost this phrase, and the translation supplies it from them.
