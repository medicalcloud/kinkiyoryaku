# Clause 31 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Another formula

Boil reed root and drink the liquid; it is good.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

[Xu Bin's text:] Boil reed root and drink it; it is good.

## Notes

Another formula for poisoning from horse meat.
