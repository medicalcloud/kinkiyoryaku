# Clause 88 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When minced raw fish is eaten and stays undigested between the heart and chest, and vomiting does not bring it out, quickly purge it to remove it; if it lasts long, it becomes a mass

disease. Formula to treat it:

Jupi (kippi) 1 liang; dahuang (daio) 2 liang; poxiao (bokusho; crude mirabilite) 2 liang.

Boil the three ingredients above in 1 large sheng of water down to a small sheng and take in a single dose; it dissolves at once.

Formula to treat eating much minced raw fish that does not digest and binds into a mass disease

Mabiancao (verbena).

Pound the one ingredient above into juice and drink it. Or drinking 1 sheng of ginger-leaf juice also dissolves it. One may also take an emetic to vomit it.

Formula to treat poisoning [other sources: being poisoned] after eating fish, with two kinds of restlessness

Jupi.

Decoct the above into a strong liquid and take it; it resolves at once.

Formula for poisoning from eating puffer fish

Reed root.

Boil the above and take the liquid; it resolves at once.

## Notes

Formulas for undigested minced raw fish and for poisoning from fish and puffer fish.
