# Clause 4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Every heart is the dwelling of spirit and consciousness; do not eat it, or it will make one suffer retribution in the next life.

## Notes

A clause stating that animal hearts, the dwelling of spirit and consciousness, must not be eaten, or one will suffer retribution in the next life. It reflects the Buddhist idea of karmic retribution.

- For 勿 ("do not"), the base reads 切, but all other sources read 勿, so it has been emended (see the emendations).
