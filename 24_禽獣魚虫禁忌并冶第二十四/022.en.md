# Clause 22 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

If one eats sour horse meat without drinking wine, it kills a person.

## Notes

A clause stating that one should drink wine after eating horse meat.

- Xu Bin's interlinear note (see the commentary of the previous unit) says that 酸 is an error for 駿 ("steed"), referring to the story of the people below Mount Qi who ate Duke Mu of Qin's steed and were given wine by the duke.
