# Clause 8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Any meat that does not dry, does not move when roasted over fire, but moves by itself on meeting water must not be eaten.

## Notes

A clause stating that meat that does not dry, does not shrink when roasted, but moves in water must not be eaten.
