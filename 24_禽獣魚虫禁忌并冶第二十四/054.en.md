# Clause 54 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Formula to treat undigested dog meat, with hardness below the heart, or abdominal distension, dry mouth and great thirst, urgency of the heart and fever,

raving like madness, or throughflux diarrhea.

Xingren 1 sheng (ground thoroughly with the skins).

Mix the above with 3 sheng of boiling water, take the liquid, and divide into three doses. When pieces of meat are purged, it is very effective.

## Notes

A formula for the various symptoms of undigested dog meat.

- The paragraph break after "fever" follows a line break in the base.
