# Clause 55 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

A pregnant woman must not eat rabbit, goat, softshell turtle, chicken, or duck; they make the child voiceless.

## Notes

A clause stating that if a pregnant woman eats rabbit, goat, softshell turtle, chicken, or duck, the child will be voiceless.
