# Clause 49 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Elk fat with plums and damsons: if a pregnant woman eats them, the child will have blue blindness; in men, they damage the essence.

## Notes

A clause stating that if a pregnant woman eats elk fat with plums or damsons, the child will be blind, and in men they damage the essence.

- For 盲 ("blindness"), the base reads 肓, but all other sources read 盲, so it has been emended (see the emendations).
