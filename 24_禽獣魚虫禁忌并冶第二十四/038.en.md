# Clause 38 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Another formula

Wash the head with rice-washing water and drink 1 sheng; there will be recovery.

Finely cut ox stomach, boil it with 1 dou of water down to 1 sheng, and drink it warm; one who sweats profusely will recover.

Formula to treat poisoning from eating beef

Boil gancao and drink the liquid; it resolves at once.

## Notes

Further formulas for poisoning from the meat of a snake-eating ox, and a formula for poisoning from beef.
