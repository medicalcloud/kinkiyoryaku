# Clause 13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Any of the five zang organs [of animals] or fish that are not soiled by dust or dirt when thrown on the ground must not be eaten.

## Notes

A clause stating that organs or fish that do not pick up dirt when thrown on the ground must not be eaten.
