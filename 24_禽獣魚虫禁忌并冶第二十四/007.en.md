# Clause 7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Any meat or fish that dogs will not eat and birds will not peck must not be eaten.

## Notes

A clause stating that meat or fish that dogs will not eat and birds will not peck must not be eaten.

- For 諸 ("any"), Xu Bin's text reads 豬 ("pork").
