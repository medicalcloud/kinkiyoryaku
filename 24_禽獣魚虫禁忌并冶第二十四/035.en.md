# Clause 35 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

From the third to the fifth month, the lungs of an ox contain worms like horsetail hairs; cut them away and do not eat them, for eating them harms people.

## Notes

A clause stating that ox lungs from the third to the fifth month contain worms and must not be eaten.
