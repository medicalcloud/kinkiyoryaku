# Clause 41 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

A white bead inside a sheep's hoof is called "sheep's hanging sinew"; eating it causes epilepsy.

## Notes

A clause stating that eating the white bead in a sheep's hoof (sheep's hanging sinew) causes epilepsy.
