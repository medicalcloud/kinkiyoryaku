# Clause 79 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Crucian carp must not be eaten together with monkey or pheasant meat (one version says) must not be eaten together with pig liver.

## Notes

A clause stating that crucian carp must not be eaten with monkey or pheasant meat (or, in one version, pig liver).
