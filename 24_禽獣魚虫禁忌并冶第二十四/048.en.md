# Clause 48 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Venison must not be made into broth [base: 羔 "lamb"] with the white part of cattail and eaten; it brings on malign sores.

## Notes

A clause stating that eating venison cooked into a broth with the white stems of cattail causes malign sores.

- For 肉 in 鹿肉, the base reads 人, but all other sources read 肉, so it has been emended (see the emendations). For 羹 ("broth"), the base reads 羔, but most other sources read 羹, so it has also been emended.
