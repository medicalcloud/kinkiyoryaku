# Clause 8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Roundworm reverse-flow: Wumei wan governs it.

Wumei wan (Mume Pill) formula

Wumei (mume) 300 pieces; xixin (asarum) 6 liang; ganjiang 10 liang; huanglian 1 jin; danggui (Chinese angelica) 4 liang; fuzi 6 liang (blast-fried); chuanjiao 4 liang (remove the sweat [base: "juice"]); guizhi 6 liang; renshen and huangbai, 6 liang each.

Pound and sift the ten ingredients above separately, then combine and process them. Soak the wumei in bitter wine (vinegar) overnight, remove the pits, and steam them under 5 sheng of rice; when the rice is cooked, pound into a paste, mix with the medicines until well combined, put into a mortar, and pound with honey 5000 [other sources: 2000] times. Make pills the size of firmiana seeds. Before meals take 10 pills with a drink, three times a day, gradually increasing to 20 pills. Forbid raw, cold, slippery [base: 潰], and strong-smelling foods.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

In roundworm disease, both zang cold and zang dryness can make them restless. So the powder, honey, and gancao of the previous clause form a formula that both kills worms and moistens dryness. In roundworm reverse-flow, 厥 means counterflow. This resembles zang reverse-flow: zang reverse-flow is from absence of yang, and roundworm reverse-flow is also zang cold, so [the worms] cannot settle and rise into [the diaphragm]. But evil may be shallow or deep: in zang reverse-flow there is vexation without even a moment's peace, whereas in roundworm reverse-flow it can stop after a while. So it first says "there should be vomiting of roundworms," to show that, owing to cold, the roundworms are restless and rise into the diaphragm, unlike unceasing vexation without roundworms. Precisely because it is due to roundworms, movement and stillness are irregular; so after vexation it stops, and when one eats again and retches with vexation, the roundworms smell the food and want to get it, rise further, and are vomited out. Its origin is cold, so acrid hot medicinals are gathered to warm it, supervised by huangbai, with wumei and huanglian added to calm the roundworms and renshen and danggui to supplement vacuity.

[Part of Xu Bin's formula:] fuzi 6 liang (blast-fried); chuanjiao 4 liang (remove the sweat); guizhi 6 liang; renshen 6 liang; huangbai 6 liang.

... increasing to 20 pills. Forbid raw, cold, slippery, and strong-smelling foods.

## Notes

A clause using Wumei wan for roundworm reverse-flow. The same formula appears in the jueyin chapter of the *Shanghan lun* (corresponding to clause 338).

- It combines the sourness of wumei, the acrid heat of chuanjiao, xixin, ganjiang, fuzi, and guizhi, and the bitter cold of huanglian and huangbai, supplementing with renshen and danggui; it is regarded as a jueyin formula for mixed cold and heat.
- Xu Bin's commentary also covers the previous unit.
- For chuanjiao, "remove the juice" is "remove the sweat" (remove the oil) in most other sources. For "5000 times," all other sources read "2000 times," and for 潰臭, most other sources read 滑臭 ("slippery and strong-smelling").
