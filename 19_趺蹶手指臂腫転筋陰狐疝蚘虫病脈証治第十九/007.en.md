# Clause 7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Roundworm reverse-flow: there should be vomiting of roundworms. The patient may be made quiet yet again have vexation at times; this is zang cold, with roundworms rising into the diaphragm, hence vexation, which stops again after a while. When one gets food and retches, and again has vexation, it is because the roundworms smell the food and come out; the person should vomit roundworms by himself or herself.

## Notes

A clause describing the signs of roundworm reverse-flow. It is nearly the same as the latter half of the Wumei wan clause in the jueyin chapter of the *Shanghan lun* (corresponding to clause 338).
