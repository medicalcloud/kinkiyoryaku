# Clause 6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Roundworm as a disease: it makes one spit drool, with heart pain that comes in attacks at times; when toxic medicines do not stop it, Gancao Fen Mi tang governs it.

Gancao Fen Mi tang (Licorice, Powder, and Honey Decoction) formula

Gancao 2 liang; fen (powder) 1 liang; honey 4 liang.

Of the three ingredients above, with 3 sheng of water first boil the gancao down to 2 sheng, remove the dregs, add the powder and honey, stir to mix, decoct to a thin gruel, and take 1 sheng warm. Stop once there is improvement.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

This discusses roundworm disease not due to zang cold. So its signs are only heart pain and spitting of drool, without vomiting of roundworms. But the pain comes in attacks at times, meaning it is not constant, unlike the lingering pain of deficiency cold. If toxic medicines do not stop it, then medicines that treat qi, treat blood, attack cold, or expel accumulation will all fail to respond. So gancao, powder, and honey govern it: white powder kills worms, and honey and gancao both harmonize the stomach and lure the roundworms.

## Notes

A clause using Gancao Fen Mi tang for roundworm disease with spitting of drool and attacks of pain in the heart (epigastrium) that toxic worm-killing medicines have not stopped.

- 粉 ("powder") is variously taken as lead powder (killing worms) or rice flour. Xu Bin says "white powder kills worms."
- The part before "decoct to a thin gruel" in the instructions is missing from the base because of a lost leaf and has been supplied from Wikisource (*Jingui yaolüe fanglun*).
