# Clause 2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When a patient constantly has swelling and movement of the fingers and arms, and this person's body twitches: Lilu Gancao tang governs it.

Lilu Gancao tang (Veratrum and Licorice Decoction) formula (not seen)

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

[...] governs it (the formula is not yet right). Commentary: the four limbs of the body belong to the spleen, but the qi of the flesh is governed by yangming: the legs belong to foot yangming and the arms to hand yangming. If the fingers and arms constantly swell and move, there is phlegm qi congesting and blocking hand yangming. And twitching of the body means the yangming qi in the flesh is not moving, so the flesh swells and moves. Lilu can cause vomiting of wind-phlegm, and gancao can calm the center qi; so they govern it. The complete formula has not been seen, so it is omitted.

## Notes

A clause using Lilu Gancao tang for swelling and movement of the fingers and arms with twitching of the body. The formula has not survived.

- This unit is missing from the base because of a lost leaf and has been supplied from Wikisource (*Jingui yaolüe fanglun*).
- Xu Bin attributes it to phlegm qi in hand yangming, explaining that lilu induces vomiting of wind-phlegm and gancao calms the center qi.
