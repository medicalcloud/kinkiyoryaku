# Clause 4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yin fox-like mounting qi: one side is smaller or larger, and from time to time it goes up and down. Zhizhu san governs it.

Zhizhu san (Spider Powder) formula

Zhizhu (spider) 14 pieces (dry-fried until scorched); guizhi half a liang.

Make the two ingredients above into powder; take eight-tenths of a spoonful, mix with a drink, and take twice a day. Honey pills are also acceptable.

## Translation of Xu Bin's commentary

Pain extending to the lesser abdomen is always called 疝 (mounting); so in antiquity there were names such as heart mounting and liver mounting. This is named inguinal hernia because it appears only in the outer kidney (scrotum), with one side smaller or larger, rising and falling at irregular times; so it is specially named yin inguinal hernia qi, depicting how its yin and yang flicker without settling. The medicine is Zhizhu san. The spider has the power of attacking toxin, and its drawing of thread and weaving of webs all take place in the lesser abdomen, so it is used as a guide; guizhi is added to attack kidney pathogen, so that when the yang pathway moves, the yin qi disperses by itself.

## Notes

A clause using Zhizhu san for yin fox-like mounting qi, in which one side of the scrotum is enlarged and goes up and down from time to time.
